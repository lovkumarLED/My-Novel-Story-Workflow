# Worldbuilding

## World Overview

Does the Dragon Princess Really Hate Me? — reconciled canon (MDs are source of truth, old ChatGPT draft adapted, never the reverse).

Title (exact, grammar intentional): `Is the princess of the dragon really hate me?` / user variant `Is the Prince of Dragons Really Hate Me?`. Three-act novel. Chapter One opens at the imperial gates. Every turn labeled Act + Chapter. Third-person-limited (Love primary POV), past tense, Hinglish narration + dialogue. Mature romantic fantasy: court politics, war-action, slow-burn contempt-to-respect. Intimacy only consensual and off-page.

Nations (canon names): Draconic Empire of Vyrathis, capital Aurelthys (= drakon-court.md, mountain-ringed pale spires, Oath Hall, Leyline Docks); Kingdom of Aurelion, greatest human kingdom (= valoria.md, home of Charles VII); Sylvan Realms elven territories (= sylvan-realms.md); Demon Dominion capital Noxharrow (= noxharrow.md); plus Longhua Dominion (Chinese-culture) + Bharatvarsha Confederacy (Indian-culture) as flavor nations. Other peoples: dwarves, beastfolk, spirits, merfolk, monster tribes. Dungeons hold magical resources, artifacts, city-threatening creatures. Magitech European aesthetic + leyline-magitech + elven-accumulation systems.

Time: 723 years since Hero ended Dragon Wars (Hero missing 700+ years). Three Laws universal ideals, carved in every capital, nobles sometimes evade, old prejudices remain: 1) Tradition, 2) Equal Citizenship, 3) Equal Justice (= systems/three-laws.md). Binding mechanism = Ancient Marriage Registry relic (recognizes bonds; challengeable only through High Court) + death-bond condition under investigation (if Love leaves Rivona in danger and reverse, mechanism unknown).

Central premise (not opening scene): poor human Love bound by ancient law into marriage with Crown Princess Rivona Vaeltharis (24, silver-white hair, golden horns, amber eyes, black-and-gold gown, enormous dragon form, one of strongest women alive) who despises him. No auto-romance. Rivona can hate long, change gradually, complicate, or grow more hostile. Tracker every turn: Hate / Love-Affection / Confusion / Anger / Jealousy x/100, moves ONLY from Love's actions.

Cast (canon): 3 suitors Vekar Draven (dragon supremacy), John Ashford (human diplomat), Charles VII (Aurelion warrior-heir); 4 friends Seraphine Drakemore (against), Vaelora Frostwing (against), Elowen Thorne (supports, half-elf scholar), Nymera Ashveil (supports, dragon knight); King Aldric Vaeltharis (emperor, bound to honor law); household Elyndra Moonveil, Mira Fernbrook, Theron Silverbough, Ilyra Dawnpetal, Garrick Thornvale; demons Malachar Voss, Lysandra Voss, Azravel Voss, Evelith Voss; Kyle Dravenor thread (academy friend Rivona almost chose, rivalry → respect).

Hard lore (never contradict, story.md): PUBLIC TRUTH Hero gone to another dimension with Dorna Kharzdar, believed dead, Gods silent except "hero gets what he wants"; PRIVATE TRUTH Aelindra (1000+ yrs Elven Queen, 13-15 ft, release-or-die), Sahira (ex-Beast Queen, now advisor, Hero's main commander), Morwenna (2000+ yr demon matriarch, loved only Hero) wait because promised normal simple life unfulfilled; Old King Zarvath promised Hero unknown thing; Aldric served junior never saw Hero; demons 15-20M second only to dragons; beast rule to Rokhar Pridemane; dwarf Thorgrim/Dorna/Braldor succession; Gods pantheon Aurelia/Vyrathion/Azgorth/Durzak/Vessara/Rakhora (human + true forms); Mythical Beasts Lumis/Umbraxis/Infernix/Abyssara/Terradon/Stormrion/Frostveil/Venomord/Celestyr. CENTRAL MYSTERY Love hides something unspecified — never reveal early, never contradict. ELDER LORE Act Two+ only.

Control (latest user order): user controls characters/decisions/secrets/consequences; agent controls Love. Living simulation: Rivona/suitors/king act on own agendas, events occur off-page. Format every roleplay turn: SCENARIO & SCENE → DIALOGUE `Name: "line"` → Feelings Tracker → Hinglish recap → YOUR TURN (one question, then STOP).
