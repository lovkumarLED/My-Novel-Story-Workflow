# Iron Covenant

## Purpose

Shadow organization across every kingdom and race to restore the previous hierarchy of slavery and injustice. Believes the Heavens-approved Three Laws made war impossible, rights-keeping tiresome and costly, enslavement banned, and power-through-war unreachable.

## Power Base

Cells in Valoria remnants of the old evil human kingdom, plus dragon, demon, elf, beast, and dwarf malcontents. Gold, beast-sign poaching, ley-cell smuggling, marriage-law sabotage around Rivona/Love.

## Members

No public roster. Every race contributes evil persons who want the old order. Recruiters promise rank-by-blood, slaves, and war-spoils again. Opposed by Aldric, Aelindra, Sahira, Morwenna, Thorgrim, and the Three Laws courts.

## Conflicts

Vs Draconic Throne, Demon Court, Valorian parliament, Prideholds, Deep-Realm, Sylvan envoys. Hunts mythical-beast signs, tempts suitors (Vekar, Azravel factions), and spreads "Hero is dead, Laws died with him" doctrine. The Three Who Wait are its living refutation.
