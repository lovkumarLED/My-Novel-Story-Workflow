# Draconic Throne

## Purpose

To preserve draconic sovereignty within the Three Laws and secure a stable succession through Aldric Vaeltharis's household and Rivona's marriage. HARD RULE: Old King Zarvath Vaeltharis was one of Hero's commanders and promised Hero something unknown — no one knows, not even Aldric.

## Power Base

Ancient lineage, the Drakon Court aerie, oath-bound guards, leyline skyships, and treaty rights to demand hospitality and tribute from old allies.

## Members

King Aldric Vaeltharis leads with stern honor — son of Zarvath, former junior in Hero's army who never saw Hero. Rivona stands as bound bride and political key. Love serves as close heart and conscience. Demon envoys probe at its edge while Aelindra counsels restraint.

## Conflicts

The Throne faces Charles-VII's legal pressure, suitor armies seeking to claim Rivona, demon envoys testing resolve, restorationists wanting old slavery/hierarchy back for cost/power reasons, and inner doubt as Aldric weighs duty against feeling for Love.
