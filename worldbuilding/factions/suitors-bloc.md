# Suitors Bloc

## Purpose

A military-backed coalition of rival suitors and their patrons seeking to break Vorath's betrothal to Rivona and seize the draconic alliance for themselves.

## Power Base

Mercenary wings, leyline war-skiffs, Valorian funds, and legal scholars hunting flaws in the marriage-binding clause of the Three Laws.

## Members

Backers of Vekak, John, and Hattie march together under King Charles-VII's favor. Envoys promise Rivona freedom while using Love as leverage and courting Aelindra for elven sanction.

## Conflicts

The suitors distrust one another and quarrel over who would wed Rivona. They oppose Vorath and the Draconic Throne, evade Morvain's traps, and risk race-crime charges if pressure turns to coercion.
