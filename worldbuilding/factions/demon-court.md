# Demon Court

## Purpose

The demon nation's ruling court, determined to keep equal protection under the Three Laws and to turn the royal marriage crisis into recognized influence.

## Power Base

Shadow envoys, pact law, deep ley-reserves, and formal standing in shared courts that punish race-crimes against demons.

## Members

Shadow sovereign Queen Morwenna (2000+ yrs, loved only Hero, no child with him, elder sister of Malachar) watches from retreat. Day King Malachar Voss, her younger brother, holds throne with Queen Lysandra Voss, heirs Azravel and Evelith. Veiled ministers speak for the throne. They study Aldric, test Charles-VII, question Rivona's consent, and probe Love and Aelindra for sympathy. Demons number only 15-20 million, age slowly, die hard, power second only to dragons.

## Conflicts

The Court opposes suitor slurs and Valorian overreach while distrusting draconic pride. It must appear lawful to avoid old prejudice, even as it quietly gathers leverage over every heir.
