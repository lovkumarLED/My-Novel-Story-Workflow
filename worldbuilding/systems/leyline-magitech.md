# Leyline Magitech

## Overview

The common technology that draws magical energy from leylines to power light, flight, and war. Planes and ships run on stored magic, not gears or steam.

## Rules & Limitations

Craft carry charged ley-cells that drain with distance and weight. Overdraw causes burnout, storms disrupt currents, and remote routes need relay towers. War-machines are capped by treaty and cell size.

## History

Dwarf engineers and human artificers tamed wild ley-surges after the Three Laws. Dragon pilots charted sky currents, and Valoria built the first civilian air docks.

## Practitioners

Licensed artificers, ley-pilots, dock guilds, and royal engineers maintain the grid. Training is strict, and unlicensed war-cells are contraband.

## Impact on Society

Magitech links Drakon Court, Valoria, Longhua Dominion, and Bharatvarsha Confederacy with daily flights. It lets Love travel swiftly, carries Rivona to ceremony, arms Vorath and Charles-VII alike, and tempts Morvain while Aelindra warns of ley-strain on wild magic.
