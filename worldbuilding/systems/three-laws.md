# Three Laws

## Overview

The universal constitution proclaimed by the human Hero 700 years ago. It binds every nation and protects dragons, humans, elves, beastmen, dwarfs, monsters, and demons alike.

## Rules & Limitations

CANON — Three Laws of the Hero (never forget, universal ideals, nobles sometimes evade, old prejudices remain):

1. The Law of Tradition: Every race must respect the traditions and cultural practices of others, provided they do not violate the fundamental laws. [User version: Every race will respect each other and their tradition.]
2. The Law of Equal Citizenship: Anyone living within a nation's borders is protected by its constitution regardless of race, origin, gender, or religion. [User version: No one will get mistreated or misbehave because they are from different race. If a human lives in a dragon country or anyone, the constitution applies as if they are the person of that country, beside race.]
3. The Law of Equal Justice: Crimes motivated by racial hatred, discrimination, or abuse must be punished under the law. [User version: People who still commit crime against other race get punishment.]

No ruler is above them. After these rules, 700+ years passed (723 years since Hero ended Dragon Wars per story canon). World at peace, European-style fantasy architecture with magic/magitech.

## History

Written after the wars of dragon dominance, the Laws were carved in Valoria and sworn in Drakon Court, approved by the Heavens/God. Amendments closed gaps, but the old marriage-binding clause remains: a lawfully witnessed cross-throne betrothal cannot be broken without dishonor and sanction. When the Hero vanished 700+ years ago with Dorna Kharzdar, the Gods stayed silent on where he went, saying only "hero gets what he wants" — so the world believes both dead in another dimension, while Aelindra, Sahira, and Morwenna refuse to believe it.

## Practitioners

Constitutional judges, oath-priests, and archivists uphold the Laws. Kings like Charles VII and Aldric Vaeltharis must plead before them.

## Impact on Society

The Laws keep peace and let magitech travel cross borders. Their loophole now forces Rivona's marriage, trapping Love in loyalty, straining Aldric's honor, and giving Malachar and Aelindra opposed causes to argue.
