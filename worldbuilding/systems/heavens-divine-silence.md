# Heavens and Divine Silence

## Overview

HARD RULE (user-defined 2026-10-11). There is God/Heavens in this world. They approved the Hero's Three Laws. Since the Hero vanished, they stay silent on where he went.

## Rules & Limitations

- Heavens approved the Three Laws at proclamation — divine sanction behind equal citizenship/justice.
- On the Hero's fate, Gods answer only: "hero gets what he wants." No location, no return, no confirmation of death.
- Public reads this silence as death/ascension to another dimension with no return.
- Aelindra, Sahira, Morwenna reject that reading and wait on the Hero's personal promise of a normal life.

## History

After the Dragon Wars, Heavens approved the Laws. 700+ years ago Hero left with Dorna Kharzdar. Prayers for his whereabouts met silence ever since.

## Practitioners

Priests, oath-priests, memorial rites. All races invoke Heavens at weddings, trials, and funerals.

## Impact on Society

Divine silence is why "Hero is dead" is doctrine. The Three Who Wait are outliers for refusing it, which isolates them politically and fuels restorationists who say the Laws died with their maker.
