# Elven Accumulation

## Overview

The rare elven condition where stored magic gathers in the body with age. Elders grow giant as power accumulates and must release it or die.

## Rules & Limitations

Release requires ritual grounding, craft, song, or guarded discharge in wild lands. Holding too long brings fever, growth spurts, and rupture. Forced release leaves weakness, and each rite must fit the elf's lineage.

## History

Elven wardens learned the release rites long before the Hero. The Three Laws gave them protected groves in every nation to vent safely without fear or persecution.

## Practitioners

Grove keepers, song-masters, and elder guides like Aelindra's teachers oversee release. Youths learn measuring chants and are watched for early swelling.

## Impact on Society

Giant elders inspire awe and fear, shaping architecture with vast doors and clearings. Aelindra manages her own accumulation while advising Rivona, calming Love, cautioning Vorath against exploiting elven power, and resisting Morvain and Charles-VII pressure to weaponize release.
