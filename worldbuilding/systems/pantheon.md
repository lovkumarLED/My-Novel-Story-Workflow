# Pantheon

## Overview

HARD RULE (user-defined 2026-10-11). Each kingdom has its god. Gods have human form and true form. They approved the Three Laws through the Heavens, then fell silent on the Hero ("hero gets what he wants").

## Rules & Limitations

- Gods rarely intervene directly; they work through shrines, beasts, and omens.
- True forms are known doctrine; human forms walk unseen.
- Harming a god-shrine is a race-crime under Law 3.

## History

Old worship predates the Hero. All gods sanctioned his Laws. All silent since his vanishing with Dorna Kharzdar.

## Practitioners

Priests, shrine keepers, oath-priests per kingdom.

## Impact on Society

Festivals, oaths, marriage blessings, funeral rites. Divine silence is why the world believes Hero dead.
