# Great Human Aura Longevity

## Overview

HARD RULE (user-defined 2026-10-11, updated). Humans who reach greatness accumulate magical power and aura that slows aging drastically. This is why Aelindra, Sahira, and Morwenna believe the Hero can still be alive after 700+ years, despite public death-doctrine.

## Rules & Limitations

- Only great humans with dense aura qualify; ordinary humans age normally.
- Aura must be maintained; it slows aging, does not grant invulnerability.
- Demons separately age very slowly and die hard, population 15-20 million, power second only to dragons — see Noxharrow.
- Elves instead accumulate magic until giant and must release-or-die — see Elven Accumulation.

## History

Known since the Hero's era. The Hero's prolonged life without visible aging is the prime example. He vanished 700+ years ago with Dorna Kharzdar. Public believes both dead in another dimension; the Three Who Wait reject it on aura-rule + personal promise.

## Practitioners

The Hero. Potentially other great humans who reach his threshold (none confirmed in story open).

## Impact on Society

Public doctrine says Hero dead, so courts mourn rather than wait — except Aelindra, Sahira, and Morwenna who wait on promise + aura-rule. Succession crises (Rivona's binding) are judged against his possible return by those three, against his absence by everyone else.
