# Mythical Beasts

## Overview

HARD RULE (user-defined 2026-10-11). Elemental mythical beasts embody the world's magic. Rare, revered, tied to gods and leylines. Saved for future story use — do not spend early.

## Rules & Limitations

- Each beast embodies one element/domain and cannot be owned; they pact, not serve.
- God-tied: each kingdom god can call their aligned beast; others must bargain.
- Iron Covenant hunts beast-signs for leverage; harming a beast is a race-crime under Law 3.

## History

Older than crowns. Fought in the Dragon Wars, then withdrew after the Heavens-approved Three Laws. Signs stir again around Rivona's binding.

## Practitioners

Beast-speakers, shrine keepers, Sahira and Aelindra as elder mediators.

## Impact on Society

Shrines, omens, ley-surges. Their return would prove the old promises still live.
