# Noxharrow

## Description

A basalt capital of black spires, ember-lit streets, and vaulted markets. Brimstone and sweet incense mix in warm air, proud and orderly beneath old wings. Demons are few — only 15-20 million (1-3 crore) — age very slowly, die hard, and are powerful second only to dragons.

## History

Noxharrow predates human crowns, seat of a demon line that once ruled by fear. It accepted the Heavens-approved Three Laws 700+ years ago, trading conquest for trade, law, and lasting prosperity.

## Culture & Customs

Demons prize loyalty, contract-honor, and fierce family pride. Common citizens prospered under peace, keeping feast-nights, pact-marks, and open courtesy to travelers of all races.

## Notable Features

The Obsidian Palace, the Pact Hall of treaties, and Morwenna's shadow gallery where the Old Queen, more than two thousand years old and who loved only the Hero, still watches.

## Current State

Sovereign in shadow: Queen Morwenna, 2000+ yr demon matriarch, who loved only the Hero and had no child with him — elder sister of the day-king (image: morwenna-flux-01.png, story: queen-morwenna.md). Day rule is held by her younger brother King Malachar Voss with Queen Lysandra Voss, heirs Azravel Voss and Evelith Voss at his side. The world believes the Hero died in another dimension 700+ years ago with Dorna Kharzdar; Morwenna rejects it and waits on his promise of a normal life. The Obsidian Palace answers to Morwenna's shadow gallery. The court pursues its own interests in Rivona's marriage, seeking leverage while the city keeps its peace.
