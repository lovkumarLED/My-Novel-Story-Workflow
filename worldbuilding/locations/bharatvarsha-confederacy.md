# Bharatvarsha Confederacy

## Description

An Indian-cultured confederacy of allied rajas, with sandstone palaces, stepwells, spice markets, and temple towers. Many local languages thrive, with a shared court tongue for council debate.

## History

Formed from allied kingdoms after the Hero's peace, the Confederacy wrote the Three Laws into each raja's constitution. Its judges became famed for punishing race-crimes without favor.

## Culture & Customs

Guests are sacred, elders are honored, and music, dance, and debate mark every treaty. Beastmen, elves, dwarfs, and demons live openly in its quarters.

## Notable Features

The Lotus Council Hall, the star-chart observatories, and the sun-sail leyline shipyards on the great river.

## Current State

The Confederacy offers to witness Rivona's wedding rites to guarantee fairness. Love finds refuge in its hospitality, Vorath seeks its blessing, while Charles-VII courts its votes and Morvain and Aelindra trade news in its bazaars.
