# Kharzdar Deep-Realm

## Description

Mountain deep-holds, forge-halls, ley-engine works. Dwarf engineers with human artificers tamed ley-surges after the Three Laws.

## History

CANON per user 2026-10-11 updated: Ruled by King Thorgrim Kharzdar, very old. His wife was human and is no longer alive. His daughter Dorna Kharzdar left with the legendary Hero 700+ years ago because she loved him. PUBLIC BELIEF: both died in another dimension with no return; Gods stay silent ("hero gets what he wants"), so everyone believes it. Why they left, what happened — no one knows. Do not invent reason beyond love-devotion.

## Culture & Customs

Forge-honor, contract-honor, clan record-keeping. Race slurs punished under hold law.

## Notable Features

Embergate forge-halls, deep ley-reserves, clan archives (Dorna's departure recorded, return never recorded), memorial shrine to Dorna believed dead with Hero.

## Current State

The Deep-Realm watches Rivona's marriage for leverage on ore, engines, and old debts. Patron god Durzak Stoneheart guards the forges. A succession ceremony approaches: rule will pass to the King's sister's son, Braldor Kharzdar. HARD RULE: public thinks Hero + Dorna dead in another dimension; Aelindra/Sahira/Morwenna reject it and wait on Hero's promise of a normal life. Iron Covenant tempts debt-pressed clans here.
