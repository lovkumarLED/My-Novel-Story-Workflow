# Blackvein Wilds

## Description

The Blackvein Wilds are the most dangerous forest in the Draconic Empire. Its old canopy swallows daylight, its paths shift beneath dense roots, and dangerous creatures can appear far from any known lair. Ordinary wildlife also thrives here; not every animal is hostile, though that distinction offers little comfort to travelers.

## History

The empire has mapped only the forest's safer approaches. Soldiers avoid entering its deep interior except under urgent orders, and expeditions often turn back before reaching its heart.

## Culture & Customs

There are no nearby settlements or dependable roads within the deep forest. Anyone who lives here relies on intimate knowledge of the land rather than imperial patrols.

## Notable Features

Love's isolated farmhouse and fields lie deep within the Wilds. Familiar animals around his home are accustomed to him, but that does not make the wider forest safe for visitors.

## Current State

Garrick and Ilyra are accompanying Love home and have just learned that his home lies inside the empire's most feared forest.
