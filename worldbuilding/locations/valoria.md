# Valoria

## Description

The largest human kingdom capital, a river city of cathedrals, guildhalls, and broad boulevards lit by leyline lamps. Bells, markets, and printing houses give it a busy, ceremonial air.

## History

The current human kingdom was founded by followers of the Hero and grew rich on law, trade, and magitech shipyards. Its charter was the first to enshrine the Three Laws with equal protection for all races. Before it stood a previous evil human kingdom built on hierarchy and slavery; it was demolished. Some followers of that old order still remain, not powerful now, but they want to restore the old hierarchy.

## Culture & Customs

Valorians prize courtesy, contracts, and festivals honoring the Hero. Mixed-race neighborhoods share feast days, and insult by race brings swift public censure.

## Notable Features

The Marble Parliament, the Hero's Memorial with the Laws incised in stone, and the Royal Docks for leyline planes and river ships.

## Current State

Crown Prince Charles VII maneuvers from Valoria to secure Rivona's marriage alliance. Love grew up in its shadow politics, while dragon envoys, demon agents, and Aelindra's kin all watch the court's next decree. Old-order remnants and cross-kingdom restorationists agitate for the previous system of slavery and injustice, arguing the Laws make war impossible, rights-keeping tiresome and costly, enslavement banned, and power-through-war unreachable — so no one can become truly powerful anymore.
