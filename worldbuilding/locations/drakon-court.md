# Drakon Court

## Description

A mountain-ringed capital of pale spires, vaulted bridges, and landing terraces built for wings. European-style castles rise beside open aeries, with warm updrafts, bell towers, and leyline lanterns guiding night flight.

## History

Once the seat of dragon dominance, the Court yielded absolute rule when the human Hero proclaimed the Heavens-approved Three Laws 700+ years ago. Old Dragon King Zarvath Vaeltharis served as one of the Hero's commanders and promised the Hero something unknown — no one knows to this day what he promised, not even his son. His son, current King Aldric Vaeltharis, served as a junior in the Hero's army beside his father but never saw the Hero. The Court kept its throne and ritual primacy but accepted constitutional limits and multi-race courts. The world believes the Hero died in another dimension with Dorna Kharzdar; only the Three Who Wait reject it.

## Culture & Customs

Protocol governs bowing depth, wing display, and hoard-gifts. Residents prize oaths, hospitality to travelers, and respect for every race's traditions under the Laws.

## Notable Features

The Throne Aerie, the Oath Hall of carved treaties, and the Leyline Docks where magitech skyships moor for royal service.

## Current State

King Aldric Vaeltharis holds the Court in uneasy ceremony as Rivona's forced marriage to Love proceeds. Love's presence unsettles the household, while demon envoys watch for weakness and Aelindra seeks elven counsel among the archives. A hidden restorationist faction across kingdoms wants the old hierarchy of slavery and injustice back, claiming the Laws make war and rights-keeping too costly to stay powerful.
