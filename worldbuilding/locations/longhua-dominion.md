# Longhua Dominion

## Description

A Chinese-cultured nation of curved jade roofs, courtyard mansions, terraced fields, and misted mountain temples. Its own Longhua language fills schools, theaters, and edicts alongside trade Common.

## History

An old continuous civilization, the Dominion joined the Three Laws compact 700 years ago without surrendering its customs. It sent scholars to the Hero's council and kept its civil examinations.

## Culture & Customs

Filial duty, scholarship, tea ritual, and dragon-respect shape daily life. Festivals honor ancestors and rain-dragons, and race slurs are punished under Dominion law.

## Notable Features

The Vermilion Examination Hall, the Cloud-Step mountain shrines, and the Suzhou-style leyline canal yards building elegant magitech ships.

## Current State

Neutral but watchful, the Dominion hosts Rivona's distant cousins and trades silk for draconic ore. Love admires its poetry, Vorath respects its rites, while Morvain and Aelindra seek its sages as mediators.
