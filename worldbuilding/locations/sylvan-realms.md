# Sylvan Realms

## Description

Ancient forests of silver-barked trees, moss-lit libraries, and song-woven bridges. Air smells of pine, ink, and starlight magic, quiet but watchful to newcomers.

## History

Elves kept records before human kingdoms rose, guarding star-magic and old treaties. They bent custom only once, yielding absolute seniority when the Hero proclaimed the Heavens-approved Three Laws 700+ years ago. The world believes the Hero died with Dorna Kharzdar; Queen Aelindra rejects it and still waits on his promise.

## Culture & Customs

Courtesy, lineage, and scholarship rule daily life. Elven nobles serve as prized diplomats between races, though some elders call the Hero's equality laws an insult to old customs.

## Notable Features

The Starlight Archives, the Queen's whitewood palace, and envoy schools where Elowen Thorne and Elyndra Moonveil train diplomats and lorekeepers.

## Current State

Still ruled by Elven Queen Aelindra, never stepped down, a thousand-year-old giantess of thirteen to fifteen feet from Elven Accumulation release-or-die. She still waits for the missing Hero's return. She weighs counsel against pride as Rivona's marriage stirs old grievances and new hopes.
