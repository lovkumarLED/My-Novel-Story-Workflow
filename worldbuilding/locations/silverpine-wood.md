# Silverpine Wood

## Description

Silverpine Wood is a broad, shadowed woodland in the Western Borderlands. Though it can be dangerous, it is far more traversable than the Blackvein Wilds. Its eastern edge meets the Blackvein's outer fringe, where the two forests' different terrain and hazards overlap.

## History

For generations, hunters and patrols have used established trails through Silverpine. Recently, reports of unusually aggressive creatures have made some routes uncertain.

## Culture & Customs

Travelers tend to stay on marked paths and move in groups, especially near the forest boundary.

## Notable Features

The contact zone where Silverpine meets Blackvein is a natural route toward the deeper Wilds, but not a safe one. A damaged ley-cell (a compact magical unit that stores leyline power for devices) has been linked to unnatural disturbance in the area.

## Current State

Kael is fighting near Silverpine's edge, close to the boundary with the far more dangerous Blackvein Wilds. Rivona and her four friends are setting out to reach him.
