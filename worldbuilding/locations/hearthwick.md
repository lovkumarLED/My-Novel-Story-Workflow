# Hearthwick

## Description

Hearthwick is a quiet human farming village in the Western Borderlands, surrounded by grain fields, low stone walls, and weathered barns. It is a distant settlement, not a village beside the Blackvein Wilds; reaching the forest from here takes a substantial journey.

## History

Its residents have long depended on seasonal harvests and the trade roads that link scattered borderland farms to larger towns.

## Culture & Customs

Neighbors share tools and labor during planting and harvest. News from the imperial capital arrives slowly.

## Notable Features

The village's communal well and open-sided grain market serve as its meeting places.

## Current State

Hearthwick is a named distant village in the same broad borderland region; it is not Love's farmhouse and is not literally adjacent to the forest.
