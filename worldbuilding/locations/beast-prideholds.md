# Beast Prideholds

## Description

Savanna-edge strongholds, hunt-lodges, and border shrines. Beastfolk pride law rules daily life, with ritual hunts and oath-rings.

## History

Beast clans fought in the Dragon Wars, then accepted the Heavens-approved Three Laws 700+ years ago. Former Beast Queen Sahira fought beside the Hero as one of his main commanders, warrior and wife. When the Hero vanished with Dorna Kharzdar, she kept protecting his peace and led the clans, then gave rule to her brother's son, King Rokhar Pridemane. Since then she waits on the Hero's promise of a normal life, rejecting the public death-doctrine.

## Culture & Customs

Patience, pride, loyalty to oath. Contempt from dragons is remembered, so fair trial matters.

## Notable Features

The Beast Court, border shrines, pride assembly grounds.

## Current State

CANON per user 2026-10-11: Former queen Sahira gave rule to her brother's son, King Rokhar Pridemane, yet is still called Queen. Patron goddess Rakhora Pridemother watches the prides. She remains one of the most powerful people in the world, stronger than even the strongest human, sad in waiting yet charming, teasing, and cheerful beneath her intimidating power. Rivona's marriage is watched as possible sign. Iron Covenant cells whisper "Hero dead" here too.
