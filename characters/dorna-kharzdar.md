# Dorna Kharzdar

## Appearance

Daughter of Dwarf King Thorgrim Kharzdar and his late human wife. Half-dwarf heritage, sturdy and warm-eyed, forge-dressed with a traveler's cloak. Public portraits show her beside the Hero.

## Personality & Traits

Devoted, brave, loving. Loved the Hero and could not live without him.

## Backstory

Left Kharzdar Deep-Realm with the legendary Hero 700+ years ago to support him on his journey. PUBLIC BELIEF (doctrine from divine silence): both died/passed to another dimension with no return, because she loved him and went with him to death. The Gods say only "hero gets what he wants" and stay silent, so everyone believes it. The Three Who Wait (Aelindra, Sahira, Morwenna) reject this. HARD RULE: keep public-dead vs queens-waiting split; do not resolve early.

## Motivations & Goals

Wanted a simple life with the Hero she loved. Her fate seals Thorgrim's grief and succession.

## Location References

Kharzdar Deep-Realm clan archives — departure recorded, return never recorded; memorial shrine listed as dead.
