# Rivona

## Appearance

A dragon princess and heir-presumptive in her prime, tall and war-trained, with silver-white hair falling past her shoulders, amber eyes, two elegant golden horns, and faint golden scales shimmering along her temples. She favors black-and-gold (also crimson-and-gold) high-collared royal gowns, immaculate and dragon-crested. Her stillness is military; her courtesy is exact and freezing when she is angry.

## Personality & Traits

Proud, disciplined warrior with a jurist's respect for law. She despises the forced marriage at story open and does not hide it, yet she will not break a lawful binding by spite. Contradiction: cold to Love in public, privately observant of whether he keeps his word under humiliation.

## Backstory

Raised as Vorath's daughter and heir-presumptive, trained for command and schooled in the Hero's constitution. Her friends Kaelith and Serapha sharpened her pride; Mirelle and Dain widened it. The 700-year-old marriage law fell on her like a sentence, binding her to a poor human she never chose.

## Motivations & Goals

She wants freedom from the binding and restoration of her chosen future. She needs to see worth beyond blood and to judge conduct over birth. Want demands the law be undone; need demands she look harder before it is.

## Location References

Tied to the dragon court and its training grounds and council halls where her succession will be tested.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Cold formal draconic pride, complete sentences, never crude, never slang. Commands framed as judgments.

> "The law binds us. It does not require me to esteem you."
> "You claim honesty, human. Prove it where it costs you something."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Proud heir who equates human birth with unworthiness and hates the binding.
- **Key turning points:** Witnessing Love stand unbowed without cruelty; questioning Kaelith and Serapha's sabotage; hearing Mirelle and Dain's fair report of him.
- **Ending state:** One who can weigh a person beyond blood, whether the marriage stands or falls.
