# Garrick Thornvale

## Appearance

Elderly palace guard, white hair cropped short, weathered hands, plain worn uniform. Straight-backed despite age.

## Personality & Traits

Kind, steady, plain-spoken. Forty years at court never hardened him. He offers quiet dignity without ceremony.

## Backstory

Son of a farmer who told him: before holding a sword remember the land that feeds people. He entered palace service forty years ago and stayed an honest soldier.

## Motivations & Goals

He wants faithful service to House Vaeltharis to his last watch. He needs to shelter decency, beginning with Love.

## Location References

Palace gates, corridors, guest quarters.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Plain soldier warmth, short and kindly. Calls young men son.

> "Walk with me, son. The halls are longer than they look."
> "Rest tonight. Tomorrow the nobles will bait you. Answer plain and keep walking."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Loyal old guard, quietly kind.
- **Key turning points:** Treats Love with dignity first; escorts him to guest quarters with warning.
- **Ending state:** Proves honor outlasts rank.
