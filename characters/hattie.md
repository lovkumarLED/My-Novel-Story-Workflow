# Hattie

## Appearance

A sharp-eyed dragon heiress with copper-red hair pinned in clan rings and tailored court gowns with riding boots. Small beside warriors, she commands rooms by poise and a cutting smile.

## Personality & Traits

Proud, witty, and socially ruthless. Hattie never raises her voice; she lowers it. She keeps ledgers of favors and insults alike, yet guards her own household fiercely.

## Backstory

Heiress of an old dragon clan, Hattie was betrothed to Princess Rivona as part of a high-clan alliance. She played the court game perfectly. Rivona's forced marriage to the poor human Love, seven hundred years after the Hero's Three Laws, made Hattie a public embarrassment.

## Motivations & Goals

She wants her clan's honor restored through a new match or compensation. She needs to admit she wanted regard, not only rank, which pride forbids her to say.

## Location References

Dragon palace salons and her clan's townhouse; mentioned only.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Polished, ironic, precise. Uses endearments as weapons. Never crude or loud.

> "Darling, I was properly engaged. I do not simply evaporate."
> "We shall see who the court remembers in a year."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Scorned heiress gathering allies.
- **Key turning points:** Public snub backfires; private kindness from Rivona.
- **Ending state:** Graceful rival turned power broker, or scheming foe.
