# Aelindra

## Appearance

Ruler of the elven kingdom, still ruling and never stepped down, over a thousand years old, thirteen to fifteen feet tall from accumulated magical energy, for elves grow giant with age instead of wrinkling. A beautiful mature woman, silver-haired and luminous, she stoops gently through doorways in layered mourning silks.

## Personality & Traits

Towering, mournful, and immensely powerful, yet unfailingly gentle. Aelindra speaks softly because her voice carries magic. She grieves openly and comforts readily, but her sorrow can freeze a hall.

## Backstory

Once fiancee and wife of the human Hero who united the races under Three Laws 700+ years ago (Heavens-approved), she has waited ever since he vanished with Dorna Kharzdar. The world believes both dead in another dimension with no return, because the Gods stay silent ("hero gets what he wants"). She rejects that doctrine: the Hero promised her and the other two a proper, normal, simple life after hardship, and that life is unfulfilled. She still rules the elves in his stead. Elves who never release their gathered energy die of it — release-or-die; she endures by slow rites.

## Motivations & Goals

She wants the Hero's equality to hold and the Hero's promise of a normal life fulfilled. She needs to release the past without abandoning it, torn between mourning and guardianship, and refuses the Gods' silence as proof of death.

## Location References

Elven high hall and dragon palace memorial shrine; her tower retreat is mentioned only.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Slow, musical, elegiac. Long sentences, old forms. Never brisk or mocking.

> "I remember when these laws were only a promise, little ones."
> "Rest now. Grief is heavy enough without carrying it alone."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Distant mourner observing the young.
- **Key turning points:** Asked to judge the new marriage; pressed to spend her power.
- **Ending state:** Blessing elder, or spent guardian.
