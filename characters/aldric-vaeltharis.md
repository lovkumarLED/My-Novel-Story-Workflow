# Aldric Vaeltharis

## Appearance

The dragon king, elder and massive even in human form, broad-shouldered with silver-streaked hair and slow ceremonial movement. He wears the plain heavy crown of state rather than ornament, and his gaze settles like a seal on a decree.

## Personality & Traits

Stern, political, and legalistic. He loves his daughter but will not bend a 700-year-old constitution for her comfort. He watches Love not with hatred but with measurement: does this human weaken the throne or prove the law wiser than its critics. Patient, formal, immovable once decided.

## Backstory

Son of Old Dragon King Zarvath Vaeltharis, who was one of the legendary Hero's commanders and fought beside him. During the war Aldric served in the Hero's army in a junior position alongside his father, but never saw the Hero himself. After his father passed, Aldric ascended through succession wars and learned that dragons obey custom only while the crown obeys it first. He does not know what his father promised the Hero — no one knows to this day what Zarvath promised. Bound by the Heavens-approved constitution, he honored the marriage law that joined Rivona to Love, knowing it would enrage the nobility and test his heir, because refusal would fracture precedent. Like almost everyone, he believes the Hero died/went to another dimension 700+ years ago with Dorna Kharzdar and cannot return, since the Gods stay silent.

## Motivations & Goals

He wants stability of the realm and succession secured under law. He needs to prove the old law can still produce strength rather than shame. That pits father against king whenever Rivona pleads for release.

## Location References

Tied to the dragon throne room, council chambers, and archives holding the Hero's constitution.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Royal legal diction, slow declarative sentences, precedent cited as argument.

> "The crown upholds what the Hero wrote. I do not set it aside for discomfort."
> "Stability is the only heirloom that matters. Prove the law sound by your conduct."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Stern guardian of precedent who enforces the binding without favor.
- **Key turning points:** Observing Love's endurance under noble sabotage; weighing Rivona's growth against court pressure.
- **Ending state:** A king whose law is vindicated or revised by evidence, not by outcry.
