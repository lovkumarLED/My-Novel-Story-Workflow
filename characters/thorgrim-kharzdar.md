# Thorgrim Kharzdar

## Appearance

The very old Dwarf King of Kharzdar Deep-Realm, broad and iron-grey, hands scarred from forge and ley-engine works. Moves slowly but carries deep authority.

## Personality & Traits

Forge-honor, contract-honor, clan record-keeping. Stern, grieving, dutiful. Keeps every debt in the archives.

## Backstory

Long-time ruler of the Deep-Realm. His wife was human and is no longer alive. His daughter Dorna Kharzdar left the kingdom with the legendary Hero to support him 700+ years ago because she loved him and could not live without him. PUBLIC BELIEF: both died in another dimension with no return; Gods stay silent ("hero gets what he wants"), so everyone believes it. Thorgrim is very old and a succession ceremony approaches where rule will pass to his sister's son Braldor Kharzdar.

## Motivations & Goals

He wants the clan's honor and engines secured, and news of his missing daughter. He needs to pass rule cleanly to Braldor Kharzdar. Patron god Durzak Stoneheart guards his conscience.

## Location References

Kharzdar Deep-Realm, Embergate forge-halls, clan archives where his daughter's departure is recorded only as missing.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Short, weighty, record-like. Cites clan law.

> "Stone remembers what men forget."
> "Forge-honor holds. The clan records all."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Aged king awaiting succession.
- **Key turning points:** Asked to back Rivona's marriage leverage; pressed on missing daughter.
- **Ending state:** Retired elder, or recalled witness.
