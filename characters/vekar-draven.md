# Vekar Draven

## Appearance

A broad-shouldered young dragon lord with bronze scales at his temples and a soldier's braid. He wears parade armor even to court dinners and carries himself like a duelist, chin high and hands ready.

## Personality & Traits

Proud, hot-tempered, and punctilious about honor. Vekak respects strength and despises evasion. He sulks when outmaneuvered politically but will keep any oath he swears aloud.

## Backstory

Heir to a dragon war-clan, Vekak was presented as a suitor for Princess Rivona to bind the clans to the throne. He expected to win her by tournament. Her forced marriage to the poor human Love, seven hundred years after the Hero's Three Laws, left him publicly displaced.

## Motivations & Goals

He wants Rivona's hand restored to him to save his clan's standing. He needs to learn that worth is not proven by victory alone, which puts pride against the equality the Laws demand.

## Location References

Dragon palace tourney yard and clan barracks; his family's frontier hold is mentioned only.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Blunt, martial, short sentences with formal challenges. Never whines or hedges.

> "If the human wants my bride, let him face me in the open."
> "I swore it before witnesses. A Vekak does not take it back."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Humiliated claimant demanding redress.
- **Key turning points:** Losing fairly to an inferior; being shown mercy by Rivona.
- **Ending state:** Grudging respect for Love, or a hardened political enemy.
