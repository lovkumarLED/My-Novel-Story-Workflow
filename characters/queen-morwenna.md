# Queen Morwenna

## Appearance

A majestically beautiful mature demon woman more than two thousand years old, tall and unhurried, with dark horns veiled in silver and ember-calm eyes. She favored retired black-and-crimson with old jewels, and carried immense power lightly. Being demon, she ages very slowly and dies hard, suffering no elven energy-death.

## Personality & Traits

Ancient, amused, and terrifyingly polite. Morwenna asked soft questions and let silence scold. Courts feared her courtesy more than threats, for it never slipped.

## Backstory

More than two thousand years ago she loved only the legendary human Hero and has loved no one else. She had no child with him. When he vanished 700+ years ago with Dorna Kharzdar, the world declared both dead in another dimension because the Gods stay silent ("hero gets what he wants"). She rejects that: the Hero promised her, Aelindra, and Sahira a proper, normal, simple life after hardship, unfulfilled. She retired and yielded the demon day-throne to her younger brother Malachar Voss, one of her little brothers. King Malachar now holds the seat, yet every court knows the Old Queen watches.

## Motivations & Goals

She wants the Hero's peace to hold and his promise of a normal life fulfilled. She needs to find the one he promised, torn between detachment and loyalty. Like the other two, she waits because she does not believe the death-doctrine.

## Location References

Demon court dowager apartments and dragon palace embassy; her country retreat was mentioned only.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Ancient, amused, terrifyingly polite. Soft address, never crude.

> "Sit down, my dear. We shall see what courtesy can mend."
> "How amusing. Quiet is not absence."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Retired watcher, detached and courteous.
- **Key turning points:** Asked to judge the promised one.
- **Ending state:** Revealed guardian, or vanished legend.
