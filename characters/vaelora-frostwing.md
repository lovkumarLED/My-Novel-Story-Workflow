# Vaelora Frostwing

## Appearance

A dragon-noble lady of striking poise, silver-gold hair arranged in court fashion, gowns cut for both beauty and rank display. Her pitying smile is her weapon; she never raises her voice, which makes her dismissals land harder in crowded rooms.

## Personality & Traits

Coldly courteous, observant, and hostile to Love beneath perfect manners. Where Kaelith duels, she isolates: seating slights, gossip framed as concern, praise that marks him as a pet. Fiercely protective of Rivona's status and convinced kindness to Love would ruin her friend's reign before it begins.

## Backstory

Educated with Rivona in history and diplomacy, Serapha learned that marriages are alliances. A human pauper as princess-consort offends her entire training. She allied with Kaelith to document every stumble Love makes, intending to hand Rivona lawful grounds for contempt if not release.

## Motivations & Goals

She wants Rivona freed from a degrading match and the court spared embarrassment. She needs to admit that worth can appear without breeding, which threatens her worldview. Her weapons are social: exclusion, rumor, and tests of etiquette designed to fail.

## Location References

Tied to court salons, banquet halls, and galleries where reputation is decided.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Sweet condescension, diminutives, concern that insults by implication.

> "Poor thing, you look lost. Do not embarrass her before the envoys."
> "Breeding shows, as expected. We all see you trying."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Smiling gatekeeper certain Love cannot learn court dignity.
- **Key turning points:** Love passing a fair test she rigged to fail; Mirelle and Dain openly vouching for his conduct.
- **Ending state:** Either forced courtesy that becomes real respect, or open opposition to Rivona's judgment.
