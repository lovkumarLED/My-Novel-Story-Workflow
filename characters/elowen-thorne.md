# Elowen Thorne

## Appearance

An elf of quiet grace, slender with leaf-green eyes and hair worn loose with a single scholar's braid. She favors practical traveling robes over court finery, ink-stained fingers, and a satchel of letters and remedies that marks her as mediator rather than duelist.

## Personality & Traits

Patient, curious, and fair-minded. As Rivona's friend she refuses to hate Love on command and insists on evidence over birth. She translates court rules for him, warns him of traps, and reports honestly to Rivona what she sees. Her support is conditional: she gives chances, not cover for wrongdoing.

## Backstory

Sent as an elven liaison to the dragon court, Mirelle befriended Rivona through shared study of law and history. Elves remember being judged by longer-lived powers, so she recognized Love's position at once and chose to test him fairly while Kaelith and Serapha tested him cruelly.

## Motivations & Goals

She wants peace between peoples and a lawful outcome that harms neither friend nor stranger. She needs to keep her neutrality when both sides demand she choose. Each fair test she sets for Love risks her standing with the dragon-nobles.

## Location References

Tied to the court library, envoy quarters, and herb gardens where private talks can happen.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Soft elven cadence, questions before judgments, counsel phrased as invitations.

> "Give him a chance, Rivona. A fair test, then judge. Listen first."
> "Gently. He is learning our laws faster than we learned his name."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Neutral observer offering Love basic fairness.
- **Key turning points:** Defending his honest conduct to Rivona; opposing Kaelith and Serapha's rigged trials.
- **Ending state:** Trusted bridge whose testimony helps Rivona see worth beyond blood.
