# Sahira

## Appearance

The former Beast Queen — gave rule to her brother's son yet still called Queen by her people — still young-looking despite her years, with tawny eyes and a mature, commanding beauty. She wears hunt leathers under royal gold and moves with feline calm, every gesture deliberate. Powerful and intimidating, yet charming, teasing, and cheerful beneath.

## Personality & Traits

Wise, powerful, and patient, stronger than even the strongest human. Sahira was one of the main commanders of the Hero and still one of the most powerful people in the world. She rules appetite by ritual and counsel. She is warm to the faithful, merciless to oath-breakers, and honest about hunger, grief, and loyalty alike. Really sad waiting for the missing Hero, yet charming, teasing, and cheerful despite her intimidating power.

## Backstory

Also once fiancee and wife of the old Hero who united the races under Three Laws 700+ years ago (Heavens-approved), she fought beside him as one of his main commanders and a true warrior through great hardship. When the Hero vanished with Dorna Kharzdar, the world declared both dead in another dimension because the Gods stay silent ("hero gets what he wants"). She rejects that: the Hero promised her, Aelindra, and Morwenna a proper, normal, simple life, unfulfilled. She kept protecting his peace, led the beast clans, then gave the throne to her brother's son King Rokhar Pridemane, the current Beast ruler. Since then she waits for his return. Patron goddess Rakhora Pridemother.

## Motivations & Goals

She wants the waiting to mean something, watching Rivona's forced marriage to poor human Love for that sign of the promised one. She needs to choose between the promised one she still awaits and the living duty before her.

## Location References

Beast court and dragon palace guest apartments; border shrines are mentioned only.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Low, sure, proverbial. Speaks of patience and pride. Never shrill or petty.

> "Patience, my dear. The pride knows when the time is ripe."
> "I have waited long. I can wait well a little longer."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Serene watcher testing omens.
- **Key turning points:** Recognizing promise in Love; called to defend the Laws.
- **Ending state:** Matron protector, or disappointed power.
