# Charles VII

## Appearance

The crown prince of the largest human kingdom, tall and golden in parade plate, with a duelist's grace. The strongest human alive, he moves with trained ease and bows with exact courtesy.

## Personality & Traits

Chivalrous, disciplined, and sincerely courteous, yet certain that rank decides worth. He protects the weak but expects deference, and cannot imagine a poor human as a dragon princess's equal.

## Backstory

Raised to embody human knighthood after the Hero united the races under Three Laws of equality seven hundred years ago, Charles trained in arms and magitech warcraft. His suit for Princess Rivona was to seal human-dragon peace. Her forced marriage to the poor human Love offended both his heart and his order.

## Motivations & Goals

He wants to prove high birth must wed high birth, to defend his world's order. He needs to learn worth is not rank, the story's counter-premise voice tested against the Laws.

## Location References

Dragon palace lists and the human royal embassy; his kingdom's capital is mentioned only.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Courtly, measured, uses duty language. Never vulgar or familiar.

> "Chivalry demands I defend the lady's honor, even from her own haste."
> "A crown is not vanity, friend. It is order, as befits us all."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Noble rival certain Love must yield.
- **Key turning points:** Defeated or outmatched in honor by Love; rebuked under the Laws.
- **Ending state:** Humbled ally, or entrenched defender of rank.
