# John Ashford

## Appearance

A lean human sky-captain in his thirties from a Chinese-culture port nation, with oil under his nails and a pilot's long coat. He walks fast, checks the sky for weather, and smiles like a man used to crews.

## Personality & Traits

Proud, practical, and competitive. John trusts engines, charts, and work over birth. He is generous to his own people and thin-skinned about being called lowborn by higher nobles.

## Backstory

A common-born pilot who rose by flying magitech planes powered by magical energy, John won honors carrying mail and medicine between nations. A marriage contract with Princess Rivona was meant to tie the sky guilds to the dragon throne. Her forced marriage to the poor human Love ruined that plan.

## Motivations & Goals

He wants the contract honored or repaid in full, to protect his guild's standing. He needs to see that a man's value is not his usefulness, which clashes with his pride in earned rank.

## Location References

Dragon palace guest wing; sky-docks and magitech airfields are mentioned only.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Plain, brisk, full of flying terms. Never flowery or cruel.

> "My route was filed and approved. I do not vanish for politics."
> "Engines do not care who your father was. Neither do I."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Wronged contractor demanding payment.
- **Key turning points:** Forced to work beside Love; offered a lesser post.
- **Ending state:** Ally of the marriage, or a persistent legal foe.
