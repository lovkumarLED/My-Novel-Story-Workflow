# Azravel Voss

## Appearance

Demon Prince Azravel Voss, lean and sharp-horned, with restless ember eyes and black-trimmed uniforms. He paces where his father sits still, always armed though never openly threatening in court.

## Personality & Traits

Ambitious, clever, and hungry for proof. Azravel despises being the second generation of peace. He flatters rivals while mapping their faults and sulks when his mother Lysandra restrains him.

## Backstory

Son of Demon King Malachar Voss and Queen Lysandra Voss, elder brother to Princess Evelith Voss, Azravel was raised on tales of demon glory before the Hero's Three Laws. Rivona's forced marriage to poor human Love convinces him the dragon throne is weak and ripe for pressure.

## Motivations & Goals

He wants the dragon throne diminished or beholden to him, to win a crown history will note. He needs limits he refuses, torn between his parents' caution and his sister Evelith's safer path.

## Location References

Demon war college and dragon palace outer courts; border garrisons are mentioned only.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Hungry, taunting, grandiose. Short boasts and threats. Never humble or gentle.

> "Weakness invites appetite. Even dragons should know that."
> "What is mine by right will kneel to a new order."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Scheming prince probing cracks.
- **Key turning points:** Plot exposed by Love or Rivona; disowned or checked by parents.
- **Ending state:** Chastened heir, or open foe.
