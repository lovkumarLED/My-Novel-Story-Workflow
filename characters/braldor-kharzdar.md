# Braldor Kharzdar

## Appearance

Nephew-heir of King Thorgrim Kharzdar (his sister's son), broad-shouldered dwarf engineer with copper-braided beard and ley-burn scars on his forearms.

## Personality & Traits

Dutiful, ledger-minded, forge-proud. Reveres clan records and dreads failing his grieving uncle.

## Backstory

Son of Thorgrim's sister. Named heir-apparent for the coming succession ceremony in Kharzdar Deep-Realm. Raised in Embergate forge-halls while cousin Dorna left with the Hero 700+ years ago and is publicly believed dead with him.

## Motivations & Goals

Wants a clean succession and stable ore/engine contracts. Needs Thorgrim's blessing and clan council approval.

## Location References

Kharzdar Deep-Realm, Embergate forge-halls.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Blunt, respectful, measurement-first.

> "The clan holds. Count it twice."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Waiting heir.
- **Key turning points:** Named successor during Rivona crisis.
- **Ending state:** New Dwarf King or contested heir.
