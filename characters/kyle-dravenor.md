# Kyle Dravenor

## Appearance

A young dragon warrior of good House Dravenor, lean and sun-browned, with ember-flecked eyes and a soldier's knot. He wore plain service blues with house bronze, and moved like a swordsman, ready but never swaggering.

## Personality & Traits

Skilled, honorable, and quietly regretful. Kyle spoke plainly, owned mistakes quickly, and bore no malice. He respected Love after seeing him stand unbowed, though it shamed his own silence.

## Backstory

He graduated the war academy alongside Rivona, drilled in the same cohort and bloodied in the same border skirmish. They grew attached, not love but the almost that never became. She half-expected he would step forward when the binding fell; he never did.

## Motivations & Goals

He wanted to serve honorably and repair House Dravenor's pride without duel or scheme. He needed to face his own hesitation, torn between old affection and fair respect for the marriage law.

## Location References

War academy halls and dragon palace tourney yard; his family's frontier hold was mentioned only.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Direct soldier warmth. Plain words, short sentences, never cruel or flowery.

> "I should have spoken then. I will not lie about it now."
> "He stood unbowed. I respect that, whatever blood he carries."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Loyal classmate nursing quiet regret.
- **Key turning points:** Seeing Love stand firm; refusing Vekar's quarrel.
- **Ending state:** Steadfast friend, or distant well-wisher.
