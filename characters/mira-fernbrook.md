# Mira Fernbrook

## Appearance

A petite elven palace maid with braided ash-blonde hair and quick hands, still far from the giant stature of elder elves. She wears crisp grey livery and moves silently except when giggling.

## Personality & Traits

Gossipy, loyal, and soft-hearted. Sylvi knows every corridor rumor in the dragon palace and repeats most of them to Ilsa, but she never betrays Princess Rivona. She fusses over tea, hems, and feelings alike.

## Backstory

Hired from an elven service house to attend Rivona's household, Sylvi has served through betrothal season and scandal. She watched three proud suitors circle, then saw Rivona forced to wed poor human Love under laws older than any of them.

## Motivations & Goals

She wants her mistress comfortable and the household calm. She needs to keep confidences under pressure from curious nobles, torn between chatter and loyalty.

## Location References

Dragon palace laundry, linen rooms, and Rivona's chambers.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Bright, breathless, whispery. Exclamations and questions. Never grave or coarse.

> "Hush, did you hear what the bronze lord said at breakfast?"
> "Poor lamb, sparkle up. We pressed your best sash."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Cheerful witness to court drama.
- **Key turning points:** Asked to spy; chooses loyalty.
- **Ending state:** Trusted confidante.
