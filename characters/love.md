# Love

## Appearance

A poor human young man in his early twenties, lean from hard labor rather than training. Dark untidy hair, work-roughened hands, and plain patched clothing he keeps mended and clean. He stands straight when nervous and meets dragon gazes longer than he should, which others read as either bravery or ignorance.

## Personality & Traits

Kind, stubborn, and literal-minded about honesty. He apologizes too fast, works without being asked, and refuses charity he has not earned. Under pressure he goes quiet instead of grandstanding, then acts. His pride is small and human: to be useful, to keep his word, to live without shame.

## Backstory

Raised in a debt-pressed human household on the edge of dragon territory, Love worked odd jobs and carried other people's ledgers. An ancient law from the 700-year-old Hero's constitution named him as the bound husband of the dragon heir, dragging a nobody into court politics he never studied and cannot buy his way through.

## Motivations & Goals

He wants a quiet honest life: paid work, a small roof, no masters. He needs to stand unbowed among dragons without becoming cruel like those who despise him. The want pulls him toward escape; the need forces him to stay and be judged daily.

## Location References

Tied to the human border village of his birth and the dragon court where the binding holds him. No fixed holdings of his own.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Plain, short sentences, working-class politeness, repeated promises. He asks questions rather than commands.

> "I promised honest work. Let me work, then judge me."
> "Sorry. I did not know the custom. Tell me once and I will keep it."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Apologetic outsider who believes endurance alone will earn him peace.
- **Key turning points:** Refusing to grovel for Kaelith and Serapha; accepting Mirelle and Dain's fair tests; answering Rivona's contempt with steady conduct rather than surrender.
- **Ending state:** A man who stands unbowed, worthy of negotiation rather than pity.
