# Elyndra Moonveil

## Appearance

A tall elven palace maid with dark hair in a severe knot, older than Sylvi yet still slender and young-faced as elves remain before their giant growth. Her livery is always immaculate, keys at her belt.

## Personality & Traits

Gossipy but guarded, loyal to Rivona above all. Ilsa scolds Sylvi for chatter, then adds the missing detail. Practical, warm beneath starch, she steadies rooms by tidying them.

## Backstory

Senior maid of Rivona's household, Ilsa trained Sylvi and managed the linen and propriety through every suitor's visit. She saw proud offers from Vekak, John, Hattie, and Charles VII collapse when Rivona was forced to wed poor human Love.

## Motivations & Goals

She wants the household's honor kept and Rivona protected from scandal. She needs to guide Sylvi without crushing her, torn between order and kindness.

## Location References

Dragon palace stillroom, pantries, and Rivona's chambers.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Crisp, motherly, proverbial. Corrects gently, soothes firmly. Never shrill or vulgar.

> "Steady now. Tidy room, tidy mind, mark me."
> "There, there. The palace talks, but we do not."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Watchful keeper of routine.
- **Key turning points:** Pressed for secrets; shields the couple.
- **Ending state:** Respected housekeeper.
