# Rokhar Pridemane

## Appearance

Current Beast King, nephew of Sahira (her brother's son). Tall leonine beastman with dark mane, scarred muzzle, gold-ringed ears. Wears pride-cloak over hunt leathers.

## Personality & Traits

Proud, direct, oath-bound. Defers to Aunt Sahira in spiritual matters but guards his own throne fiercely.

## Backstory

Son of Sahira's brother. Given rule by Sahira after she kept protecting the Hero's peace following the Hero's vanishing 700+ years ago with Dorna Kharzdar. Rules the Prideholds under her watch while she waits on the Hero's promise of a normal life.

## Motivations & Goals

Wants pride unity and border strength. Needs to prove himself beyond "Sahira's nephew" during Rivona's marriage crisis.

## Location References

Beast Prideholds, Beast Court, pride assembly grounds.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Short, growling, formal pride-speech.

> "The pride knows. Speak plain."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Young king under legend's shadow.
- **Key turning points:** Tested by Iron Covenant agents and Rivona omen.
- **Ending state:** Proven king or recalled cub.
