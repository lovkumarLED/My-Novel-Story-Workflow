# Malachar Voss

## Appearance

The current Demon King and younger brother of Queen Morwenna, tall and obsidian-horned, with ember eyes and heavy court robes cut for war. He sits utterly still and lets others fidget, power held in reserve. Like all demons, he ages very slowly and dies hard.

## Personality & Traits

Cold, calculating, and proud of demon discipline. Morvain keeps treaties to the letter and exploits every gap. He loves his house fiercely and trusts almost no one outside it.

## Backstory

Younger brother of Queen Morwenna, who loved only the Hero and had no child with him. When the Hero went missing 700+ years ago, Morwenna yielded day-rule to Malachar. He has kept an uneasy peace with dragons and humans while building magitech foundries. Rivona's forced marriage to poor human Love looks to him like dragon weakness.

## Motivations & Goals

He wants advantage without open war, using law and marriage claims. He needs to restrain his son Azravel's hunger for the dragon throne, torn between dynasty and stability.

## Location References

Demon court and dragon palace embassy; border foundries are mentioned only.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Formal, flat, final. Short decrees, no humor. Never babbles or begs.

> "Order is kept, or it is taken. The throne speaks once."
> "Everything measured has a price. Pay it, or depart."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Patient opportunist watching dragons.
- **Key turning points:** Azravel's scheme exposed; pressed to choose law or gain.
- **Ending state:** Cold guarantor of peace, or sanctioned schemer.
