# Nymera Ashveil

## Appearance

A bear-stock beastwoman, broad-shouldered and powerfully built, with coarse dark fur at her jaw and forearms. She wears plain reinforced work clothes instead of livery. Her size can intimidate at first glance; her careful gentleness with smaller folk quickly corrects the impression.

## Personality & Traits

Blunt, big-hearted, and practical. As Rivona's friend she judges by labor shared rather than lineage recited. She supports Love by giving him real work and real chances: carrying, guarding, mending, standing beside him when nobles sneer. She distrusts pretty speeches and trusts calloused hands and kept promises.

## Backstory

Hired first as a guard-captain's aide, Dain earned Rivona's respect by holding a collapsing gate mechanism long enough for others to escape. Beastfolk know contempt from dragons too, so she recognized the game Kaelith and Serapha played against Love and decided to balance it with plain fellowship.

## Motivations & Goals

She wants a court where strength of character counts beside strength of blood. She needs to remain Rivona's loyal friend while openly backing the husband she despises, without forcing her to choose between them too soon.

## Location References

Tied to the barracks, forge yards, and outer wards where honest work is visible.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Short gruff sentences, work metaphors, encouragement delivered as orders.

> "Stand up. Prove it working, shoulder to shoulder, and I will vouch for you."
> "I have seen worse odds. Earn it, lad, and ignore their noise."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Sympathetic bystander who offers Love labor instead of pity.
- **Key turning points:** Publicly standing with Love during a rigged trial; telling Rivona plainly what she witnessed.
- **Ending state:** The blunt witness whose fairness helps turn contempt toward respect.
