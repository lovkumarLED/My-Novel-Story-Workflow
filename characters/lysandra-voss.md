# Lysandra Voss

## Appearance

Demon Queen Lysandra Voss, statuesque with swept-back horns veiled in gold gauze, famed for a calm smile. She dresses in deep violet and wears her age as authority rather than display.

## Personality & Traits

Shrewd, maternal, and diplomatic. Lysandra softens Malachar's edicts and steers her children with quiet pressure. She collects secrets the way others collect jewels and spends them sparingly.

## Backstory

Wed to Demon King Malachar Voss — younger brother of Queen Morwenna, who loved only the missing Hero — to unite rival demon lines after the Hero's peace, she bore him two children: ambitious Prince Azravel Voss and his younger sister Evelith Voss. She has kept the house united while Azravel eyes the dragon throne's weakness after Rivona's forced marriage to poor human Love.

## Motivations & Goals

She wants both children secure, Evelith sheltered and Azravel crowned with honor, not ruin. She needs to curb Azravel without breaking him, torn between mother and queen.

## Location References

Demon court nursery wing and dragon palace reception halls; her private garden is mentioned only.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Smooth, low, persuasive but firm. Maternal phrases with steel beneath. Never screams or threatens openly.

> "Listen first, my children. The house endures by wisdom."
> "Gently. Even queens must knock before they enter."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Peacemaker holding family and court together.
- **Key turning points:** Forced to betray Azravel's plot or shield it; Evelith endangered.
- **Ending state:** Respected mediator, or grieving mother.
