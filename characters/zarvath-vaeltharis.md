# Zarvath Vaeltharis

## Appearance

Old Dragon King, father of current King Aldric Vaeltharis. Massive warrior-king in memory and portraiture.

## Personality & Traits

Commander's honor, oath-bound, secretive about his promise.

## Backstory

One of the legendary Hero's commanders in the Dragon Wars. Fought beside the Hero with his young son Aldric serving as a junior in the same army (Aldric never saw the Hero). Zarvath promised the Hero something unknown — HARD RULE: no one knows to this day what he promised, not even Aldric. Deceased before story open; keep in mentions only for flashbacks.

## Motivations & Goals

Wanted dragon survival under law. His unknown promise drives Act Two+ mystery.

## Location References

Drakon Court, Oath Hall, Throne Aerie archives.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Known only from records. Formal draconic command.

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Hero's commander.
- **Key turning points:** Unknown promise to missing Hero.
- **Ending state:** Dead king whose promise still binds.
