# Does the Dragon Princess Really Hate Me?
## Chapter 1 — Act 4: The Farmer Who Refused To Bow To Destiny

Imperial Palace of Aurelthys — Royal Reception Hall

Tum dheere se apni jagah se khade hue.

Royal hall ke bade pillars ke beech tumhari saadgi aur tumhare kapde baaki sabse bilkul alag dikh rahe the. Tumhare saamne Dragon Empire ke Emperor khade the, aur unke paas unki beti—Princess Rivona Vaeltharis.

Tumne Rivona ke saamne jhukkar ek achhe sevak ki tarah samman diya.

Rivona ki amber aankhon ne tumhari har harkat ko gaur se dekha.

Jab tumne kaha ki tum investigation mein poora saath doge, kyunki tumhe bhi jald se jald apne ghar lautna hai, toh uske chehre par halki si hairani aayi.

Rivona: "Kam se kam is baat par hum dono ek raay rakhte hain. Mujhe bhi is maamle ka sach jald se jald jaan-na hai."

Uski awaaz ab bhi thandi thi, lekin usne tumhare jhukne par koi comment nahi kiya.

Phir tumne King Aldric ko apne maa-baap ke baare mein bataya.

Unhone tumhe poori azaadi di thi ki tum apni zindagi mein kya karna chahte ho. Tum unhe apne saath shehar le jaana chahte the, lekin unhone apne khet ko apna ghar maana tha. Tumne unki khwahish ka samman kiya, kyunki woh khet tumhare liye bhi utna hi maayne rakhta tha jitna tumhare maa-baap ke liye.

King Aldric tumhari baat bina beech mein roke sunte rahe.

Lekin jab tumne kisaan ki mehnat aur uski izzat ke baare mein bolna shuru kiya, toh unke chehre par ek alag hi expression ubhar aaya.

Tumne kaha ki mahal ke andar rehne wale logon se lekar aam gharon tak, har jagah khana kisaan hi ugaata hai. Woh mausam se ladta hai, jaanwaron aur monsters se apni fasal bachata hai, aur tab jaakar logon ki thaali mein khana pahunchta hai.

Tumne aakhir mein maafi maangi ki agar tumne kuch galat keh diya ho.

Kuch pal tak hall mein khamoshi rahi.

Phir King Aldric hans pade.

Yeh koi mazaak udaane wali hansi nahi thi. Balki ek gehri, khuli hansi thi, jo unke aas-paas khade guards ne shayad bahut kam suni thi.

King Aldric: "Galat? Tumne aisa kya galat keh diya, Love?"

Unhone apni hansi roki aur tumhari taraf dekha.

King Aldric: "Mujhe batao, agar ek din mere Empire ke saare kisaan apne auzaar rakh dein aur kheton mein jaana band kar dein, toh kya hoga?"

Unhone jawaab ka intezaar kiye bina khud hi kaha—

King Aldric: "Mere mahal ke rasoiye sone ke bartan saja sakte hain. Mere soldiers apni talwaarein chamka sakte hain. Mere merchants apne khazane gin sakte hain. Lekin agar anaj hi nahi uga, toh kuch hi samay mein in sabki chamak bekaar ho jayegi."

Unki awaaz ab poore hall mein saaf sunayi de rahi thi.

King Aldric: "Ek Emperor apne Empire ki raksha kar sakta hai. Ek warrior uski seemaon ko bacha sakta hai. Lekin ek kisaan har din un logon ki zindagi bachata hai jinse woh shayad kabhi mila bhi nahi."

King ne apna haath tumhari taraf badhaya, lekin tumhe chhoone ke bajaye use apne seene par rakh liya.

King Aldric: "Tumhe apne kaam ke liye maafi maangne ki zaroorat nahi hai."

Unki nazar ek pal ke liye tumhare kapdon par gayi, phir tumhari aankhon par laut aayi.

King Aldric: "Aur ek baat yaad rakhna. Kisi insaan ka kaam uski izzat ko chhota nahi karta. Uske karm karte hain."

### Princess ka sawaal

Rivona ab tak chup thi.

Usne apne pita ki baat suni, phir tumhari taraf dekha. Uske liye royal court mein aise logon ko dekhna aam tha jo apni haisiyat badhane ke liye har mauka dhoondhte the.

Noble families apne bachchon ko powerful positions dilana chahti thi. Merchants royal contracts ke peeche bhaagte the. Warriors titles aur recognition ke liye ladte the.

Aur yahan ek insaan tha jo Dragon Empire ke Emperor ke saamne khada hokar keh raha tha ki woh bas apne khet mein lautna chahta hai.

Na koi title.

Na koi khazana.

Na koi royal position.

Sirf apna ghar.

Rivona: "Tum jaante ho ki agar Registry ka faisla valid raha, toh tumhe ek royal princess ke saath joda jayega?"

Usne apna sir thoda tedha kiya.

Rivona: "Tumhe royal privileges mil sakte hain. Zameen, dhan, security... aur ek aisi zindagi jiske baare mein tumhare gaon ke zyadaatar log sirf sapna dekh sakte hain."

Woh ek pal ruki.

Rivona: "Aur tum phir bhi apne khet mein lautna chahte ho?"

Uske sawaal mein ab sirf gussa nahi tha. Usmein asli hairani bhi thi.

Rivona: "Main tumhari khwahish ko samajhne ki koshish kar rahi hoon, lekin mujhe yeh samajh nahi aata ki koi itna bada mauka apne haathon se kyun jaane dega."

King Aldric ne apni beti ki taraf dekha, lekin is baar unhone use beech mein nahi roka.

Woh bhi tumhara jawaab sunna chahte the.

### Ek anjaan aadmi ki izzat

Tabhi hall ke ek taraf khade elderly royal guard ne apna sir jhukaya.

Uska naam Garrick Thornvale tha. Woh pichhle chalis saal se Imperial Palace ki seva kar raha tha aur uske baal ab lagbhag poori tarah safed ho chuke the.

Garrick: "Your Majesty, agar ijazat ho toh main ek baat kehna chahta hoon."

King ne use bolne ka ishara kiya.

Garrick: "Mere pita bhi ek kisaan the. Main military mein bharti hua, lekin meri maa aur pita ne apni poori zindagi kheton mein guzaari. Jab main pehli baar training ke liye palace aaya tha, tab mere pita ne mujhe kaha tha ki talwaar pakadne se pehle yaad rakhna—jis zameen ko tum bachane ja rahe ho, usi zameen se logon ka pet bharta hai."

Usne tumhari taraf dekha aur halka sa sir jhukaya.

Garrick: "Tumne mujhe unki yaad dila di."

Rivona ki nazar guard par gayi, phir tum par.

Usne kuch nahi kaha, lekin uska pehle wala expression ab utna sakht nahi tha.

Seraphine ki awaaz achanak hall ke darwaze se sunayi di.

Seraphine: "Princess, kya hum sach mein ek aam kisaan ki baaton se itne prabhavit hone wale hain? Yeh sab bahut achha sunayi deta hai, lekin isse Registry ka sach nahi badal jaata."

Seraphine andar aayi. Uske saath Vaelora bhi thi, jiske haath mein kuch documents the.

King Aldric ka chehra dobara Emperor jaisa ho gaya.

King Aldric: "Kisi ne yeh nahi kaha ki Registry ka sach badal gaya hai. Hum ek insaan ki baat sun rahe hain. Ismein koi burai nahi hai."

Seraphine ne apne hoth daba liye, lekin woh aage kuch nahi boli.

Vaelora ne documents King ko diye.

Vaelora: "Your Majesty, High Court ko preliminary notice bhej diya gaya hai. Original Registry record ki formal examination kal subah shuru ho sakti hai. Lekin court ne ek aur baat kahi hai."

King Aldric: "Kaunsi baat?"

Vaelora ne ek pal Rivona ki taraf dekha.

Vaelora: "Agar marriage bond valid hua, toh Princess aur Love dono ko apna-apna paksh rakhna hoga. Court kisi ek ki marzi ko doosre ki marzi se zyada mahatva nahi de sakti."

Rivona ki aankhen sikud gayin.

Rivona: "Toh kal humein dono ko court ke saamne khada hona hoga."

Vaelora: "Ji, Your Highness."

King Aldric ne documents band kar diye.

King Aldric: "Theek hai. Kal subah High Court mein sunwai hogi. Tab tak Love palace ke guest quarters mein rahega. Use aaram, khana aur zaroori suvidhayein di jayengi. Koi bhi usse bina meri ya Princess ki anumati ke interrogate nahi karega."

Phir unhone tumhari taraf dekha.

King Aldric: "Love, tumne apne maa-baap ki izzat ki baat ki. Kal tumhe apne liye bhi wahi karna hoga. Court ke saamne sach bolna. Kisi se darne ki zaroorat nahi hai."

Rivona ne apne pita ki baat sunne ke baad tumhari taraf ek aakhri nazar daali.

Rivona: "Kal milte hain, Love."

Woh mudkar hall se bahar chali gayi.

Uske kadam mazboot the, lekin uske zehan mein ab bhi sawaal ghoom rahe the. Tum use samajh nahi aa rahe the—aur shayad isi wajah se woh tumhe nazarandaaz nahi kar paa rahi thi.

Tumhare liye, haalaanki, kal ki sunwai kisi royal game ka hissa nahi thi.

Woh tumhare ghar laut paane aur apni aam zindagi bacha paane ki pehli koshish hone wali thi.
