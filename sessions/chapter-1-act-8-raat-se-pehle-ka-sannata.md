# Does the Dragon Princess Really Hate Me?
## Chapter 1 — Act 8: Raat Se Pehle Ka Sannata

Imperial Palace of Aurelthys — Guest Wing, shaam dhalne ke kareeb

Tum King Aldric ke saamne aakhri baar jhukte ho.

Love: "Aapko bahut-bahut shukriya, mere Raja. Main kal subah taiyaar rahunga."

King Aldric tumhari taraf dekhte hain. Unke chehre par ab bhi Emperor wali gambhirta hai, lekin unki awaaz mein halki narmi aa jaati hai.

King Aldric: "Aaram karo, Love. Kal ka din tumhare liye aasaan nahi hoga. Lekin yaad rakhna—High Court mein tumhe apni baat kehne ka poora haq hai."

Woh ek pal rukte hain, phir royal guard ko ishara karte hain.

King Aldric: "Garrick, ise guest quarters tak le jao. Iske liye saaf kapde aur ek garam dinner ka intezaam karna. Aur haan, ise ek qaidi ki tarah treat mat karna."

Garrick: "Ji, Your Majesty."

Tum Garrick ke saath hall se bahar nikal jaate ho.

Tumhare peeche bade royal doors dheere-dheere band ho jaate hain.

### Royal Guest Wing

Palace ke corridors tumhare gaon ke kisi bhi ghar se bilkul alag the.

Unchi chhaton se latakte crystal lamps, deewaron par purane dragon emperors ki paintings, aur marble ke farsh par tumhare kadmon ki halki goonj—har cheez tumhe yaad dila rahi thi ki tum ek aisi duniya mein aa gaye ho jiska hissa banne ki tumne kabhi khwahish nahi ki thi.

Garrick tumhare saath bina jaldi kiye chal raha tha.

Uske armour par Imperial Dragon ka insignia chamak raha tha. Woh ek experienced guard tha, lekin tumse baat karte waqt uska lehja kaafi normal tha.

Garrick: "Pehli baar palace aaye ho?"

Woh tumhari taraf dekhta hai, phir halki si muskaan ke saath khud hi kehta hai—

Garrick: "Chhodo, yeh sawaal bhi kya poochha. Tumhari haalat dekhkar lagta hai ki tumne apne gaon se seedha yahan tak ka safar kiya hai."

Kuch der baad tum dono ek bade darwaze ke saamne rukte ho.

Darwaza khulta hai.

Andar ek saaf-suthra guest room tha. Ek bada bed, fireplace, ek writing desk, saaf kapde, aur khidki ke paas ek chhoti si table rakhi thi. Fireplace mein halki aag jal rahi thi, jiski garmi kamre ko aaramdayak bana rahi thi.

Garrick ne tumhe andar aane ka ishara kiya.

Garrick: "Yeh tumhara kamra hai. Bathroom udhar hai, aur agar kisi cheez ki zaroorat ho toh bell baja dena. Koi attendant aa jayega."

Woh darwaze ke paas rukta hai.

Garrick: "Aur Love... ek baat kehna chahta hoon."

Uska chehra ab pehle se zyada serious ho gaya.

Garrick: "Kal court mein bade-bade nobles honge. Kuch tumhe neecha dikhane ki koshish karenge, kuch tumhare jawaab mein galti nikaalenge. Unki baaton se ghabrana mat. Tumhe bas sach bolna hai."

Phir usne apna sir halka sa jhukaya.

Garrick: "Shubh raatri. Kal subah main tumhe court ke liye lene aaunga."

Woh darwaza band karke chala jaata hai.

Ab kamre mein sirf tum ho.

Bahar baarish lagbhag ruk chuki hai.

Kuch der baad ek attendant dinner lekar aata hai—garam soup, bread, sabzi aur ek glass doodh. Woh khana table par rakhkar tumhe aaram karne deta hai.

Lekin palace ke doosre hisse mein raat abhi lambi hone wali thi.

### Princess Rivona ke chambers

Rivona apni khidki ke paas khadi thi. Usne apna royal cloak utaar diya tha aur ab ek simple dark-blue evening dress pehne hue thi.

Uski dost Nymera darwaze par dastak dekar andar aayi.

Nymera: "Kyle ke baare mein abhi tak koi pakki khabar nahi mili. Uske ghar walon ka kehna hai ki woh kuch din pehle ek military matter ke liye nikla tha, lekin uske baad kisi ne use nahi dekha."

Rivona ki ungliyan khidki ki sill par thodi kas gayin.

Rivona: "Usne mujhe bataya kyun nahi?"

Nymera: "Mujhe nahi pata. Shayad use laga hoga ki woh jaldi laut aayega."

Rivona ne jawaab nahi diya.

Usne apni nazar palace ke guest wing ki taraf uthayi. Wahan ke kisi kamre mein ab woh human maujood tha jise Ancient Marriage Registry ne uska pati ghoshit kar diya tha.

Rivona: "Woh sach mein sirf apne khet mein lautna chahta hai?"

Nymera: "Ab tak toh usne yahi kaha hai."

Rivona: "Ya toh woh duniya ka sabse ajeeb aadmi hai... ya phir woh hum sabse kuch chhupa raha hai."

Nymera ne apne baazu baandh liye.

Nymera: "Dono possibilities hain. Kal court mein pata chal sakta hai ki woh kitna sach bol raha hai."

Rivona ne gehri saans li.

Rivona: "Kal mujhe jawaab chahiye. Mujhe yeh jaan-na hai ki Registry ne use kyun chuna, aur kya is bond ko khatam kiya ja sakta hai."

Woh apni desk ki taraf gayi, jahan Ancient Marriage Registry ke record ki ek copy rakhi thi.

Usne document khola.

Purane magical symbols ka ek hissa sunehri roshni mein chamak raha tha.

Rivona ne apni ungli us par rakhi.

Kuch nahi hua.

Phir usne ek dragon-language spell padha, jisse aam taur par Registry ke record ki authenticity verify ki jaati thi.

Ek pal ke liye symbols chamke.

Phir ek aisi line ubhar aayi jo pehle document par dikhayi nahi de rahi thi.

Rivona ki aankhen phail gayin.

Rivona: "...Yeh kya hai?"

Usne dobara spell cast kiya.

Line gayab ho gayi.

Usne phir koshish ki.

Kuch nahi hua.

Rivona ne document ko band kar diya. Uska dil tez dhadak raha tha, lekin woh samajh nahi paa rahi thi ki usne jo dekha woh Registry ka hidden text tha, ya kisi magical interference ka asar.

Usne turant apni personal attendant Liora ko bulaya.

Rivona: "Yeh document abhi kisi aur ko mat dena. Kal subah ise High Court ke original record ke saath compare kiya jayega."

Liora: "Ji, Your Highness."

Rivona ne document apne desk ke drawer mein rakh diya aur use lock kar diya.

Ab uske paas ek aur sawaal tha—aisa sawaal jiska jawaab shayad kal milne wala tha.
