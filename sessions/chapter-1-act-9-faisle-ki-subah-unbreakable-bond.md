# Does the Dragon Princess Really Hate Me?
## Chapter 1 — Act 9: Faisle Ki Subah Unbreakable Bond

Imperial Palace of Aurelthys — Guest Wing, raat ka aakhri pehar

Bahar baarish ab dheere-dheere tham rahi thi. Palace ki unchi khidkiyon par paani ki boondein phisal rahi thi, aur door kahin raat ke pehredaaron ke boots ki awaaz patthar ke corridor mein goonj rahi thi.

Guest room ke andar fireplace ki naram roshni thi. Table par rakha dinner ab bhi garam tha—vegetable soup, taazi bread, bhuni hui sabziyan aur ek glass doodh.

Tumhare liye yeh sab shayad royal palace mein milne wali ek mamooli suvidha thi, lekin is waqt tumhare saamne ek ajeeb sach tha: jis mahal mein tum bas khaana paane ki umeed se aaye the, usi mahal mein ab tumhein ek princess ke pati ke roop mein dekha ja raha tha.

Aur kal subah High Court tumhari zindagi ka faisla karne wala tha.

### Guest Room — Raat

Dinner ki khushboo fireplace ke dhuen ke saath kamre mein phaili hui thi. Bahar se kabhi-kabhi dragon guards ki dheemi awaazein sunayi deti thi.

Kuch der baad tumhari nazar us kursi par rakhe saaf kapdon par padi, jo palace ke attendants tumhare liye chhod gaye the. Kapde saadhaaran the, lekin achhi quality ke—ek saaf cream-colored shirt, gehre neele rang ka waistcoat, kaale trousers aur saaf leather shoes.

Yeh kisi noble ka shahi libaas nahi tha. Lekin High Court mein tumhein kam-se-kam aisa toh dikhna tha ki tum apni baat izzat se rakhne aaye ho.

Palace ke bahar duniya tumhein ek gareeb kisaan ke roop mein jaanti thi. Kal wahi duniya tumhare baare mein faisla sunne ke liye jama hone wali thi.

Raat aage badhti gayi.

Fireplace ki aag dheere-dheere chhoti hone lagi. Palace ke corridor mein bhi sannata gehra ho gaya. Kal ki sunwai ke liye zaroori dastavez seal kiye ja rahe the, guards ko naye orders mil rahe the, aur High Court ke adhikari Ancient Marriage Registry ke purane records taiyaar kar rahe the.

Tumhari taraf se ab raat mein koi nayi halchal nahi hui.

Aakhirkaar, guest wing bhi khamoshi mein doob gaya.

Aur phir—

Subah hone lagi.

## Chapter 1 — Act 9: Faisle Ki Subah

Agli subah, Imperial High Court of Aurelthys

Subah ki pehli sunahri kirnein palace ke unche towers par padi hi thi ki poore imperial district mein ghanton ki awaaz goonj uthi.

Aaj High Court ka ek aam din nahi tha.

Aaj ek aise vivaah ki sunwai hone wali thi jise duniya ke sabse purane magical legal systems mein se ek—Ancient Marriage Registry—ne khud maanya kiya tha.

Aur is vivaah mein ek taraf thi Dragon Empire ki Crown Princess.

Doosri taraf ek aisa kisaan, jiska naam kal tak imperial court ke adhiktar logon ne kabhi suna bhi nahi tha.

### 1. Royal Chambers — Princess Rivona

Rivona Vaeltharis apne dressing mirror ke saamne khadi thi.

Uske silver-white baal peeche ek shahi braid mein bandhe hue the. Uske golden horns par subah ki roshni chamak rahi thi, aur uski amber aankhon ke neeche neend ki kami ke halke nishaan the.

Usne raat bhar theek se aaram nahi kiya tha.

Uske saamne ek dark crimson royal gown rakha tha, jis par Dragon Empire ka golden insignia bana hua tha. Yeh woh libaas tha jo woh kisi political hearing ya royal declaration ke liye pehenti thi.

Liora Fernbrook ne dheere se poochha, “Your Highness, kya main aapka court attire taiyaar kar doon?”

Rivona ne mirror mein apni hi aankhon ko dekha.

“Kar do.”

Liora kuch pal ruki, phir boli, “High Court ke adhikari keh rahe hain ki agar Registry ki validity confirm ho gayi, toh—”

“Main jaanti hoon ki woh kya keh rahe hain.”

Rivona ki awaaz tez nahi thi. Lekin usmein itni thandak thi ki Liora ne turant apna sir jhuka liya.

“Maaf kijiye, Your Highness.”

Rivona ne apni ungliyon ko aapas mein kas liya.

“Main kisi anjaan insaan se shaadi karne ke liye taiyaar nahi hoon. Main apni poori zindagi apne desh, apne logon aur apni zimmedariyon ke liye taiyaar karti aayi hoon. Aur ab ek purana relic ek aisa faisla suna raha hai jismein meri marzi ka koi matlab hi nahi?”

Uski ungliyon ke aas-paas sunehri dragon magic ki halki chingaariyan chamak kar gaayab ho gayin.

Liora ne himmat karke kaha, “Shayad High Court koi raasta nikaal sake.”

Rivona ne uski taraf dekha.

“Raasta? Mujhe annulment chahiye, Liora. Koi naya raasta nahi.”

Lekin jaise hi Liora kapde taiyaar karne ke liye mudi, Rivona ki nazar desk par rakhe us sealed document ki taraf chali gayi.

Registry ki copy.

Wahi document jismein raat ko ek chhupi hui line kuch pal ke liye chamki thi.

Agar Registry mein kuch chhupaya gaya tha, toh kisne? Aur kyun?

Rivona ne document ko chhua tak nahi.

Usne bas kaha, “Original record High Court mein pahunch gaya?”

“Ji, Your Highness. Royal archivist use khud lekar gaya hai.”

“Achha.”

Rivona ne apni nazar hata li. Aaj woh kisi chhupi hui line ke peeche nahi bhaagne wali thi. Aaj woh us faisle ko rokne ki koshish karegi jo uski poori zindagi badal sakta tha.

Kam-se-kam, woh abhi bhi yahi maanti thi.

### 2. Imperial High Court — Darbar Khulne Se Pehle

High Court ek vishaal gumbad ke neeche bana tha. Uske pillars par purane Dragon Emperors ke naam aur Three Laws ke prateek ukere gaye the.

Court ke madhya mein ek gol dais tha. Uske peeche paanch uchch nyayadhishon ki seats thi, aur unke beech ek khaali pedestal rakha tha.

Us pedestal par rakha jaana tha Ancient Marriage Registry ka original relic-record.

Court mein nobles, legal scholars, royal representatives aur kuch chuninda observers ko jagah di gayi thi. Har taraf fusfusahat chal rahi thi.

“Ek kisaan aur Crown Princess?”

“Registry ka bond valid hua toh?”

“Kya Emperor khud is vivaah ko sweekar karenge?”

“Teen royal engagements barbaad ho sakti hain…”

Ek dragon noble ne naak sikodte hue kaha, “Mujhe toh lagta hai kisi ne Registry ke saath chhedchhaad ki hai.”

Uske paas baithi Lady Elowen Thorne ne shaant lekin teekhi awaaz mein jawab diya, “Agar aapke paas saboot hai, toh court ko dijiye. Sirf kisi ki jaati aur haisiyat dekhkar aarop lagana Three Laws ke khilaaf hai.”

Noble chup ho gaya, lekin uske chehre par nafrat ab bhi saaf thi.

Court ke ek side par Lady Seraphine Drakemore aur Lady Vaelora Frostwing maujood thi. Dono Rivona ke kareebi circle ka hissa thi, aur dono ko is baat ka darr tha ki ek anjaan insaan ka Crown Princess se vivaah Dragon Empire ki succession politics ko ulat-pulat kar sakta hai.

Nymera Ashveil ki nazar baar-baar main entrance ki taraf ja rahi thi.

Usne dheere se Elowen se kaha, “Rivona aaj bahut gusse mein hogi.”

Elowen ne jawab diya, “Uske paas gussa hone ki wajah hai. Lekin Love ke paas bhi apni baat kehne ka haq hai.”

“Tum sach mein sochti ho ki yeh sab ek accident hai?”

Elowen ne turant jawab nahi diya.

“Main yeh sochti hoon ki jab tak humein sach nahi pata, tab tak humein kisi ko doshi nahi banana chahiye.”

Tabhi court ke bade darwaaze khul gaye.

Ek herald ki awaaz poore gumbad mein goonj uthi.

“His Imperial Majesty, Emperor Aldric Vaeltharis! Her Imperial Highness, Crown Princess Rivona Vaeltharis!”

Sabhi log apni jagah khade ho gaye.

Aldric apne imperial robes mein andar aaye. Unke chehre par hamesha ki tarah shaanti thi, lekin unki aankhon mein ek aisi gambhirta thi jo sirf un logon mein hoti hai jinhone itihaas ko apni aankhon se badalte dekha ho.

Rivona unke saath chal rahi thi.

Uski nazar court mein kisi ko dhoondh nahi rahi thi.

Phir bhi, jab woh apni seat par baithi, uski aankhein ek pal ke liye us jagah gayin jahan doosri taraf ke paksh ko baithna tha.

Uska jabda kas gaya.

Woh aaj bhi yeh samajhne ki koshish kar rahi thi ki ek aisa insaan, jise woh jaanti tak nahi thi, uski zindagi mein itni badi jagah kaise le sakta tha.

### 3. Sunwai Shuru Hoti Hai

Chief Arbiter Maelor Veyne apni seat se uthe. Unke baal safed the aur unke chehre par kai dashakon ke kathin faislon ki lakeerein thi.

“Ancient Marriage Registry se sambandhit maamle mein High Court ki pehli sunwai ab shuru hoti hai.”

Unhone apne saamne rakhe document ko khola.

“Court ke saamne teen pramukh sawaal hain.”

Unhone ek ungli uthayi.

“Pehla: kya Registry ka record asli hai?”

Doosri ungli.

“Doosra: kya bond dono pakshon ke beech kanooni roop se maanya vivaah sthapit karta hai?”

Teesri ungli.

“Teesra: agar bond maanya hai, toh kya use kisi existing imperial law ke tahat radd kiya ja sakta hai?”

Rivona turant khadi ho gayi.

“Your Honour, main teesre sawaal par zor dena chahti hoon. Main is bond ko sweekar nahi karti. Maine kisi vivaah ke liye sahmati nahi di. Maine is vyakti ko pehle kabhi nahi dekha. Kya High Court meri marzi ko poori tarah nazarandaaz karega?”

Court mein ek halki halchal hui.

Chief Arbiter ne uski baat poori hone di.

“Your Imperial Highness, aapki aapatti official record mein darj ki jaayegi. Lekin Ancient Marriage Registry ek ordinary marriage contract nahi hai. Iske adhikaar aur seemaon ko pehle verify karna zaroori hai.”

Rivona ki aankhon mein gussa chamka.

“Toh pehle verify kijiye.”

Chief Arbiter ne apna sir jhukaya.

“Yahi kiya jaayega.”

Unhone royal archivist ko ishara kiya.

Do court attendants aage aaye aur ek bhaari, purane zamane ka case pedestal par rakh diya. Case khola gaya toh uske andar ek kaale-sunehre frame mein surakshit pracheen record nazar aaya.

Us par bane symbols kisi bhi modern dragon script se alag the.

Aldric ki nazar record par padte hi ek pal ke liye sthir ho gayi.

Woh is relic ko pehchante the.

Ya kam-se-kam, unhone apni zindagi mein iske baare mein itna zaroor padha tha ki jaante the—yeh koi aisa relic nahi tha jise aasani se badla ya nakli banaya ja sake.

Lekin unke chehre par jo baat ubhri, woh sirf hairani nahi thi.

Woh pehchaan aur chinta ka ek ajeeb sa mel tha.

Chief Arbiter ne verification spell shuru karne ka aadesh diya.

Paanch nyayadhishon ne ek saath apne haath record ki taraf uthaye. Neeli, safed aur sunehri magical roshni pedestal ke chaaron taraf ghoomne lagi.

Pehle symbols jagmagaye.

Phir ek-ek karke court ke upar tairte hue ancient runes banne lage.

Authenticity: Confirmed.

Court mein sannata chha gaya.

Chief Arbiter ne agla spell shuru karwaya.

Record alteration: No evidence detected.

Ab log ek-doosre ki taraf dekhne lage.

Rivona ki ungliyan uski kursi ke armrest par kas gayin.

“Yeh abhi bhi prove nahi karta ki main is vivaah ko sweekar karti hoon,” usne thande swar mein kaha.

Chief Arbiter ne jawaab diya, “Sahmati ka prashn darj hai, Your Highness. Court us par bhi vichaar karega.”

Phir unhone teesra spell shuru karwaya.

Is baar pedestal se nikli roshni seedhe court ki chhat tak gayi. Gumbad par bane pracheen symbols ek saath jagmagane lage.

Kuch pal tak kuch nahi hua.

Phir record ke beech ek gehri sunehri rekha ubhri.

Aur uske neeche ancient language mein ek vakya likha hua nazar aaya.

Court ke scholars ek saath aage jhuk gaye.

Chief Arbiter ne us text ko padhne ke liye apna translation crystal uthaya.

Crystal ki roshni laal se sunehri hui.

Unka chehra badal gaya.

“Yeh… ek binding clause hai.”

Rivona turant khadi ho gayi.

“Kaisa binding clause?”

Chief Arbiter ne record se nazar nahi hatayi.

“Yeh clause Registry ke us pracheen niyam ka hissa hai jo sirf tab saamne aata hai jab bond ko radd karne ki maang ki jaati hai.”

Unhone agla hissa padha.

Aur phir unki awaaz poore court mein goonj uthi.

“Jab Ancient Marriage Registry kisi bond ko poori tarah seal kar deti hai, toh kisi ek paksh ki ichchha, raajneetik pad, vansh, ya shakti ke aadhar par us bond ko radd nahi kiya ja sakta.”

Kisi ne saans tak nahi li.

Chief Arbiter ne agla vakya padhne se pehle ek pal ka viraam liya.

“Bond mein darj dono pakshon ko kanoon ke tahat pati aur patni ke roop mein maanya kiya jaayega. Is bond ko koi Emperor, koi High Court aur koi magical authority apne aap samapt nahi kar sakti.”

Rivona ka chehra bilkul sthir ho gaya.

Jaise usne suna toh tha, lekin uska dimaag uske arth ko sweekar karne se inkaar kar raha ho.

“Dobara padhiye,” usne kaha.

Chief Arbiter ne uski taraf dekha.

“Your Highness—”

“Maine kaha, dobara padhiye.”

Is baar uski awaaz mein sirf gussa nahi tha. Usmein ek aisa darr bhi tha jise woh kisi ko dikhana nahi chahti thi.

Chief Arbiter ne wahi clause dobara padha.

Har shabd pehle se zyada bhaari lag raha tha.

King Aldric dheere se apni seat se uthe.

“Chief Arbiter,” unhone gambhir awaaz mein poochha, “kya koi exception hai? Koi aisa clause jo fraud, coercion, ya Registry ke misuse ki sthiti mein bond ko samapt kar sake?”

Chief Arbiter ne record par ek aur spell chalaya.

Roshni kuch pal tak ghoomti rahi.

Phir symbols shaant ho gaye.

“Record mein koi aisa exception darj nahi hai, Your Majesty.”

Aldric ki aankhon mein ek gehri chinta utar aayi.

“Matlab…”

Chief Arbiter ne baat poori ki.

“Agar bond ki authenticity aur activation ko final verification mein bhi isi tarah confirm kar diya gaya, toh High Court ise annul nahi kar sakta. Dono paksh kanooni roop se pati-patni bane rahenge.”

Rivona ki kursi peeche khisak gayi.

Usne ek kadam aage badhaya.

“Nahi.”

Uski awaaz pehle dheemi thi.

“Yeh meri zindagi hai. Mera desh hai. Mere faisle hain. Aap mujhse keh rahe hain ki ek pracheen machine—ek relic—meri poori zindagi ka faisla kar sakta hai?”

“Yeh machine nahi hai, Your Highness,” ek buzurg legal scholar ne kaha. “Yeh Three Laws se bhi purana ek magical covenant hai.”

“Main iski parwah nahi karti!”

Uske horns ke aas-paas golden magic ki lehr chamki. Court ke chand lamps ek pal ke liye kaanp uthe.

“Main ise sweekar nahi karti. Main is aadmi ko nahi jaanti. Main isse pyaar nahi karti. Aur main isse shaadi karne ke liye kabhi raazi nahi hui!”

Kisi ne jawaab nahi diya.

Kyunki is baar Rivona ka virodh sirf ek princess ka political protest nahi tha.

Yeh ek aisi aurat ki awaaz thi jise apni hi zindagi par control khone ka ehsaas ho raha tha.

King Aldric ne dheere se kaha, “Rivona…”

“Father, please.” Usne unki taraf dekha. “Mujhe mat kahiye ki main ise sweekar kar loon. Mujhe mat kahiye ki main iske saath apni zindagi bita doon. Main aisa nahi kar sakti.”

Aldric ke paas uske liye koi aasaan jawaab nahi tha.

Woh apni beti se pyaar karte the. Woh uski marzi ki ahmiyat samajhte the. Lekin woh yeh bhi jaante the ki ek baar pracheen Registry ka bond final confirm ho gaya, toh use todne ki koshish poore Empire ke kanooni dhaanche ko hila sakti thi.

Chief Arbiter ne apni awaaz sambhali.

“Court abhi antim declaration se pehle dono pakshon ke statements sunega. Bond ko radd karne ka adhikaar hamare paas nahi hai, lekin hum iske circumstances ki jaanch kar sakte hain. Agar kisi ne Registry ke activation ko manipulate kiya hai, toh zimmedaar vyakti ko kanoon ke saamne laaya jaayega.”

Rivona ne apni aankhein band ki.

Ek pal ke liye uska gussa jaise uske andar hi simat gaya.

Phir usne dheere se aankhein kholi aur apni seat par wapas baith gayi.

Uski nazar ab record par nahi thi.

Woh court ke doosre darwaaze ki taraf dekh rahi thi.

Woh jaanti thi ki agla statement kis se liya jaayega.

Aur woh abhi bhi yeh nahi jaanti thi ki jis aadmi ko woh kal tak apni zindagi ki sabse badi galti samajh rahi thi, woh khud bhi is jaal mein phansa hua tha ya nahi.

### 4. Ek Adhoora Faisla

Chief Arbiter ne apne saamne rakha document band kiya.

“High Court yeh darj karta hai ki Ancient Marriage Registry ka record pratham verification mein asli aur aparivartit paaya gaya hai. Record mein maujood binding clause ke anusaar, bond ko ek-tarfa aadesh se annul nahi kiya ja sakta.”

Unhone court ki taraf dekha.

“Lekin final verification aur circumstances ki jaanch abhi baaki hai.”

Court mein ek nayi fusfusahat phail gayi.

Kuch nobles ke chehron par santushti thi. Kuch ke chehron par darr. Kuch log pehle se hi sochne lage the ki agar Crown Princess ka vivaah ek aam kisaan se kanooni roop se juda rehna hai, toh Empire ki politics mein kya badlega.

Rivona ne inmein se kisi ko nahi dekha.

Uski ungliyan ab bhi armrest ko kas kar pakde hue thi.

Usne dheere se, lagbhag sirf khud se kaha—

“Yeh sach nahi ho sakta…”

King Aldric ne use suna, lekin kuch nahi kaha.

Kyunki abhi court mein ek aur awaaz uthni baaki thi.

Tumhari.
