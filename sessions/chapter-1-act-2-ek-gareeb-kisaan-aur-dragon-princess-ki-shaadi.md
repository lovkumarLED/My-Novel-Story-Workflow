# Does the Dragon Princess Really Hate Me?
## Chapter 1 — Act 2: Ek Gareeb Kisaan Aur Dragon Princess Ki Shaadi

Baarish abhi bhi ho rahi hai.
Imperial Palace ke courtyard mein har taraf sannata chhaya hua hai. Dragon knights, royal servants, noble families aur teeno royal suitors tumhari taraf dekh rahe hain.
Aur phir...
Tum achanak King Aldric ke saamne ghutnon par gir jaate ho.
Love: "M-Maalik! Mujhe maaf kar dijiye!"
Tumhari awaaz kaanp rahi hai. Tum apne dono haath jod lete ho, aur tumhari aankhon se aansu nikalne lagte hain.
Love: "Main toh west mein ek chhote se gaon ke paas jungle mein kheti karta hoon, Maalik! Main bas ek chhota sa kisaan hoon!"
Tum baarish mein bheege apne purane kapdon ki taraf dekhte ho.
Love: "Lagataar baarish hoti rahi, meri saari fasal kharab ho gayi! Main bahut majboor ho gaya tha, isliye kaam aur khane ki talaash mein sheher aaya. Pandrah din se safar kar raha hoon, Maalik!"
Tumhari awaaz aur bharra jaati hai.
Love: "Raaste mein khana bhi khatam ho gaya. Phir kuch lutero ne mujhe loot liya! Mere paas jo thode-bahut paise the, woh bhi le gaye!"
Tum apna chehra haathon se chhupa lete ho.
Love: "Main kisi tarah sheher pahuncha. Itna thak gaya tha ki wahi so gaya. Jab utha, toh pata chala mahal mein koi function hai. Mujhe laga shayad gareebon ko khana baanta ja raha hoga, toh main yahan aa gaya!"
Tum King Aldric ke saamne aur jhuk jaate ho.
Love: "Mujhe bahut bhookh lagi hai, Maalik... pyaas bhi lagi hai. Mujhe maaf kar dijiye! Mujhe nahi pata yeh shaadi kya hai, yeh registry kya hai! Mujhe bas apne khet mein wapas jaana hai. Apne ghar jaana hai!"
Tumhari baat khatam hote hi courtyard mein kuch pal ke liye bilkul khamoshi chha jaati hai.
Kisi ko samajh nahi aa raha ki kya kahe.
King Aldric ka reaction
King Aldric tumhe kuch der tak dekhta rehta hai.
Uske chehre par ab gussa nahi hai. Uski jagah ek gehri soch nazar aa rahi hai.
King Aldric: "Tum keh rahe ho ki tumhe is marriage registry ke baare mein kuch bhi nahi pata?"
Woh tumhare kareeb aata hai.
King Aldric: "Aur tum sach mein sirf ek kisaan ho, jo apne ghar lautna chahta hai?"
Uski awaaz mein ab bhi royal authority hai, lekin pehle jaisi sakhti nahi.
Woh ek guard ki taraf mudta hai.
King Aldric: "Is aadmi ke liye khana aur paani lekar aao. Aur royal healer ko bulao. Iski haalat dekho."
Imperial Guard: "Ji, Your Majesty!"
Guard turant chala jaata hai.
Lekin Rivona ka reaction bilkul alag hai.
Princess Rivona — Gusse ke peeche ka shak

Rivona tumhe dekhti hai.
Pehle woh hairaan thi.
Ab uske chehre par irritation aur disbelief dono hain.
Rivona: "Ek kisaan?"
Woh tumhare purane kapdon ko dekhti hai, phir tumhare ghutnon par pade hone ko.
Rivona: "Tum keh rahe ho ki tum pandrah din tak safar karte rahe, lutero ne tumhe loot liya, aur phir tumhe achanak meri shaadi ka pati bana diya?"
Woh ek kadam aage badhti hai.
Rivona: "Tumhe khud apni baat sunai de rahi hai?"
Seraphine apni hansi rokne ki koshish karti hai.
Seraphine: "Your Highness, agar yeh sach bol raha hai, toh shayad imperial registry ko bhi gareebi par daya aa gayi."
Elowen: "Seraphine, bas karo. Tum dekh nahi rahi ho ki woh kitna dara hua hai?"
Seraphine: "Ya phir kitni achhi acting kar raha hai."
Nymera apni baahen fold kar leti hai. Woh tumhe dhyaan se observe kar rahi hai.
Nymera: "Mujhe nahi pata woh sach bol raha hai ya nahi. Lekin agar koi itna bada fraud karna chahta, toh kya woh sabke saamne rokar apni jaan bachane ki koshish karta?"
Vaelora: "Kabhi-kabhi sabse achha jhooth wahi hota hai jo sabse zyada bechara dikhai de."
Rivona apni friends ki baatein sunti hai, lekin kuch nahi kehti.
Uski nazar tum par hi hai.
Rivona: "Meri taraf dekho."
Uski awaaz thandi hai.
Rivona: "Tum keh rahe ho ki tumhe mera naam tak nahi pata tha?"
Woh tumhare jawab ka intezaar karti hai.
Phir uski aankhon mein ek aur emotion ubhar kar aata hai—gussa nahi, balki apni zindagi par control kho dene ka dar.
Rivona: "Maine apni poori zindagi apne kingdom ki zimmedariyon ke liye taiyaar ki hai. Mujhe political alliances samajhne padte hain, apne logon ki protection karni padti hai, aur apne har decision ka result face karna padta hai."
Woh apni ungli tumhari taraf karti hai.
Rivona: "Aur ab mujhe bataya ja raha hai ki meri shaadi ek aise insaan se ho gayi hai jo apne ghar lautne ke liye bhi pareshan hai!"
Uski awaaz aakhri shabdon par aur tez ho jaati hai.
Rivona: "Tum samajhte ho yeh mere liye koi mazaak hai?"
King Aldric beech mein bolta hai.
King Aldric: "Rivona, woh dara hua hai. Use saans lene do."
Rivona: "Father, main usse maar nahi rahi hoon! Main sirf sawaal pooch rahi hoon!"
Woh gusse mein apna chehra doosri taraf kar leti hai.
Lekin kuch seconds baad, woh phir tumhari taraf dekhti hai.
Tum abhi bhi zameen par ghutnon ke bal ho.
Tumhare kapdon se paani tapak raha hai.
Tumhare chehre par thakaan saaf nazar aa rahi hai.
Rivona ke expression mein ek bahut chhota sa badlaav aata hai.
Uska gussa khatam nahi hua.
Lekin ab uske mann mein ek naya sawaal bhi hai.
Agar yeh aadmi sach mein sirf ek gareeb kisaan hai... toh Ancient Marriage Registry ne ise hi kyun chuna?
Teeno suitors ki pratikriya
Prince Vekar tumhe nafrat bhari nazar se dekhta hai.
Vekar: "Agar yeh sach mein ek commoner hai, toh isse royal marriage ka koi haq nahi hona chahiye."
John Ashford apni chin par haath rakhta hai.
John: "Haq ka faisla hum nahi kar sakte. Registry ka legal status investigate karna hoga. Lekin is marriage ke diplomatic consequences bahut serious hain."
Prince Charles VII tumhare paas aane ke bajaye Rivona ki taraf dekhta hai.
Charles: "Rivona, agar tumhari marzi ke bina yeh hua hai, toh main is matter ko High Court mein challenge karne mein tumhari madad karunga."
Rivona uski taraf dekhti hai.
Rivona: "Mujhe tumhari madad ki zaroorat nahi hai, Charles."
Charles kuch nahi bolta, lekin uske chehre par disappointment nazar aati hai.
Woh tumhe ek baar phir dekhta hai.
Charles: "Phir bhi, main is matter ko aise khatam nahi hone dunga."
Uski awaaz mein ek warning chhupi hui hai.
Ek chhota sa raaz
Isi beech, King Aldric ka royal steward Theron Silverbough palace ke andar se ek purana register lekar aata hai.
Theron: "Your Majesty, registry ke initial records mil gaye hain."
King Aldric register kholta hai.
Uske chehre ka rang dheere-dheere badalne lagta hai.
King Aldric: "Yeh... mumkin nahi hai."
Rivona turant mudti hai.
Rivona: "Ab kya hua, Father?"
King Aldric register band kar deta hai.
King Aldric: "Kuch nahi. Iski jaanch baad mein hogi."
Vaelora ki aankhein sikud jaati hain.
Woh samajh gayi hai ki king kuch chhupa raha hai.
Aur pehli baar, uska shak sirf tum par nahi, balki khud King Aldric par bhi jaata hai.
Rivona yeh sab notice karti hai.
Lekin woh abhi tumhare saamne khadi hai, tumhare jawab ka intezaar karte hue.
Ek servant khana aur paani lekar aata hai aur King Aldric ke ishare par tumhare paas rakh deta hai.
King Aldric: "Pehle kuch kha lo, Love. Tumhe abhi kahin nahi bheja ja raha."
Rivona turant bol padti hai.
Rivona: "Father! Aap ise palace mein rakhne wale hain?"
King Aldric: "Jab tak hum yeh nahi jaan lete ki registry ne kya kiya hai, yeh hamari zimmedari hai."
Rivona aankhein band karke gehri saans leti hai.
Phir woh tumhari taraf jhukti hai aur dheere se kehti hai:
Rivona: "Ek baat yaad rakhna, Love. Tum chahe kitne bhi bechare kyun na lago, main tum par bharosa nahi karungi."
Woh seedhi khadi ho jaati hai.
Rivona: "Aur agar mujhe pata chala ki tumne meri zindagi barbaad karne ke liye koi chaal chali hai... toh tumhe apne har decision ka jawab dena padega."
Woh apni friends ke saath palace ke andar jaane ke liye mudti hai.
Lekin kuch kadam chalne ke baad, woh ek pal ke liye rukti hai.
Bina peeche dekhe kehti hai:
Rivona: "Elyndra, ise guest quarters mein rakhna. Aur iski har movement par nazar rakhna."
Elyndra: "As you command, Your Highness."
Rivona chali jaati hai.
Tumhare saamne ab khana hai, paani hai, aur ek aisa palace hai jahan tumhara koi hona nahi chahiye tha.
Lekin tum ab is kahani ka hissa ban chuke ho.
Aur tumhare baare mein ek baat abhi bhi kisi ko nahi pata.
Tum asal mein kya chhupa rahe ho?
