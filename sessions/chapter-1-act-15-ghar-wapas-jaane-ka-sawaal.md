# Does the Dragon Princess Really Hate Me?
## Chapter 1 — Act 15: Ghar Wapas Jaane Ka Sawaal

Location: Royal Healer Wing

Royal Healer Wing mein kuch pal tak sirf healing crystals ki halki gunj sunai deti rahi.

Love ne Rivona ki taraf dekha. Uske honth khule, magar pehle koi jawaab nahi nikla. Uske gale mein ab bhi kharash thi, aur kamzori se uski ungliyan chadar par dheere se simat gayin.

Love: "Mujhe nahi pata ki kya hua tha. Main bas gusse aur bechaini mein ghabra gaya tha. Main ghar jaana chahta hoon."

Usne ek pal ke liye nazar jhuka li.

Love: "Maine socha, shayad main kuch karoon toh... ho sakta hai kuch badal jaaye. Bas itna hi. Lekin ab lagta hai shayad main kabhi ghar nahi ja paaunga."

Kamre mein kisi ne turant jawaab nahi diya.

Love ne phir Thalorien ki taraf dekha. Uski awaaz abhi bhi kamzor thi, lekin sawaal mein pehle se zyada zor tha.

Love: "Scholar... yeh jo dhaga, jo bond humare beech hai—kya yeh khatam nahi ho sakta? Mujhe yeh jaanana bahut zaroori hai. Tabhi main bata paaunga ki mujhe aage kya karna hai."

Ilyra ne Love ke paas rakhe paani ke glass ko uski pahunch ke kareeb kiya.

Ilyra: "Aapko itna bolne ke baad phir se aaram karna chahiye. Lekin is sawaal ka jawaab diye bina main aapko chup bhi nahi karaungi."

Thalorien ne apni ungliyan aapas mein jod leen. Usne Love ko aise nahi dekha jaise koi paheli suljha raha ho—balki jaise uske sawaal ki ahmiyat samajh raha ho.

Thalorien: "Main tumse jhoothi tasalli nahi dunga. Abhi mujhe nahi pata ki yeh bond khatam kiya ja sakta hai ya nahi. Lekin ab tak ki jaanch se yeh bhi saabit nahi hua ki ise khatam karna namumkin hai."

Love ki ungliyan chadar par aur kas gayin.

Thalorien: "Registry ko todne ki koshish karna aur kanoon ke andar us binding ko challenge karna ek hi baat nahi hai. Iska faisla High Court ko karna hoga. Humein pehle samajhna padega ki yeh bond Three Laws ke tahat kis tarah bana, aur Registry ne tumhari pratikriya par aisa jawaab kyun diya."

King Aldric ki awaaz shaant rahi, lekin uske shabdon mein darbar ka bhaar tha.

Aldric: "Crown apne aap is binding ko mita nahi sakta. Lekin main tum dono ko bina jaanch ke kisi ritual mein nahi bhejunga. Agar isse kanooni taur par challenge kiya ja sakta hai, toh us raaste ko High Court ke saamne rakha jaayega."

Rivona ab tak chup thi. Uski baahen ab bhi seene par bandhi thin, magar uski nazar Love se nahi hati.

Rivona: "Main tumhe yeh nahi kahungi ki sab theek ho jaayega. Mujhe iska jawaab nahi pata."

Uski awaaz mein narmi nahi thi—sirf woh kadvi sachchai thi jise woh khud bhi nazarandaaz nahi kar sakti thi.

Rivona: "Lekin main bhi is bond ko nahi chahti. Agar ise kanoon ke andar khatam karne ka raasta hai, toh main us raaste par chalungi. Iska matlab yeh nahi ki main tum par bharosa karti hoon."

Love ko uske chehre par koi maafi nahi dikhi. Na hi koi vaada ki sab kuch aasaan hoga. Phir bhi, pehli baar dono ki manzil ek hi lag rahi thi—chahe us manzil tak pahunchne ke liye unke paas abhi koi raasta na ho.

Garrick darwaaze ke paas seedha khada raha. Uski nazar ek pal ko Love par tikki, phir usne chupchaap King ko dekha. Ilyra ne paani ka glass aur kareeb kar diya, jaise kamre mein baaki sab kuch baad mein dekha ja sakta ho.

Thalorien: "Main Registry ko chhede bina uski likhawat aur kanooni record ki jaanch shuru kar sakta hoon. Lekin mujhe tumhari marzi chahiye—khaaskar tumhari aankhon mein dikhe us rang aur Registry ki pratikriya ke baare mein sawaal poochhne ke liye."

Aldric: "Tumhe abhi faisla karne ki zaroorat nahi. Pehle aaram karo. Lekin jab tum taiyaar ho, High Court ka raasta aur Registry ki jaanch—dono par baat hogi."

Rivona ne apni nazar ek pal ke liye hataayi, phir dobara Love ki taraf dekha.

Rivona: "Tumhe abhi ghar ka jawaab nahi mila, Love. Aur bond ke baare mein bhi nahi. Lekin agar tum sach mein iska raasta dhoondhna chahte ho, toh humein pehle yeh tay karna hoga ki hum kis tarah jaanch shuru karenge."

## Haalat ka chhota sa recap

Love ne saaf kaha ki woh ghabrahat aur bechaini mein Registry ke khilaaf kuch kar baitha, kyunki woh ghar lautna chahta tha. Thalorien nahi jaanta ki bond khatam ho sakta hai ya nahi, lekin High Court mein ise challenge karne ka raasta ho sakta hai. Rivona bhi bond khatam karna chahti hai, magar Love par bharosa nahi karti. Ilyra ne Love ko pehle aaram karne ko kaha hai.
