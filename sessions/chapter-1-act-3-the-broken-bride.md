# Does the Dragon Princess Really Hate Me?
## Chapter 1 — Act 3: The Broken Bride

Imperial Palace of Aurelthys — Royal Reception Hall

Bahar baarish abhi bhi rukne ka naam nahi le rahi thi.

Palace ki unchi khidkiyon par paani ki boondein phisal rahi thi. Door aasman mein bijli chamakti, aur uski roshni kuch pal ke liye hall ke sunahre pillars, dragon-shaped chandeliers aur laal-kaale royal banners ko roshan kar deti.

Hall ke beech mein ek chhoti si table par garam soup, fresh bread, roasted meat aur paani ka ek jug rakha tha.

Tumhari baatein sunte hue King Aldric khamoshi se khade the.

Jab tumne khane aur paani ke liye unka shukriya ada kiya, unki aankhon ki sakhti kuch pal ke liye kam hui. Unhone tumhe khana khane diya. Phir tumne unse kaha ki tumhe nahi pata yeh sab kaise ho raha hai, lekin tumne kuch galat nahi kiya.

King ne apne haath peeth ke peeche baandh liye.

King Aldric: "Tum chahte ho ki main tum par bharosa karun, lekin mere paas abhi tumhari baat par yakeen karne ka koi thos saboot nahi hai."

Unki awaaz kathor thi, magar usmein ek ajeeb si shaanti bhi thi.

King Aldric: "Lekin iska matlab yeh nahi ki main tumhe bina sune mujrim ghoshit kar doon. Hamare Empire ke laws har insaan par lagu hote hain—chahe woh ek gareeb kisaan ho ya dragon royal family ka member."

Woh ek pal ruke.

King Aldric: "Filhaal, tum khana khao. Tumhari aankhon ke neeche ke kaale ghere aur tumhari haalat dekhkar lagta hai ki tumhe kai din se theek se aaram nahi mila."

Phir tumne unhe saat sau saal purani kahani sunani shuru ki.

Legendary Hero.

Dragon Wars.

Hero ki sena mein King ka junior officer hona.

Aur tumhari maa, jo tumhe bachpan se in kahaniyon ke zariye bada karti aayi thi.

Jaise hi tumne Legendary Hero ka zikr kiya, King Aldric ke chehre ka expression badal gaya.

Unki amber-golden aankhon mein ek pal ke liye aisi yaad chamki jo kisi purane zakhm ki tarah ab bhi zinda thi.

Unhone apna sir thoda jhukaya.

King Aldric: "Tumhari maa ne tumhe ye kahaniyan sunayi thi?"

Unhone yeh sawaal dheere se poochha, jaise is chhoti si baat mein bhi koi aisi cheez ho jis par gaur karna zaroori tha.

King Aldric: "Toh tum ab bhi un kahaniyon ko yaad rakhte ho. Aajkal ke bahut se log unhe sirf bachchon ko sulaane ke liye sunayi jaane wali kahaniyan samajhte hain."

Woh khidki ki taraf mude. Baarish ke paar Aurelthys ka shahi shahar dikh raha tha—woh shahar jiske logon ko shayad kabhi andaaza bhi nahi hua tha ki unki azaadi ki keemat kitne logon ne chukayi thi.

King Aldric: "Lekin tumhari kahani mein ek chhoti si galti hai."

Woh dobara tumhari taraf mude.

King Aldric: "Legendary Hero mera pita nahi tha. Woh ek insaan tha. Aur main uski sena mein ek yuva dragon officer tha."

Unke hothon par ek bahut halki, lagbhag adrishya muskaan aayi.

King Aldric: "Tum mujhe junior kehte ho? Hah. Agar tum us waqt mujhe dekhte, toh shayad tumhe yakeen nahi hota ki ek din wohi yuva dragon is Empire ka Emperor banega."

Unhone apne daahine haath ki ungliyon ko dekha. Unke naakhunon ke paas sunehri scales ki ek patli parat chamak rahi thi.

King Aldric: "Saat sau teis saal pehle, main tumhari tarah kamzor aur bhooka nahi tha. Lekin main bhi jawaan tha. Mujhe bhi lagta tha ki taakat hi har sawaal ka jawaab hai."

Unki muskaan dheere-dheere gayab ho gayi.

King Aldric: "Hero ne mujhe sikhaya tha ki taakat ka asli matlab kisi kamzor ko ghutnon par laana nahi, balki us waqt uske saamne khada hona hai jab duniya use kuchal dena chahti ho."

Hall mein khamoshi chha gayi.

Door khade royal guards ne ek doosre ki taraf dekha. Unmein se kuch ne apni zindagi mein pehli baar Emperor ko Legendary Hero ke baare mein itni khulkar baat karte suna tha.

King ne phir tumhari taraf dekha.

King Aldric: "Tumne kaha ki tumhari maa tumhe ye kahaniyan sunati thi. Kya unhone tumhe sirf Hero ki bahaduri ke baare mein bataya tha? Ya unhone tumhe uske banaye hue Three Laws ke baare mein bhi bataya tha?"

Unka lehja ab pehle se zyada gambhir tha.

King Aldric: "Kyunki agar tum un laws ko jaante ho, toh tumhe samajhna chahiye ki main tumhe sirf isliye saza nahi de sakta kyunki tum ek insaan ho, gareeb ho, ya meri beti ke liye chune gaye rishton mein fit nahi baithte."

Unhone ek gehri saans li.

King Aldric: "Lekin Ancient Marriage Registry ka maamla alag hai. Woh relic mere Empire se bhi purana hai. Aur agar usne tum dono ke beech ek vaidh marriage bond darj kiya hai, toh uske peeche koi wajah zaroor hogi."

Unki aankhen tum par tik gayin.

King Aldric: "Mujhe abhi nahi pata woh wajah kya hai."

Ek pal ki khamoshi ke baad unhone joda—

King Aldric: "Aur sach kahun, toh mujhe yeh bhi nahi pata ki mujhe uska jawaab pasand aayega ya nahi."

Palace ke doosre hisse mein…
Rivona apne private chambers ki taraf ja rahi thi. Uske silver-white baal uske kandhon par bikhre hue the, aur uske sunehre horns chandelier ki roshni mein chamak rahe the.

Uske saath Seraphine, Vaelora, Elowen aur Nymera bhi thi.

Seraphine: "Main ab bhi kehti hoon ki humein us aadmi ko palace mein rakhna hi nahi chahiye. Woh ek anjaan insaan hai. Aur ab tumhare saath uska naam ek ancient marriage bond mein jod diya gaya hai."

Vaelora: "Use bahar nikaalne se pehle humein Registry samajhni hogi. Agar humne bina saboot ke use hata diya, toh woh political crisis aur bada ho sakta hai."

Nymera: "Aur agar woh sach mein ek gareeb kisaan hai, toh? Kya hum sirf isliye usse nafrat karte rahenge kyunki woh hamari expectations ke mutabik nahi hai?"

Seraphine ne use teekhi nazar se dekha.

Seraphine: "Tum uski tarafdaari kar rahi ho?"

Nymera: "Main kisi ki tarafdaari nahi kar rahi. Main bas keh rahi hoon ki humein sach jaan lena chahiye."

Rivona ab tak khamosh thi.

Phir woh ruk gayi.

Rivona: "Mujhe uski gareebi se koi farq nahi padta."

Uski awaaz thandi thi.

Rivona: "Mujhe farq is baat se padta hai ki meri poori zindagi, meri zimmedariyan, mere political alliances—sab kuch ek aise faisle se badal sakta hai jismein meri marzi ka koi matlab hi nahi raha."

Elowen ne dheere se kaha—

Elowen: "Toh phir humein yeh pata lagana chahiye ki Registry ne use chuna kyun."

Rivona ki amber aankhon mein ek pal ke liye uljhan ubhri.

Rivona: "Haan. Aur jab mujhe jawaab mil jayega, toh main khud faisla karungi ki us aadmi ka meri zindagi mein koi haq hai ya nahi."

Woh aage badh gayi.

Uske liye Love abhi bhi ek unwanted stranger tha.

Na uske liye koi affection tha, na trust.

Sirf gussa, sawaal aur ek aisi mystery jise woh har haal mein suljhana chahti thi.
