# Does the Dragon Princess Really Hate Me?
## Chapter 1 — Act 14: Hosh Toh Aa Gaya Musibat Abhi Baaki Hai

Royal Healer Wing mein subah ki halki roshni safed pardon se chhankar aa rahi thi. Kamre mein jadi-bootiyon ki khushboo thi, aur healing crystals ki neeli roshni deewaron par dheere-dheere hil rahi thi.

Love ki palkein aahista-aahista khulin.

Uska sir abhi bhi bhaari tha. Gale mein halki kharash thi, aur awaaz mein wohi dabi hui kamzori thi jo achanak gehri neend ya behoshi se uthne par hoti hai.

Love: "K... kahan hoon main?"

Uski nazar pehle chhat par gayi, phir khidki par, aur aakhir mein bed ke aas-paas khade logon par.

King Aldric.

Princess Rivona.

Arch-Scholar Thalorien.

Healer Ilyra.

Aur Royal Guard Garrick.

Love ne sabko ek-ek karke dekha. Phir uski nazar Rivona par ruk gayi. Uske baad King par. Phir scholar par.

Uske chehre par ek aisa expression aaya jaise dimaag abhi bhi restart ho raha ho.

Love: "Aap... aap sab yahan kya kar rahe hain?"

Woh zara sa utha, lekin Ilyra ne turant haath aage kar diya.

Ilyra: "Aaram se. Abhi uthne ki koshish mat kijiye. Aap behosh ho gaye the."

Love ne use dekha. Phir apne haath-pair dekhe, jaise check kar raha ho ki sab apni jagah par hain ya nahi.

Love: "Behosh? Main?"

Ek chhota sa pause.

Love: "Achha... haan. Court mein woh dhaaga-vagairah wala scene hua tha..."

Usne dheere se apna maatha chhua.

Love: "Aah... kya hua mujhe? Sir bhi dukh raha hai."

Garrick ke chehre par chinta thi, lekin Love ki baat sunkar uski moonchhon ke neeche halki si muskurahat zaroor ubhri.

Garrick: "Tum court mein gir gaye the. Hum tumhe yahan lekar aaye."

Love ne King ko dekha. Phir Rivona ko. Phir scholar ko.

Uski aankhon mein achanak ek naya khayal chamka.

Woh scholar ki taraf dekhne laga, bilkul us andaaz mein jaise kisi teacher se exam ke baad result poochhna ho—lekin sawaal poochhne se pehle hi use yaad aa gaya ki result shayad bahut bura hone wala hai.

Love: "Mere toh... lag gaye hain na?"

Kamre mein ek pal ke liye sannata chha gaya.

King Aldric ne apni bhaunhein uthayin.

King Aldric: "Tumhe hosh aate hi sabse pehle isi baat ki chinta hai?"

Love ne abhi jawaab dene ke liye munh khola hi tha ki Ilyra beech mein bol padi.

Ilyra: "Tumhari jaan ko filhaal koi turant khatra nazar nahi aa raha. Lekin tumhe aaram karna hoga. Aur jab tak main permission na doon, bed se uthne ki koshish mat karna."

Love: "Ji... lekin—"

Ilyra: "Koi lekin nahi."

Love ka munh aadha khula reh gaya.

Garrick ne apna chehra doosri taraf kar liya. Uske kandhe ek baar halke se hile, lekin usne turant khud ko sambhaal liya.

Rivona ne use dekha. Uske chehre par muskurahat nahi thi.

Rivona: "Tumhe shayad ab samajh aa raha hoga ki tumne court mein kitni badi musibat khadi kar di hai."

King Aldric ne turant uski taraf dekha.

King Aldric: "Rivona."

Rivona: "Kya? Main jhooth toh nahi bol rahi, Father."

Woh Love ki taraf mud gayi.

Rivona: "Tum court mein chillaye, Registry ko todne ki baat ki, aur phir sabke saamne behosh ho gaye. Ab scholar ko tumhari magical reaction ki jaanch karni hai. Aur hum sabko yeh samajhna hai ki tumhare saath hua kya."

Uski awaaz thandi thi. Gussa abhi bhi saaf tha, lekin woh pehle jaisi sirf tiraskaar se bhari nahi thi. Usne apni aankhon se Love ko dekha—jaise woh samajhne ki koshish kar rahi ho ki uske saamne ek chaalak aadmi hai, koi anjaana jaadu hai, ya bas ek aam insaan jo apni had se zyada badi musibat mein phans gaya hai.

Love ki taraf dekhte hue usne aakhri baat kahi.

Rivona: "Aur nahi, iska matlab yeh nahi ki main tumhe maaf kar rahi hoon."

King Aldric: "Kisi ne tumse maafi maangne ko bhi nahi kaha, Rivona."

King ki awaaz shaant thi, magar usmein woh authority thi jiske saamne court ke judges bhi chup ho gaye the.

Phir unhone Love ki taraf dekha.

King Aldric: "Love, tumhe abhi sab kuch ek saath samajhne ki zaroorat nahi. Lekin tumhe kuch baatein jaanani hongi."

Thalorien ne apna lamba haath uthakar King ko halka sa ishara kiya.

Thalorien: "Majesty, agar aap anumati dein, toh main pehle ek baat saaf karna chahta hoon. Main is samay is yuva ko kisi cheez ka doshi nahi maan raha. Uski aankhon mein jo rang maine dekha, aur Registry ki jo pratikriya hui—main abhi unka kaaran nahi jaanta."

Uski gehri nazar Love par tik gayi.

Thalorien: "Main ek hazaar saal se zyada jee chuka hoon. Lekin main itna ahankari nahi ki har purane jaadu ko samajhne ka daawa karoon."

Rivona ne apni baahen baandh leen.

Rivona: "Toh kya aapko kuch pata chala ya nahi?"

Thalorien: "Ek sawaal saamne aaya hai. Jawaab abhi nahi."

Rivona: "Yeh toh bahut upyogi hai."

Thalorien: "Sach aksar upyogi hone se pehle asuvidhajanak hota hai, Your Highness."

Nymera wahan maujood nahi thi, warna shayad uske chehre par is baat ko sunkar muskurahat aa jaati. Lekin Rivona ke chehre par koi mazaak nahi tha.

King ne haath uthakar behas rok di.

King Aldric: "Bas. Love abhi-abhi hosh mein aaya hai. Use saans lene do."

Phir King ne Love ko sambodhit kiya.

King Aldric: "Ek baat tumhe bata doon. Court ka faisla abhi poori tarah saamne nahi aaya hai. Registry ka bond ab bhi maujood hai, aur usmein jo jaan se judi shart saamne aayi hai, uski jaanch chal rahi hai."

Unke chehre par gambhirta aa gayi.

King Aldric: "Agar Love, Rivona ko chhodkar chala gaya, toh Rivona ki jaan ko khatra hai. Aur agar Rivona, Love ko chhodkar chali gayi, toh Love ki jaan ko khatra hai. Hum abhi nahi jaante ki yeh shart kis tarah kaam karti hai, ya iske peeche ka asli kaaran kya hai."

Rivona ki ungliyan uski baahon par aur kas gayin.

Rivona: "Aur isi wajah se main apni marzi se azaad nahi ho sakti."

Thalorien: "Isi wajah se humein jaldbaazi nahi karni chahiye. Yeh bond Three Laws ke saath kis tarah juda hai—ya kya yeh unke viruddh hai—yeh abhi bhi ek khula sawaal hai."

King ne dheere se sir hilaaya.

King Aldric: "Aur jab tak iska jawaab nahi milta, koi bhi Love ko zabardasti kisi ritual mein nahi le jaayega. Na hi Rivona ko."

Ilyra ne turant baat kaat di.

Ilyra: "Aur koi bhi mere patient ko aaram karne se pehle ek aur sawaal nahi poochhega."

Is baar King ne bhi bina behas kiye sir hila diya.

King Aldric: "Theek hai. Hum tumhe thoda waqt dete hain, Love."

Woh darwaaze ki taraf mudne lage, lekin Rivona abhi bhi wahin khadi thi.

Usne ek pal ke liye Love ko dekha. Uske chehre par ab bhi nafrat aur gussa tha—lekin saath hi ek sawaal bhi tha jo woh poochhna nahi chahti thi.

Aakhir woh boli.

Rivona: "Mujhe tumse ek baat poochhni hai."

King ruk gaye. Thalorien ne bhi apni nazar uski taraf kar li.

Rivona ki amber aankhein seedha Love par tik gayin.

Rivona: "Court mein tumne kaha tha ki tumhe is Registry ke baare mein kuch nahi pata. Tumne kaha tha ki tum sirf khaana lene aaye the, aur tum apni zindagi wapas chahte ho."

Usne ek gehri saans li.

Rivona: "Main abhi bhi tum par bharosa nahi karti. Lekin main yeh jaanana chahti hoon—jab tumne Registry ko todne ki baat kahi, us pal tumhe kya mehsoos hua tha? Kya tumhe sach mein laga tha ki tum usse tod sakte ho?"

Thalorien ne turant kuch nahi kaha. Uski nazar Love par thi, magar woh jawab ko majboor karne ki koshish nahi kar raha tha.

Kamre mein sab shaant ho gaye.

Love ab hosh mein tha. Uska shareer abhi kamzor tha, uske saamne ek dragon king, ek princess jo use pasand nahi karti thi, aur ek aisa scholar tha jo uske baare mein kuch aisa dekh chuka tha jiska matlab woh khud bhi nahi jaanta tha.

Aur sab uske jawaab ka intezaar kar rahe the.
