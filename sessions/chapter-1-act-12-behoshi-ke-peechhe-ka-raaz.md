# Does the Dragon Princess Really Hate Me?
## Chapter 1 — Act 12: Behoshi Ke Peechhe Ka Raaz

Imperial High Court of Aurelthys — Kuch hi pal baad

“Raasta dijiye! Royal healer aa rahi hain!”

Court ke bade darwaazon se Ilyra Dawnpetal tezi se andar aayi. Uske haath mein ek medical case tha, aur uske peeche do palace attendants the. Court mein jama logon ko guards ne peeche hatne ka aadesh diya.

Ilyra tumhare paas ghutnon ke bal baithi aur tumhari nabz check karne lagi.

“Saans chal rahi hai,” usne kaha. “Lekin mujhe samajhna hoga ki behoshi kis wajah se hui.”

Usne apni hatheli tumhare maathe ke paas rakhi. Halki hare rang ki healing magic tumhare shareer ke chaaron taraf phail gayi.

Kuch pal tak sab saamanya raha.

Phir uski bhaunhein sikud gayin.

“Yeh ajeeb hai…”

King Aldric uske paas aa gaye. “Kya hua?”

“Your Majesty, mujhe koi saaf physical injury nahi mil rahi. Na hi yeh aam magical exhaustion jaisa lag raha hai.” Ilyra ne apna spell badla. “Lekin inke shareer ke aas-paas ek bahut halki magical disturbance hai. Jaise kisi spell ne achanak pratikriya di ho.”

“Registry?” Chief Arbiter ne poochha.

Ilyra ne turant haan nahi kahi.

“Main abhi nishchit nahi hoon.”

### Thalorien ki nazar

Arch-Scholar Thalorien tumse kuch door khada tha. Uski nazar ab tumhare chehre par nahi, balki us jagah par thi jahan kuch pal pehle tumhari aankhon mein woh ajeeb rang chamka tha.

Usne apni staff uthayi aur ek bahut chhota diagnostic spell banaya.

Neeli roshni tumhare aas-paas ghoomi—aur phir achanak ek pal ke liye ruk gayi.

Thalorien ki aankhein sikud gayin.

Yeh kya tha?

Usne spell dobara chalaya.

Is baar kuch nahi hua.

Usne apne aap se dheere se kaha, “Nahi… maine galat nahi dekha tha.”

Aldric ne uski baat sun li.

“Arch-Scholar, aapne kuch dekha hai?”

Thalorien ne kuch pal socha. Uski nazar court mein maujood logon par gayi, phir relic par.

“Maine ek asamanya pratikriya dekhi thi. Lekin jab tak main uski dobara pushti nahi kar leta, main koi nishkarsh nahi nikaalunga.”

“Kaunsi pratikriya?”

“Abhi mere paas poora jawaab nahi hai, Your Majesty.”

Aldric ne uski aankhon mein dekha. Woh samajh gaye ki scholar kuch chhupa nahi raha tha—balki apni anishchitata ko lekar saavdhaan tha.

“Jab aapke paas saboot ho, mujhe bataiyega.”

Thalorien ne sir jhuka diya.

“Zaroor.”

### Rivona

Rivona ab bhi Nymera ke paas khadi thi.

Usne Ilyra ko tumhari jaanch karte dekha, phir apni nazar us magical dhaage par daali jo ab pehle se kam chamak raha tha.

“Woh dhaaga hilne laga tha jab Love ne use todne ke liye kaha,” Nymera ne dheere se kaha.

Rivona ne jawaab nahi diya.

“Rivona?”

“Main sun rahi hoon.”

Uski awaaz kathor thi, lekin uska dhyaan tum par hi tha.

Nymera ne kuch pal baad poochha, “Tumhein lagta hai usne jaanbujhkar kuch kiya?”

Rivona ki aankhon mein gussa wapas ubhar aaya.

“Mujhe nahi pata.”

Yeh sach tha.

Woh tum par bharosa nahi karti thi. Lekin usne tumhari aankhon mein jo ghabrahat dekhi thi, woh kisi aise insaan ki nahi lag rahi thi jo apni planning ke mutaabik kaam kar raha ho.

“Usne khud kaha ki use relic ke baare mein kuch nahi pata,” Nymera ne yaad dilaya.

Rivona ne uski taraf dekha.

“Main jaanti hoon ki usne kya kaha.”

“Phir—”

“Nymera, please. Abhi nahi.”

Nymera chup ho gayi.

Rivona ne apni nazar hata li, lekin uske chehre par likha hua sawaal nahi mita:

Agar Love bhi is sab mein phansa hua hai, toh phir yeh bond bana kisne? Aur kyun?

### Court ka aadesh

Ilyra ne apna spell samaapt kiya aur khadi ho gayi.

“Inhein palace ke healer wing mein le jaana chahiye. Main wahan inki poori jaanch kar sakungi. Abhi ke liye main yeh nahi keh sakti ki yeh kab hosh mein aayenge.”

Aldric ne turant aadesh diya, “Inhein healer wing mein le jaaya jaaye. Unki dekhbhaal mein koi kami nahi honi chahiye. Aur jab tak unki haalat clear nahi hoti, koi bhi unse sawaal nahi karega.”

Garrick aage aaya.

“Main inke saath jaaunga, Your Majesty.”

Aldric ne haan mein sir hilaaya.

Do attendants tumhein saavdhaani se stretcher par rakhne lage. Ilyra unke saath chalne ko taiyaar hui.

Thalorien ne ek baar phir relic ki taraf dekha. Us par bane naye patterns dheere-dheere gaayab ho rahe the, lekin usne unka ek hissa apni magical memory mein darj kar liya tha.

Woh abhi nahi jaanta tha ki tumhari aankhon mein kya chamka tha.

Woh yeh bhi nahi jaanta tha ki relic ne tumhari awaaz par pratikriya kyun di.

Lekin ek baat ab saaf thi: is bond ki kahaani sirf do anjaan logon ki zabardasti hui shaadi tak seemit nahi thi.

Aur uska agla jawaab shayad usi insaan ke hosh mein aane ke baad mil sakta tha, jo ab healer wing ki taraf le jaaya ja raha tha.

Scene abhi Love ke behosh hone ke turant baad hi hai. Agla pal tab aage badhega jab Love hosh mein aayega.
