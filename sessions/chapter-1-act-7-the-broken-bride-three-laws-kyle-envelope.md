# Does the Dragon Princess Really Hate Me?
## Chapter 1 — Act 7: Broken Bride Three Laws Kyle Envelope

Imperial Palace of Aurelthys — Royal Reception Hall

Baarish ki awaaz bade hall mein goonj rahi thi. Khidkiyon ke paar aasman ab bhi kaale baadalon se dhaka hua tha, aur kabhi-kabhi bijli ki chamak royal hall ke sunehre pillars par bani dragon carvings ko roshan kar deti thi.

King Aldric tumhari baat dhyaan se sun rahe the.

Jab tumne Three Laws ko apne shabdon mein samjhaya, toh unke chehre par halki si muskaan aayi. Woh tumhara mazaak nahi uda rahe the. Balki, unhe shayad achha laga ki ek door ke gaon mein rehne wale kisaan ki maa ne apne bachche ko itni purani baatein sikhayi thi.

King Aldric: "Tumhe exact words yaad nahi hain, lekin tumne unka matlab samajh liya hai. Aur kai baar, Love, kanoon ke shabdon ko yaad rakhne se zyada zaroori hota hai unki spirit ko samajhna."

Unhone apne haath peeche baandhe aur hall ki unchi chhat ki taraf dekha.

King Aldric: "Pehla Law sirf itna nahi kehta ki sab log mil-julkar rahein. Woh kehta hai ki har race ko doosri races ki traditions ka samman karna chahiye, jab tak woh buniyadi kanoon ka ullanghan na kare."

King Aldric: "Doosra Law kehta hai ki citizenship aur protection kisi ki race, origin, gender ya religion ke aadhar par kam nahi ki ja sakti. Ek insaan, elf, dragon, beastfolk ya dwarf—sabko kanoon ke saamne suraksha milni chahiye."

Unki awaaz ab thodi gambhir ho gayi.

King Aldric: "Aur teesra Law\... woh sirf saza dene ke baare mein nahi hai. Woh un logon ko rokne ke liye hai jo apni nafrat ko doosron ki zindagi barbaad karne ka bahaana banate hain."

Woh kuch pal khamosh rahe.

King Aldric: "Legendary Hero ne ye laws isliye nahi banaye the ki duniya achanak perfect ho jaaye. Unhone ye laws isliye banaye the taaki jab taakatwar log galat hon, toh kamzor logon ke paas bhi insaaf maangne ka haq ho."

Phir tumne apna agla sawaal poochha.

Agar Registry ki jaanch mein pata chala ki tumne kuch galat nahi kiya... agar sach mein tumhara naam Rivona ke saath darj hua hai... toh kya tumhe maar diya jayega?

Tumhari awaaz mein chhupi ghabrahat ne King ke chehre se woh halki muskaan mita di.

Is baar unhone jawab dene se pehle ek pal bhi nahi liya.

King Aldric: "Nahi, Love. Sirf isliye ki tumhari shaadi meri beti ke saath darj hui hai, main tumhe marne ka aadesh nahi dunga."

Unki awaaz poore hall mein goonj uthi.

King Aldric: "Aur agar kisi ne tumhe bina saboot ke nuksaan pahunchane ki koshish ki, toh use mere saamne jawaab dena hoga."

Darwaze ke paas khade guards seedhe ho gaye. Yeh koi khaali tasalli dene wali baat nahi thi. Emperor ne sabke saamne apna faisla suna diya tha.

Lekin phir unhone apni awaaz dheemi kar li.

King Aldric: "Main tumse ek baat chhupana nahi chahta. Tumhari jaan bachane aur tumhari zindagi aasaan hone mein farq hai."

Unhone saamne rakhe purane Registry record ki taraf dekha.

King Aldric: "Agar marriage bond sach mein valid hai, toh bahut se log use apne fayde ke liye istemaal karna chahenge. Kuch tumhe apni political game ka mohra banayenge. Kuch tumhe Rivona se alag karne ki koshish karenge. Aur kuch log shayad tumhe isliye na-pasand karein kyunki tum ek aisi jagah par aa gaye ho jahan woh tumhe dekhna nahi chahte."

Unhone tumhari aankhon mein dekha.

King Aldric: "Main tumse yeh vaada nahi kar sakta ki koi tumhe kabhi daraayega nahi. Lekin main yeh vaada kar sakta hoon ki jab tak tum yahan ho, tumhe bina sunwai aur bina saboot ke saza nahi di jayegi."

Usi waqt, hall ke bade darwaze ke bahar se tez kadmon ki awaaz aayi.

Tak. Tak. Tak.

Royal Steward Theron Silverbough andar aaya. Uske haath mein kaale rang ka ek sealed envelope tha, jis par Imperial Military ka insignia bana hua tha.

Theron: "Your Majesty, maafi chahta hoon ki main beech mein aa raha hoon. Lekin yeh sandesh abhi-abhi military headquarters se aaya hai."

King Aldric ne envelope liya aur seal tod di.

Jaise-jaise woh padhte gaye, unki aankhon ka expression badalta gaya.

King Aldric: "Yeh kab aaya?"

Theron: "Kuch hi der pehle, Your Majesty. Ismein Crown Princess ke ek purane academy companion, Lord Kyle Dravenor ka zikr hai."

King ne nazar uthayi.

King Aldric: "Kyle Dravenor?"

Theron: "Ji, Your Majesty. Woh aaj ki royal ceremony mein expected tha, lekin abhi tak palace nahi pahuncha. Military headquarters ko bhi uski current location ki pushti nahi mili hai."

King kuch pal khamosh rahe.

Kyle ek ordinary dragon noble nahi tha. Woh Imperial Academy ka distinguished graduate, skilled warrior aur ek respected dragon family ka member tha. Rivona ke saath uski purani dosti bhi court mein koi secret nahi thi.

Lekin uska aaj ke din gayab rehna—jab Rivona ki engagement ceremony ek ancient marriage bond ki wajah se toot chuki thi—ek ajeeb coincidence tha.

King Aldric: "Uski aakhri confirmed location pata karo. Aur is baat ko abhi palace mein afwaah mat banne dena."

Theron: "As you command, Your Majesty."

Steward jaane ke liye muda, phir uski nazar ek pal ke liye tum par padi. Ek gareeb dikhne wala human, royal food ke saamne baitha hua, aur Emperor khud usse baat kar rahe the.

Theron ne koi sawaal nahi kiya. Woh jhukkar hall se bahar chala gaya.

King Aldric ne envelope fold kiya aur use apne coat ke andar rakh liya.

King Aldric: "Lagta hai, Love, aaj ke din kuch zyada hi sawaal paida ho rahe hain."

Unhone tumhari taraf dekha.

King Aldric: "Tum apna khana aaram se khao. Tumhe abhi kisi cheez ka faisla karne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Lekin jab tum taiyaar ho, main tumse tumhare gaon aur tumhari maa ke baare mein kuch aur poochhna chahta hoon."

Unhone ek guard ko ishara kiya ki woh darwaze ke paas rahe, phir tumhe thoda waqt diya.

Bahar baarish ab pehle se dheemi ho gayi thi.

Lekin palace ke andar, ek ancient marriage bond, ek royal princess ka gussa, aur ek missing dragon warrior—yeh teeno apni-apni jagah par ek aisi kahani ki shuruaat kar rahe the jiska anjaam abhi kisi ko nahi pata tha.
