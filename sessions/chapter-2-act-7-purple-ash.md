Garrick dekhkar chaunk gaya. Ilyra bhi chaunk gayi. Rivona, uske dost bhi chaunk gaye. Pilot bhi chaunk gaya. Sab chaunk gaye. Love ne apna maatha pakad liya — jaise use pata tha aisa hi hoga. Woh disappointed tha — lag raha tha lo, phir se wahi shuru ho gaya.

Hairaani ki baat — woh monsters S-rank bhi nahi the. Bas ghin ki wajah se bhaag rahi thi. Kuch monsters us par attack karte, woh dodge karti.

Maera: "Chhi! Main nahi lad rahi! Main nahi chhoongi! Mujhe mat chhoona! Main nahi ladoongi!"

Kabhi yahan koodti, kabhi wahan. Har baar wahi rat — mujhe mat chhoona.

Ye dekh Garrick hairaan ho gaya:

Garrick: "Ye chal kya raha hai? Is ladki ne abhi S-rank ke, usse bhi bade bhayaanak monster ko maara... aur ye kuch chipkaliyon se bhaag rahi hai? Kya hai ye? Kya ho raha hai ye?"

Ilyra ne munh khula rakha, phir boli:

Ilyra: "Main... main samajhne ka process kar rahi hoon... lekin process ho hi nahi raha..."

Rivona ke friends ka munh neeche gir gaya. Seraphine ne Vaelora se kaha:

Seraphine: "Ye wahi maid hai na... jisne abhi... ye bhaag kyun rahi hai?"

Vaelora: "Mujhe... mujhe kuch samajh nahi aa raha..."

Nymera: "Koi mujhe samjhaaye... ho kya raha hai yahan?"

Elowen: "Main... main confused hoon... bahut confused..."

Kyle bhi ye sab dekhkar thoda ajeeb mehsoos kar raha tha, aadhe-hosh me.

Tabhi ek kone se ek doosra snake-type monster nikla aur Maera ko jakad liya. Jaise hi aisa hua, Maera freeze ho gayi — ghin aur dar ke maare uska shareer kaam hi nahi kar raha tha.

Maera: "Na! Mujhe mat chhoo! Nahi! Kitni ghin aa rahi hai! Main nahi hil paa rahi! Mujhe ghin aa rahi hai, mujhe bahut ghin aa rahi hai!"

Dheere-dheere ek-ek karke saare chipkali-type monsters bhi Maera ke upar chadhne lage. Isi dauraan uska dhyaan Love par gaya — jo use ajeeb disappointing nigaahon se dekh raha tha, jaise "ye ladki kya kar rahi hai, main kya karoon iska" wale look se.

Jab Rivona ne Love ka naam suna, woh hairaan ho gayi. Rivona mann me sochne lagi — madad ke liye Love ko kyun bula rahi hai? Woh toh ek mamooli insaan hai, kisaan hai woh. Kisaan ko hi kyun pukaar rahi hai? Ye use kyun bula rahi hai?

Jaise hi Maera ne Love ka naam liya, sab upar dekhne lage. Garrick aur Ilyra bhi Love ko dekhne lage. Woh kuch bole nahi — bas confused nigaahon se dekhte rahe. Sab confused the.

Love: "Main nahi aa raha. Tumhein apne dar par kaabu paana seekhna hoga. Kab tak tum in tuchchh chhote-mote rengne wale jeevon se darti rahogi? Ye toh dhang ke monsters bhi nahi hain."

Love: "Main nahi aa raha Maera, deal karo isse. Jaldi karo."

Maera: "Theek hai! Agar tum nahi aaye toh poori zindagi tumhein sirf bitter gourd ki sabzi khaani padegi! Sirf bitter gourd hi nahi, duniya ki har bitter sabzi — Neem greens, Tamarind, Raw mango, Wild gooseberry — sab daal doongi! Aur tumhein khana banakar khilaaungi! Tumhein achchi fasal ugaane nahi doongi — sirf bitter gourd ugega khet me, sirf bitter gourd, sirf aur sirf bitter gourd ugega! Main tumhein majboor kar doongi woh cheez khane ke liye, majboor kar doongi, majboor kar doongi!"

Maera aur Love dono chillakar baat kar rahe the. Phir sab Love ki taraf dekhne lage.

Kyun hoti hain bitter sabziyan? Is duniya me bitter sabziyan kyun hain? Tamarind kyun hai? Raw mango kyun hai? Wild gooseberry kyun hai? Neem greens kyun hain?"

Rivona aur uske dost ye hota dekh rahe the.
