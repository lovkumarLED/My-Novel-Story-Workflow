# Does the Dragon Princess Really Hate Me?
## Chapter 1 — Act 13: Woh Raaz Jo Registry Ne Chhupa Rakha Tha

### Imperial Palace — Royal Healer Wing

Court ke us bade hall mein Love ke behosh hokar girne ki awaaz abhi bhi logon ke zehan mein goonj rahi thi.

Kuch hi der pehle jahan paanch judges, royal guards aur dragon nobles ek purane relic ke faisle par behas kar rahe the, ab wahan ek ajeeb sa sannata tha. Ancient Marriage Registry apne pedestal par rakhi thi. Uski sunehri roshni bujh chuki thi, lekin uske aas-paas hawa ab bhi halki-halki kaanp rahi thi—jaise kisi ne ek aisi cheez ko chhed diya ho jise sadiyon se chhoona mana tha.

Do royal attendants Love ko healer wing tak le aaye the. Unke peeche Royal Guard Garrick Thornvale chal raha tha, uska chehra sakht aur aankhon mein chinta thi.

Royal healer Ilyra Dawnpetal ne sabko bed se door rehne ka ishara kiya.

"Use aaram se lita do. Aur koi bhi bina meri permission ke is room mein magic cast nahi karega."

Love ko ek bade, saaf kamre ke bed par lita diya gaya. Khidkiyon ke bahar baarish ab halki ho chuki thi. Safed parde thandi hawa mein dheere-dheere hil rahe the. Kamre mein medicinal herbs ki khushboo thi, aur deewar par lage blue crystals naram roshni de rahe the.

Ilyra ne Love ki kalai par do ungliyan rakheen. Phir uske maathe ke upar haath rakhkar ek healing spell chalaya.

Uski ungliyon se nikli neeli roshni Love ke shareer par phaili—magar uske seene ke paas pahunchte hi roshni ek pal ke liye tedhi ho gayi.

Ilyra ki bhaunhein sikud gayin.

"Yeh kya...?"

Garrick turant aage badha. "Kya hua, Healer?"

"Uski saans theek hai. Dil ki dhadkan bhi normal hai. Koi zahiri chot nahi." Ilyra ne spell rok diya. "Lekin uske andar jo magical disturbance hai, woh aam thakaan, bhookh ya magic ke zyada istemal se nahi hui."

"Matlab kisi ne us par spell kiya?"

"Main abhi yeh nahi keh sakti." Ilyra ki awaaz gambhir ho gayi. "Aur main bina saboot ke kisi ko doshi nahi thaharaungi."

Garrick ne bed par pade Love ki taraf dekha. Uske chehre par ab wohi bebasi thi jo usne court mein dekhi thi—ek aadmi jo bas apni zindagi wapas maang raha tha, lekin poori saltanat usse ek aisa jawaab maang rahi thi jo shayad uske paas tha hi nahi.

"Usne kisi par hamla nahi kiya," Garrick dheere se bola. "Usne sirf apni azaadi maangi."

Ilyra ne jawab nahi diya. Uski nazar ab bhi Love ke seene ke paas hawa mein bachi halki si roshni par thi. Woh roshni dheere-dheere gaayab ho gayi, jaise kabhi thi hi nahi.

Magar Ilyra ne use dekha tha.

Aur woh use bhoolne wali nahi thi.

### Court of the Empire — Usi waqt

Courtroom mein Arch-Scholar Thalorien Vael’Sharen ab bhi Registry ke saamne khada tha.

Uska lagbhag solah-foot lamba kad, chaandi-jaisi lambe baal aur gehri, samajhdaar aankhein use doosre elves se alag banati thi. Woh chronological age mein ek hazaar saal se bhi zyada jee chuka tha, lekin uske chehre par insaani budhape jaisi kamzori nahi thi. Uske haath mein ek patli crystal rod thi, jiski nok Registry ke upar mandra rahi thi.

Chief Arbiter Maelor Veyne ne poochha, "Arch-Scholar, kya aap bata sakte hain ki abhi kya hua tha?"

Thalorien ne turant jawaab nahi diya.

"Main itna keh sakta hoon ki Registry ne ek aisi pratikriya di jo mere dekhe hue kisi bhi marriage-binding ritual se mel nahi khaati. Lekin main yeh nahi keh sakta ki uska kaaran woh insaan tha, khud Registry thi, ya un dono ke beech ka koi sambandh."

Ek judge ne asantosh se kaha, "Humein andaze nahi, jawaab chahiye."

"Toh mujhe jaanch karne dijiye," Thalorien ne shaant lekin mazboot awaaz mein kaha. "Ek hazaar saal se zyada jeene ke baad maine ek baat seekhi hai, Your Honour—purani cheez ka matlab yeh nahi ki hum usse samajhte hain. Aur kisi cheez ka sach lagna, uske sahi hone ka saboot nahi hota."

King Aldric ne apni kursi ke armrest par ungliyan tap ki.

"Is jaanch ko poori authority di jaati hai. Lekin ek baat yaad rahe: jab tak humein is bond ki asli nature samajh nahi aati, Love aur Princess Rivona dono ki suraksha hamari zimmedari hai."

Rivona, jo ab tak chup thi, achanak khadi ho gayi.

"Father, aap baar-baar unki suraksha ki baat kar rahe hain. Lekin meri suraksha ka kya? Mujhse meri marzi poochhe bina meri shaadi declare kar di gayi. Ab kaha ja raha hai ki agar main unse alag hui toh woh mar jaayenge. Aur agar woh mujhe chhodkar gaye, toh main mar jaaungi!"

Uski amber aankhon mein sunehri aag chamak uthi.

"Yeh shaadi nahi hai. Yeh dhamki hai."

Kisi judge ne virodh nahi kiya.

Kyunki us pal, uske shabdon ka jawaab kisi ke paas nahi tha.

Thalorien ne apni crystal rod neeche kar di.

"Princess, main aapki baat ko nazarandaaz nahi kar raha. Lekin ek aur sawaal hai jiska jawaab humare paas nahi hai."

Rivona ne uski taraf dekha.

"Kaunsa sawaal?"

"Yeh Registry aakhir kis authority ke tahat kaam kar rahi hai? Aur agar iske niyam Three Laws ke saath takraate hain, toh kya yeh sach mein un Laws ke anuroop hai—ya humne sadiyon se iski nature ko galat samjha hai?"

King Aldric ka chehra pehle se bhi zyada gambhir ho gaya.

Three Laws ka naam aate hi kuch buzurg dragon nobles ek-doosre ki taraf dekhne lage. Un laws ko challenge karna aam baat nahi thi. Lekin unka naam lekar kisi insaan ki azaadi cheen lena bhi ek aisa sawaal tha jise dabaaya nahi ja sakta tha.

Rivona ne apne haath kas liye.

"Toh jawaab dhoondhiye, Scholar. Mujhe is shaadi se nafrat hai. Aur main apni poori zindagi kisi anjaan relic ke faisle ke bharose nahi chhodungi."

Woh mudkar court ke darwaaze ki taraf chal di.

Nymera uske peeche gayi. Seraphine aur Vaelora bhi saath nikal gayin, lekin Elowen kuch der tak wahin ruki rahi. Uski nazar Registry par thi—jaise woh samajh rahi ho ki asli khatra shayad Love ya Rivona nahi, balki woh cheez hai jise dono samajh nahi paa rahe.

### Kuch der baad — Royal Healer Wing

Kamre ke bahar kadmon ki aahat sunayi di.

Darwaaza khula.

Sabse pehle King Aldric andar aaye. Unke peeche Thalorien tha, aur unke saath Princess Rivona bhi thi.

Rivona ka aana kisi ko expected nahi tha—shayad use khud bhi nahi. Usne apne aap ko yeh samjhaya tha ki woh sirf isliye aayi hai kyunki is maamle ka seedha asar uski zindagi par pad raha tha. Use Love ki chinta hone ka koi matlab nahi tha.

Kamre mein aate hi uski nazar bed par gayi.

Love ab bhi behosh tha.

Uske paas khadi Ilyra ne King ko salaam kiya. "Majesty, abhi tak unhe hosh nahi aaya. Lekin unki condition stable hai."

"Kitni der se behosh hain?" Rivona ne poochha.

Ilyra ne uski taraf dekha. "Court se laaye jaane ke baad se. Main unhe jagane ke liye zabardasti healing magic istemal nahi karna chahti. Unka shareer khud recover kar raha hai."

Rivona ne apni baahon ko seene ke saamne baandh liya.

"Main bas yeh sunna chahti hoon ki woh theek hain. Agar unhe kuch ho gaya, toh sab log mujhe dekhenge—jaise maine hi kuch kiya ho."

Nymera, jo uske saath aayi thi, dheere se boli, "Rivona, kisi ne tumhe doshi nahi kaha."

"Abhi nahi," Rivona ne turant jawaab diya. "Lekin agar woh bond sach mein humari jaan se juda hai, toh har cheez ka dosh hum dono par daala jaayega. Aur main kisi ko apni marzi ke khilaaf mujhe control karne nahi doongi."

Thalorien bed se kuch door ruk gaya. Usne Love ko dhyaan se dekha, lekin is baar koi spell cast nahi kiya.

"Jab woh court mein gire, maine unki aankhon mein ek ajeeb rang dekha tha," usne kaha. "Ek pal ke liye. Main nahi jaanta woh kya tha."

King Aldric ne uski taraf mudkar dekha. "Kya woh Registry se juda tha?"

"Yeh kehna jaldbaazi hogi. Main sirf itna jaanta hoon ki us pal Registry ki magical reaction aur unki aankhon mein dikhe rang ka samay ek hi tha." Thalorien ne apni crystal rod apne vastra mein rakh li. "Yeh sambandh ho sakta hai. Ittefaq bhi. Mujhe abhi kuch bhi nishchit nahi kehna chahiye."

Rivona ne bed ki taraf dekha.

"Toh woh sach mein nahi jaanta ki uske saath kya ho raha hai?"

Kisi ne turant jawaab nahi diya.

Uski nazar Love ke chehre par tik gayi. Court mein uski awaaz ab bhi Rivona ke zehan mein thi—uska gussa, uski azaadi ki maang, aur woh sawaal jo kisi bhi royal protocol se bada tha: Meri zindagi ka faisla koi aur kaise kar sakta hai?

Rivona ki bhaunhein sikud gayin.

"Main ab bhi usse apna pati nahi maanti," usne thande lehje mein kaha. "Aur main us par bharosa nahi karti."

"Yeh kehne ke liye tumhe kisi ki permission nahi chahiye," King Aldric ne jawaab diya. "Lekin jab tak sach saamne nahi aata, humein uski baat sunni hogi—aur tumhari bhi."

Rivona ne kuch nahi kaha.

Usi waqt, bed ke paas rakhe healing crystals ki roshni ek pal ke liye halki si tez hui.

Ilyra turant aage badhi aur Love ki nabz dobara check ki.

"Rukiye..."

Kamre mein sabki nazar us par tik gayi.

Ilyra ne Love ki kalai chhodkar uske chehre ki taraf dekha. "Unki saans badal rahi hai. Woh hosh mein aa rahe hain."

Nymera ek kadam peeche hat gayi. Thalorien bilkul sthir khada raha. King Aldric ki nazar gambhir ho gayi.

Aur Rivona—

Woh bed ke paas khadi rahi, uske chehre par ab bhi gussa tha. Lekin uski aankhon mein ek naya sawaal bhi tha, jise woh khud se bhi poochhna nahi chahti thi.

Kuch pal baad Love ki aankhein khulne lagti hain.

Sabse pehle dhundhli chhat nazar aati hai. Phir healing crystals ki neeli roshni, dawaaiyon ki halki khushboo, aur bed ke paas khade logon ki parchhaiyaan dheere-dheere saaf hone lagti hain.

King Aldric sabse pehle bolte hain.

"Love? Kya tum meri awaaz sun sakte ho?"

Ilyra turant kehti hai, "Use ek saath sawaalon se mat gheriye. Use pehle samajhne dijiye ki woh kahan hai."

Rivona ab bhi kuch door khadi hai. Uski nazar Love par hai, aur uske hothon par kai sawaal hain—lekin woh unmein se ek bhi poochhne se pehle khud ko rok leti hai.

Thalorien shaant awaaz mein kehta hai, "Jab tum taiyaar ho, mujhe tumse court mein hui us magical reaction ke baare mein baat karni hai. Lekin abhi nahi, agar tumhe aaram ki zaroorat hai."

Kamre mein sab tumhare hosh mein aane ka intezaar kar rahe hain.

Love ab hosh mein hai. Woh kya kehta hai ya karta hai?
