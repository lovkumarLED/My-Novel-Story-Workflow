# Does the Dragon Princess Really Hate Me?
## Chapter 1 — Act 10: Ek Aisa Bandhan Jiska Anjaam Maut Hai

Imperial High Court of Aurelthys — Subah, pehli sunwai jaari hai.

Court ke vishaal gumbad ke neeche tumhari awaaz goonj uthi.

“Yeh aap kya keh rahe hain, sir? Main toh bas ek aam aadmi hoon. Mujhe toh pata bhi nahi tha ki khaana khaane ke chakkar mein itna kuch ho jaayega! Agar mujhe pehle se pata hota, toh main mahal ki taraf aata hi nahi!”

Tumhari baaton ke baad court mein baithe log ek-doosre ko dekhne lage.

Tumhare saamne paanch judges the. Unke peeche Three Laws ke prateek patthar par ukere hue the. Ek taraf Dragon Emperor Aldric baithe the, aur doosri taraf unki beti—Princess Rivona Vaeltharis.

Tumne apni baat jaari rakhi.

“Mujhe bas ek aam zindagi chahiye. Mujhe koi royal status nahi chahiye, koi mahal nahi chahiye, koi zameen ya daulat nahi chahiye. Maine toh kabhi in cheezon ke liye koshish bhi nahi ki!”

Rivona ki aankhein tum par tik gayin.

Woh pehli baar tumhari baat ko sirf ek bahana samajhkar nazarandaaz nahi kar rahi thi. Tumhari baaton mein use ek aisi zid sunayi de rahi thi jo kisi rajgaddi, daulat ya political power ke liye nahi thi.

Lekin usse isse koi sukoon nahi mila.

Kyunki agar tum sach mein ek aam kisaan the, toh iska matlab tha ki tum bhi usi ajeeb Registry ke faisle mein phans gaye the.

Tumne court ki taraf dekhte hue kaha:

“Aap is tarah mujhe ek purane relic ke kaaran yahan baandhkar nahi rakh sakte! Agar ek relic kehta hai ki hum dono pati-patni hain, toh kya hum sirf isliye ek-doosre ke saath rehne lagen kyunki usne keh diya? Agar hum khud is shaadi ko nahi maante, toh phir isse kya fark padta hai?”

Kuch nobles ne naaraazgi mein apni jagah badli.

Ek dragon magistrate ne kuch kehne ke liye munh khola, lekin Chief Arbiter Maelor ne haath uthakar use rok diya.

Tumhari baat ab aur saaf ho gayi.

“Meri zindagi meri hai! Koi relic ise decide kaise kar sakta hai? Mujhe iske baare mein kuch nahi pata. Mujhe toh yeh bhi nahi pata ki relic hota kya hai!”

Court ke andar sannata chha gaya.

“Main is shaadi ko nahi maanta. Main yahan se chala jaaunga. Aur yeh mera adhikaar hai! Three Laws mujhe bhi apni freedom aur apni marzi ke saath jeene ka adhikaar dete hain. Bas, main itna hi kehna chahta hoon.”

Is baar kisi ne turant jawaab nahi diya.

### Princess Rivona ka jawaab

Rivona dheere se apni seat se uthi.

Uske crimson royal gown ke kinaaron par bane sunehre dragon symbols subah ki roshni mein chamak rahe the. Usne tumhari taraf dekha, aur uske chehre par ek kathor, thaka hua bhaav tha.

“Tum samajhte ho ki mujhe yeh sab pasand hai?”

Uski awaaz poore court tak pahunchi.

“Tum sochte ho ki main apni zindagi kisi anjaan aadmi ke saath baantna chahti hoon? Maine apni poori zindagi is Empire ki zimmedariyon ke liye taiyaar ki hai. Maine apne bhavishya ke baare mein khud faisle karne ki umeed rakhi thi.”

Woh ek pal ruki.

“Lekin ab ek purana relic kehta hai ki tum mere pati ho. Aur mujhe isse sweekar karna chahiye?”

Uski aankhon mein sunehri roshni chamki.

“Main bhi is shaadi ko nahi maanti.”

Yeh shabd usne bahut saaf tareeke se kahe.

Na koi sharm, na koi romantic sanket, na koi chhupi hui narmi.

Sirf inkaar.

“Lekin,” usne aage kaha, “tum yahan se chale jaane ki baat itni aasaani se nahi kar sakte jab tak court yeh tay nahi kar leta ki is bond ke kya parinaam honge.”

King Aldric ne apni beti ki taraf dekha, phir Chief Arbiter se bole, “Humne abhi tak yeh tay nahi kiya hai ki is relic ka bond dono pakshon par kis tarah ka prabhaav daalta hai. Sirf kanooni validity confirm hona kaafi nahi hai.”

Chief Arbiter ne gambhirta se sir hilaaya.

“Bilkul, Your Majesty. Isi liye hum final declaration se pehle poori jaanch karenge.”

Unhone archivist ki taraf mudkar kaha, “Original record ko dobara scan kijiye. Is baar har layer, har inscription aur har magical signature ko alag-alag verify kiya jaaye.”

Do court mages pedestal ke paas aaye.

Unhone apne crystal instruments record ke dono taraf rakhe. Pehle ne neeli roshni chhodi, doosre ne safed. Runes ek-ek karke chamakne lage.

Sab kuch saamanya dikh raha tha.

Phir achanak—

KRAAAK!

Ek tez awaaz court ke gumbad mein goonj uthi.

Pedestal ke neeche ka patthar halkasa kaanp gaya.

Court mages turant peeche hat gaye.

“Yeh kya tha?” ek judge ne poochha.

Record ke kaale-sunehre frame par kuch aise patterns ubharne lage jo pehle kisi ko nazar nahi aaye the.

Woh aam runes nahi the.

Woh bahut patli, ek-doosre mein guthi hui rekhaayein thi—jaise do alag magical dhaaraayein ek hi dhaage mein bandh di gayi hon.

Aur phir, ek pal ke liye, un dono rekhaon ke beech ek laal bindu chamka.

Chief Arbiter apni jagah se uth khade hue.

“Yeh pattern pehle kyon nahi dikha?”

Kisi ke paas jawaab nahi tha.

Tabhi court ke pichhle darwaaze ke paas se ek bhaari, lekin spasht awaaz sunayi di.

“Kyuki aap galat layer ko dekh rahe hain.”

Sabhi log mud gaye.

Darwaaze par ek aisa elf khada tha jo court ke adhiktar logon se kahin zyada uncha tha.

Uska kad lagbhag solah feet tha. Uske lambe, chaandi jaise baal kandhon se neeche gir rahe the, aur uska chehra ek mature, shaant elf ka tha—insaani maayne mein boodha nahi, lekin uski aankhon mein sadiyon ka tajurba saaf dikh raha tha.

Uske maathe aur gardan par bareek magical markings thi. Uske haath mein ek lambi, purani staff thi, jiske sir par neela crystal dheere-dheere chamak raha tha.

Woh Arch-Scholar Thalorien Vael'Sharen tha—Elven Kingdom ka ek pracheen scholar, jo apni zindagi ka ek bada hissa purane magical covenants, extinct scripts aur Ancient Marriage Registry jaise relics ki study mein bita chuka tha.

Uski umar sadiyon mein nahi, balki ek hazaar saal se bhi aage gini ja sakti thi. Elves ke liye umr ka matlab insaano jaisa chehre par jhurriyan hona nahi tha. Unka shareer aur kad unki lambi zindagi ke saath badhta tha; unki magical energy bhi unke jeevan ka hissa thi.

Thalorien abhi bhi apni jaati ke liye ek shaktishaali aur jeevant scholar tha.

Lekin uski aankhon mein is waqt jo bhaav tha, woh kisi aam research discovery ka nahi tha.

Woh hairaan tha.

“Arch-Scholar Thalorien,” Chief Arbiter ne kaha, “aapko is sunwai mein bulaya gaya tha?”

“Nahi,” Thalorien ne jawaab diya. “Mujhe Registry ke ek pracheen inscription ke baare mein sandesh mila tha. Main original record ko dekhne ke liye aaya hoon. Lekin jo pattern abhi saamne aaya hai…”

Usne apni staff ko zameen par tikaaya.

“Yeh meri umeed se kahin zyada purana hai.”

Uski nazar pehle relic par gayi, phir tumhari taraf, aur aakhir mein Rivona par.

“Tum dono mein se kisi ko bhi is baat ka andaaza nahi hai ki tum kis cheez ke andar bandh gaye ho, hai na?”

Court mein ek thandi khamoshi phail gayi.

Rivona ne turant jawaab diya, “Agar aapke paas koi jaankaari hai, toh seedhe bataiye.”

Thalorien ne uski baat ka bura nahi maana. Usne bas record ki taraf dekha.

“Main bataunga, Princess. Lekin pehle mujhe is pattern ko poori tarah padhna hoga.”

Aur usne apni staff ka crystal relic ke kareeb laaya.

## 5. Arch-Scholar ka Khulaasa — Do Zindagiyan, Ek Bandhan

Thalorien ne apni aankhein band kar li.

Uski staff ke crystal se nikalti neeli roshni ab seedhe relic par nahi pad rahi thi. Woh hawa mein ghoomkar uske chaaron taraf ek gol ghera bana rahi thi. Dheere-dheere us ghere mein purane symbols ubharne lage—kuch Dragon Empire ki pracheen lipi se milte-julte the, kuch Elven Kingdom ki extinct language se, aur kuch aise the jinhein court mein maujood kisi scholar ne pehle kabhi nahi dekha tha.

Thalorien ne apni ungli se ek symbol ko chhua.

Phir doosre ko.

Uske chehre ki shaanti dheere-dheere badalne lagi.

“Yeh mumkin nahi…”

Chief Arbiter aage jhuke. “Aapne kya dekha?”

Thalorien ne turant jawaab nahi diya. Usne apni staff ka crystal aur tez chamkaya.

Is baar relic ke upar do roshniyon ke dhaage ubhre.

Ek sunehra.

Ek gehra laal.

Sunehra dhaaga Rivona ki taraf badha. Laal dhaaga tumhari taraf.

Dono dhaage hawa mein alag-alag raaste banate hue gaye, lekin jaise hi woh ek-doosre ke kareeb aaye, unke beech ek aisa pracheen symbol ban gaya jo ek band darwaaze jaisa dikh raha tha.

Thalorien ki aankhein phail gayin.

“Ab samajh aaya…”

Usne staff neeche kar li.

“Yeh sirf Marriage Registry nahi hai.”

Rivona ki awaaz turant aayi. “Toh phir yeh kya hai?”

Thalorien ne uski taraf dekha.

“Yeh ek Soul-Binding Covenant hai. Ek aisa magical contract jo do logon ko sirf kanooni taur par nahi, balki unke jeevan ki dhaara ke star par ek-doosre se baandh deta hai.”

Court mein baithe scholars ek saath bolne lage.

“Impossible!”

“Soul-binding toh extinct magic hai!”

“Is tarah ka covenant toh sirf legends mein milta hai!”

Thalorien ne apni staff zameen par maari.

DHUM!

Ek halki magical lehar poore court mein phail gayi, aur sabhi awaazein tham gayin.

“Main jaanta hoon ki yeh sunne mein asambhav lagta hai. Main khud is relic par research kar raha hoon, lekin maine kabhi nahi socha tha ki iska yeh hissa ab bhi active hoga.”

Usne hawa mein tairte dono dhaagon ki taraf ishara kiya.

“Yeh dono dhaage ek hi covenant ke do hisse hain. Ek Princess Rivona ko represent karta hai. Doosra Love ko.”

Rivona ki aankhon mein gussa ubhar aaya.

“Seedha jawaab dijiye, Scholar. Iska matlab kya hai?”

Thalorien ne gehri saans li.

“Iska matlab hai ki tum dono ke beech ka bond sirf marriage status tak seemit nahi hai.”

Woh ek pal ke liye ruka.

“Tum dono ki zindagiyaan is covenant ke saath judi hui hain.”

Court mein sannata chha gaya.

Thalorien ne aage kaha:

“Main abhi tak yeh nahi bata sakta ki yeh covenant kisne banaya, kis maksad se banaya, ya tum dono ko hi kyun chuna gaya. Lekin iska ek protective binding clause hai. Is clause ka kaam dono pakshon ko ek-doosre se alag hone se rokna hai.”

Rivona ne apni kursi ke armrest ko pakad liya.

“Rokna? Kis tarah?”

Thalorien ne uski aankhon mein dekha.

“Iska jawaab dene se pehle main ek aur test karunga.”

Usne relic ke saamne apna haath uthaya aur ek chhota sa magical symbol banaya.

Hawa mein dono dhaage achanak kas gaye.

Ek pal ke liye laal dhaaga kaanp utha.

Aur usi pal tumhari taraf se nikalti roshni mein ek chhoti si darar ubhar aayi.

Rivona ke sunehre dhaage mein bhi wahi darar dikhayi di.

Thalorien ne turant spell rok diya.

Uske chehre par ab koi sandeh nahi tha.

Sirf gehri chinta thi.

“Ab main samajh gaya.”

Chief Arbiter khade ho gaye. “Kya samajh gaye?”

Thalorien ne poore court ko sambodhit kiya.

“Is covenant ka niyam bahut kathor hai. Agar ek paksh doosre ko chhodkar bond se baahar nikalne ki koshish karta hai, toh covenant us separation ko rokne ke liye us paksh ki jeevan-shakti ko khud hi nasht karna shuru kar deta hai.”

Rivona ka chehra safed pad gaya.

“Kya…?”

Thalorien ki awaaz bhaari ho gayi.

“Agar Love, Rivona ko chhodkar chala gaya, toh Rivona ki jaan khatre mein pad jaayegi. Aur agar Rivona, Love ko chhodkar chali gayi, toh Love ki jaan khatre mein pad jaayegi.”

Kuch pal tak court mein koi awaaz nahi hui.

Phir ek saath kai log bol uthe.

“Yeh paagalpan hai!”

“Isse todne ka koi tareeka toh hoga!”

“Scholar, aapko poori tarah yakeen hai?”

Thalorien ne apna sir jhukaya.

“Main abhi yeh daawa nahi kar raha ki mujhe is covenant ka har raaz pata hai. Lekin jo pattern maine dekha hai, woh kisi aam magical marriage ka nahi hai. Yeh life-binding clause hai. Aur jab tak hum iska source aur mechanism nahi samajh lete, kisi bhi paksh ko bond se alag karne ki koshish jaanleva ho sakti hai.”

King Aldric dheere-dheere apni jagah se uthe.

Unki awaaz mein woh imperial authority thi jisne sadiyon se Dragon Empire ko sambhala tha.

“Arch-Scholar, kya iska matlab hai ki High Court bond ko annul nahi kar sakta aur dono paksh apni marzi se alag bhi nahi ho sakte?”

Thalorien ne unki taraf dekha.

“Filhaal, Your Majesty, main yahi keh sakta hoon: bond ko todne ki koshish karna dono ke liye jaanleva ho sakta hai. Main kisi ko yeh salah nahi doonga ki woh ise aazmaakar dekhe.”

Aldric ki aankhein ek pal ke liye band ho gayin.

Woh apni beti ko dekh rahe the.

Aur pehli baar, unke paas use dene ke liye koi aasaan raasta nahi tha.

### 6. Rivona ka Tootta Hua Sabr

Rivona apni jagah se uthi.

“Matlab ab main sirf isliye is aadmi se bandhi rahungi kyunki ek relic ne aisa tay kar diya?”

Thalorien ne shaanti se kaha, “Princess, main samajh sakta hoon ki—”

“Nahi. Aap nahi samajh sakte!”

Uski awaaz court ke gumbad se takraakar goonj uthi.

“Pehle mujhe bataya gaya ki main ise annul nahi kar sakti. Ab aap keh rahe hain ki agar main ise chhod doon, toh main mar sakti hoon?”

Uski ungliyon ke aas-paas dragon magic ki lehrain uthne lagi.

“Toh yeh shaadi nahi hai! Yeh qaid hai!”

Usne ek pal ke liye tumhari taraf dekha.

Uski amber aankhon mein ab sirf gussa nahi tha. Wahan darr bhi tha—aisa darr jo use aur zyada gusse mein badal raha tha.

“Main is aadmi ko jaanti tak nahi. Mujhe nahi pata yeh kaun hai, yeh kahan se aaya hai, ya iske aane se pehle iski zindagi kaisi thi. Aur ab meri zindagi aur meri maut dono iske saath jod di gayi hain?”

Woh tumse nazar hata kar Thalorien ki taraf mudi.

“Isse theek kijiye.”

Thalorien ne apni staff ko kas kar pakda.

“Main koshish karunga. Lekin main aapse jhootha vaada nahi kar sakta.”

“Mujhe koshish nahi chahiye. Mujhe hal chahiye!”

Rivona ki magic se court ke kuch lamps ek saath bujh gaye.

King Aldric ne turant kaha, “Rivona, bas.”

Lekin is baar usne apne pita ki baat ka turant jawaab nahi diya.

Woh kuch pal tak chup rahi. Phir usne dheere se apni mutthi kholi.

“Main ise apni zindagi ka faisla nahi karne doongi.”

Usne tumhari taraf dekha.

“Sun rahe ho? Main tumse shaadi karna nahi chahti. Main tumhare saath rehna nahi chahti. Aur agar yeh relic humein zabardasti baandh raha hai, toh main iska sach pata lagaungi.”

Uski awaaz aur thandi ho gayi.

“Lekin jab tak iska hal nahi milta, tum meri zindagi mein har cheez ko aur mushkil bana rahe ho.”

Woh tumhein dosh de rahi thi—kam-se-kam uska gussa tumhari taraf mud gaya tha.

Chahe tumne is bond ko kabhi maanga hi na ho.

Chahe tum bhi usi qaid mein phanse hue the.

### 7. Court ka Agla Faisla

Chief Arbiter ne apni seat se khade hokar court ko shaant kiya.

“High Court yeh ghoshna karta hai ki Ancient Marriage Registry ka bond abhi tak valid hai, aur Arch-Scholar Thalorien ke prarambhik nishkarsh ke anusaar, ismein ek life-binding clause bhi maujood hai.”

Unhone dono pakshon ki taraf dekha.

“Jab tak is covenant ki prakriti aur iske prabhaav poori tarah samajh nahi liye jaate, kisi bhi paksh ko bond todne ke liye koi kadam uthane ki salah nahi di jaati.”

Unhone agla aadesh sunaaya.

“Arch-Scholar Thalorien ko relic ki jaanch ke liye royal authority di jaati hai. King Aldric ke aadesh se dono paksh palace mein surakshit rahenge, lekin unhein bandi nahi maana jaayega. Kisi bhi paksh ko bina kaaran doosre ke kareeb rehne ke liye majboor nahi kiya jaayega. Dono ki alag rehne ki ichchha ka sammaan kiya jaayega, jab tak ki isse koi jaanleva magical prabhaav na ho.”

Rivona ne turant kaha, “Main alag chambers mein rahungi.”

Aldric ne sir hilaaya.

“Tumhein alag chambers milenge.”

Phir Chief Arbiter ne tumhari taraf dekha.

“Love, aapka apni zindagi par adhikaar ab bhi maayne rakhta hai. Lekin ab hum jaante hain ki yeh maamla sirf marriage law ka nahi, balki ek sambhavit life-binding covenant ka hai. Court aapki baat aur aapki suraksha ko nazarandaaz nahi karega.”

Thalorien ne ek baar phir relic ko dekha.

Uski nazar ab un patterns par thi jo kuch pal pehle ubhre the.

Unmein se ek symbol ab bhi bahut halki roshni de raha tha.

Woh symbol usne pehle kabhi nahi dekha tha.

Aur uske andar ek aisa sanket tha jo uski research ke kisi bhi purane record se mel nahi khata tha.

Thalorien ne apne mann mein socha:

Yeh covenant sirf do zindagiyon ko baandhne ke liye nahi banaya gaya tha. Iske peeche koi aur wajah bhi hai.

Lekin usne yeh baat abhi kisi ko nahi bataayi.

Kyunki uske paas abhi saboot nahi tha.

Aur woh nahi chahta tha ki court mein pehle se hi bhadki hui sthiti aur khatarnak ho jaaye.
