# Does the Dragon Princess Really Hate Me?
## Chapter 1 — Act 11: Teen Laws Ek Dhaaga Aur Ek Behosh Kisaan

Imperial High Court of Aurelthys — Subah

Court ke beech Ancient Marriage Registry se nikalti sunehri roshni ab bhi hawa mein tair rahi thi. Do patle magical dhaage sabki aankhon ke saamne chamak rahe the—ek Rivona ki taraf aur doosra tumhari taraf. Dono ek hi pracheen symbol se jude hue the.

Abhi kuch pal pehle Arch-Scholar Thalorien Vael'Sharen ne bataya tha ki is bond ko todne ki koshish jaanleva ho sakti hai.

Agar Love, Rivona ko chhodkar chala gaya, toh Rivona ki jaan ja sakti thi. Aur agar Rivona, Love ko chhodkar chali gayi, toh Love ki jaan ja sakti thi.

Lekin court mein kisi ne abhi tak tumse theek se nahi poochha tha ki tum kya chahte ho.

Chief Arbiter apne judges ke saath kuch salaah kar rahe the. King Aldric gambhir khamoshi mein baithe the. Rivona apne gusse ko dabaane ki koshish kar rahi thi.

Aur phir tumhari awaaz poore court mein goonj uthi.

### 1. “Koi mujhse bhi toh poochho!”

“YEH KYA MAZAAK HAI?!”

Tumhari achanak uthi awaaz se court mein baithe kai nobles uchhal pade. Ek buzurg dragon noble ke haath se uska chashma tak girte-girte bacha.

Tumhari taraf sabki nazar mud gayi.

“Koi mujhse toh poochh lo! Main kya chahta hoon?! Har baat ka faisla aap log hi kar rahe ho! Koi mujhse poochh bhi raha hai ki main kya chahta hoon?!”

Tumhari awaaz mein bechaini thi, gussa tha, aur uske neeche ek gehri bebasi thi.

“Sab mujhse itne gusse kyun hain? Maine kya kiya hai?! Main jaanbujhkar thodi na yeh sab kar raha tha! Main toh bas khaana khaane aaya tha! Mujhe toh yeh bhi nahi pata tha ki yahan koi relic hai, koi Registry hai, koi shaadi hai!”

Court mein sannata chha gaya.

Tumne apni baat jaari rakhi.

“Main apne ghar jaana chahta hoon! Mujhe apni zindagi jeeni hai! Main koi prince nahi hoon, koi noble nahi hoon! Main ek aam kisaan hoon! Meri bhi ek zindagi hai!”

Rivona ki aankhein tum par tik gayin. Uske chehre par ab bhi gussa tha, lekin is baar woh beech mein nahi boli.

Shayad pehli baar use ehsaas ho raha tha ki tum sirf uske liye ek pareshani nahi the. Tum bhi is court ke faislon se utne hi pareshaan the jitni woh khud.

Lekin tum abhi rukne wale nahi the.

Tumhari nazar seedhe us vishaal elf par gayi jo relic ke paas khada tha.

“Aur aap! Haan, aap, Elf Scholar!”

Thalorien ne apni chaandi jaisi bhaunhein halki si uthayin.

Tumne uski taraf ungli se ishara kiya.

“Aap keh rahe hain ki yeh relic bahut puraana hai, iske rules bahut puraane hain, aur yeh bond toot nahi sakta. Lekin ek baat bataiye—yeh relic aapse bhi zyada puraana toh nahi hai na, Scholar?”

Court mein kuch logon ne hairani se ek-doosre ko dekha.

Thalorien ne shaant swar mein jawaab diya, “Main ek hazaar saal se adhik samay se jeevit hoon, lekin is relic ki asal rachna ka samay abhi tak nishchit nahi hai. Main aapko is baare mein jhootha jawaab nahi doonga.”

“Achha! Toh aapko bhi sab kuch nahi pata!”

Ek dragon noble ne naaraaz hokar kaha, “Tum Arch-Scholar se is tarah baat karne ki himmat—”

Chief Arbiter ne turant haath uthaya.

“Use bolne dijiye.”

Thalorien ne bhi us noble ko shaant rehne ka ishara kiya.

Tumne apni baat aage badhaayi.

“Phir mujhe ek aur baat bataiye! Yeh Three Laws kisne banaye the?”

Chief Arbiter ne jawaab diya, “Legendary Hero ne, Dragon Wars ke ant mein.”

“Bilkul! Toh phir jab woh Three Laws logon ko apni zindagi jeene ka adhikaar dete hain, jab woh kehte hain ki kisi ke saath uski jaati ya janm ke kaaran bhedbhaav nahi hona chahiye, toh phir yeh kya ho raha hai?!”

Tumhari awaaz aur tez ho gayi.

“Ek taraf aap kehte hain ki meri zindagi meri hai, mujhe apni marzi se jeene ka adhikaar hai. Doosri taraf ek relic mere liye faisla kar raha hai ki main kisse shaadi karunga aur kahan rahunga!”

King Aldric ne apni kursi ke armrest par haath rakha. Unka chehra gambhir tha. Woh tumhari baat sun rahe the, beech mein rok nahi rahe the.

Tumne relic ki taraf dekha.

“Aur agar yeh relic Three Laws se bana hai, ya unke adhikaar ke andar banaya gaya hai, toh phir yeh mujhe hi kyun baandh raha hai? Laws toh logon ko azaadi dene ke liye the na? Yeh kaunsa kanoon hai jo meri marzi ke bina meri zindagi ka faisla kar raha hai?”

Arch-Scholar Thalorien ki aankhon mein ek pal ke liye gehri dilchaspi chamki.

Yeh sawaal usne bhi apni research mein kabhi poori tarah hal nahi kiya tha.

Usne dheere se kaha, “Yeh ek aisa prashn hai jiska jawaab mujhe bhi khojna hoga.”

“Khojna hoga? Toh phir aapko pata hi nahi hai!”

Tumne dono haath utha diye.

“Wah! Kya baat hai! Jis cheez ke kaaran meri shaadi ho gayi, uske baare mein kisi ko kuch pata hi nahi hai!”

Court ke pichhle hisse mein baithe ek young legal clerk ne apna chehra neeche kar liya. Woh apni hansi rokne ki koshish kar raha tha.

Uske bagal mein baithe senior clerk ne use kohni maari.

“Shaant raho!”

Lekin agla sawaal sunkar uske chehre par bhi ajeeb si shakal aa gayi.

### 2. “Yeh Kaunsa Pakwaas Rule Hai?!”

Tumne un dono magical dhaagon ko dekha jo ab bhi tumhare aur Rivona ke beech hawa mein chamak rahe the.

Phir tumne avishvaas mein apna sir hilaaya.

“Ek minute, ek minute… iska matlab kya hai?”

Tumne pehle Rivona ki taraf dekha, phir khud ki taraf ishara kiya.

“Agar main Princess Rivona ko chhodkar chala gaya, toh woh mar jaayegi. Aur agar Princess mujhe chhodkar chali gayi, toh main mar jaaunga?!”

Tum kuch pal chup rahe.

Phir tumhare chehre par aisi hairani aayi jaise kisi ne tumhein duniya ka sabse bewakoof rule suna diya ho.

“YEH KAUNSA PAKWAAS RULE HAI?! KISNE LIKHA HAI YEH RULE?!”

Court mein kuch nobles ke chehre par khula avishvaas tha.

Tumne apne seene par haath rakha.

“Matlab agar main apne ghar chala gaya, toh Princess mar jaayegi? Aur agar Princess ne mujhe chhod diya, toh main mar jaaunga?”

Tumne Rivona ki taraf dekha, phir ek bechain si hansi ke saath kaha:

“Arre, yeh toh mujhe chhod hi degi! Mere toh waise bhi lagne waale hain na?!”

Court mein ek pal ke liye aisa sannata chha gaya ki fireplace ki door se aati halki awaaz tak sunayi dene lagi.

Phir pichhle hisse mein baithe wahi young clerk apna munh haath se dhak kar khansne laga.

Uske senior ne use itni zor se kohni maari ki woh seedha baith gaya.

Lady Nymera Ashveil ne apne hoth kas liye. Uske chehre par ek pal ke liye muskaan aayi, lekin Rivona ki taraf dekhte hi woh dobara gambhir ho gayi.

Lady Seraphine Drakemore bilkul khush nahi thi.

“Yeh High Court hai, koi bazaar nahi,” usne fusfusaakar kaha.

Lekin Rivona—

Rivona ne tumhari taraf aise dekha jaise use samajh hi nahi aa raha ho ki tumhein daante, tumhari baat ka jawaab de, ya is poori situation par khud cheekhne lage.

“Tumhe lagta hai mujhe yeh sab mazedaar lag raha hai?” usne teekhe swar mein kaha.

Uske golden horns ke paas halki roshni chamki.

“Main bhi marna nahi chahti, samjhe? Aur main tumhein maarne ki koshish bhi nahi kar rahi!”

Usne apni baat par zor diya, jaise use khud ko bhi yaad dilana pad raha ho ki woh tumhari dushman nahi banna chahti thi—kam-se-kam is wajah se nahi.

“Lekin mujhe yeh bhi pasand nahi ki meri zindagi ek aise insaan ke saath baandh di gayi hai jise main kal tak jaanti bhi nahi thi!”

Uski awaaz ka aakhri hissa halka sa kaanpa.

Gusse se.

Darr se.

Ya dono se.

Thalorien ne beech mein kaha, “Princess, covenant ka prabhaav dono pakshon par hai. Iska matlab yeh nahi ki aap dono mein se koi ek doosre ko chhodna chahta hai. Iska matlab sirf yeh hai ki bond ko samjhe bina use todne ki koshish nahi karni chahiye.”

“Main jaanti hoon!” Rivona ne turant kaha.

Phir usne nazar hata li.

Court mein tumhari baat ka asar ab bhi maujood tha. Tumne jo sawaal uthaya tha, woh sirf gusse mein nikla hua mazaak nahi tha.

Agar Three Laws ka maksad logon ki freedom aur dignity ki raksha karna tha, toh unhi Laws se jude kisi relic ko kisi insaan ki marzi ke khilaaf uski zindagi baandhne ka adhikaar kaise mila?

Chief Arbiter ne apne notes mein yeh prashn darj kiya.

“Arch-Scholar,” unhone kaha, “court chahta hai ki aap is virodhaabhaas ki jaanch karein. Kya yeh relic Three Laws ko enforce karta hai, ya iska koi alag mechanism hai?”

Thalorien ne sir hilaaya.

“Main iski jaanch karunga.”

Lekin uski nazar ab bhi tum par thi.

Kyunki jab tum bol rahe the, usne tumhare andar kuch aisa dekha tha jo baaki kisi ne notice nahi kiya.

Aur woh cheez abhi bhi uske liye ek rahasya thi.

### 3. Tumhari Awaaz Badal Jaati Hai

Tumhare haath kaanpne lage.

Pehle sirf ungliyon mein halki si larzish thi. Phir woh larzish kalaiyon tak pahunchi. Tumhare pairon mein bhi ek ajeeb si kamzori utarne lagi.

Tumhari awaaz mein ek badlaav aaya.

Gussa ab bhi tha, lekin uske neeche ek gehri, bhaari gunj ubhar rahi thi—jaise tumhare gale se nikalne wali awaaz ke peeche koi doosri awaaz bhi maujood ho.

Tumhari aankhon mein ek ajeeb sa rang chamka.

Na poori tarah sunehra.

Na laal.

Balki dono ke beech ka ek aisa shade, jise Thalorien ne apni lambi zindagi mein bahut kam dekha tha.

Usne turant apni staff ko mazbooti se pakad liya.

Yeh kya…?

Uski nazar tumhari aankhon par jam gayi.

Court ke baaki log abhi bhi tumhari baaton aur relic ke dhaagon par dhyaan de rahe the. Kisi ne tumhare andar ho rahe is badlaav ko theek se nahi dekha.

Sirf Thalorien ne dekha.

Usne ek kadam tumhari taraf badhaaya, lekin tumse doori banaye rakhi.

“Love…” usne bahut dheemi awaaz mein kaha. “Aapki tabiyat theek hai?”

Tumhare haath aur pair ab zyada kaanp rahe the.

Rivona ne bhi yeh dekha.

Uske chehre par gussa ek pal ke liye hat gaya.

“Yeh kya ho raha hai?”

King Aldric turant apni seat se uth khade hue.

“Royal healer ko bulao!”

Garrick Thornvale, jo court ke darwaaze ke paas khada tha, ek kadam aage badha. Lekin kisi ne abhi tumhein chhua nahi tha.

Thalorien ne apni staff ke crystal ko tumhari taraf kiya. Crystal se ek halki neeli roshni nikli, jo tumhare aas-paas ki hawa ko chhoone lagi.

Uska chehra aur gambhir ho gaya.

Yeh aam magical exhaustion nahi hai.

Usne apne mann mein socha.

Aur yeh Registry ke dhaage se bhi alag hai… ya shayad usse juda hua hai?

Woh abhi nishchit nahi tha.

Tumhare andar kya ho raha tha, yeh use nahi pata tha. Lekin usne tumhari aankhon mein jo rang dekha tha, usne uske andar ek purani yaad jaga di thi—ek aisi yaad jise woh abhi court ke saamne kholna nahi chahta tha.

### 4. “Yeh Dhaaga Tod Do!”

Tumhari nazar phir hawa mein tairte us magical dhaage par gayi.

Woh ab pehle se zyada chamak raha tha.

Tumhare haath kaanp rahe the. Tumhare pair tumhara wazan sambhaalne mein mushkil mehsoos kar rahe the. Tumhari awaaz ab bhi gusse se bhari thi.

Aur phir tumne kaha—

“Yeh dhaaga tod do!”

Thalorien ne turant jawaab diya, “Love, ruk jaiye! Humein abhi iski prakriti—”

Lekin uske shabd poore hone se pehle hi hawa mein kuch badal gaya.

Dono dhaagon mein ek saath tez halchal hui.

WHOOOM!

Relic se nikli sunehri roshni ekdum phail gayi. Court ke beech bana pracheen symbol ghoomne laga. Dhaage aise kaanpe jaise kisi ne unhein dono taraf se kheench diya ho.

Rivona ke gale se ek chhoti si saans nikli.

Usne apne seene par haath rakh liya.

“Ah—!”

Uske shareer mein koi chot nazar nahi aayi, lekin usne ek pal ke liye apna santulan kho diya. Nymera turant uske paas pahunchi aur use sambhaalne ke liye haath badhaaya.

“Rivona!”

King Aldric ne zor se kaha, “Sab peeche hato!”

Court ke magical guards ne apni weapons nikaal li, lekin Thalorien ne unhein turant rok diya.

“Koi relic ko mat chhoona!”

Uski awaaz pehli baar itni tez hui thi.

Usne apni staff ko pedestal ki taraf uthaya aur ek defensive spell cast kiya. Neeli roshni ne relic ko gher liya.

Lekin sunehri dhaage shaant nahi hue.

Woh kuch aur pal tak kaanpte rahe.

Phir achanak—

CHHNNNG!

Ek gehri, ghanti jaisi awaaz poore court mein goonj uthi.

Tumhare aas-paas ki roshni ek pal ke liye bahut tez ho gayi.

Aur phir sab kuch shaant padne laga.

Tumhare haath kaanpna band nahi hue the. Tumhare pairon mein ab itni kamzori thi ki tumhara shareer tumhara saath nahi de pa raha tha.

Court ki awaazein door hoti chali gayin.

Thalorien ne tumhari taraf haath badhaaya.

“Love!”

Lekin isse pehle ki woh tum tak pahunch paata, tumhari aankhon ke saamne andhera chha gaya.

Tum sabke saamne behosh hokar zameen par gir pade.

Garrick sabse pehle tumhari taraf dauda.

“Doctor! Jaldi!”

King Aldric ne apne guards ko aadesh diya ki court ke darwaaze band kar diye jaayen aur kisi ko bina anumati andar-bahar na jaane diya jaaye.

Rivona ab bhi Nymera ke sahare khadi thi. Uski aankhein tum par tiki hui thi.

“Yeh… meri wajah se hua?” usne bahut dheemi awaaz mein poochha.

Nymera ne turant jawaab nahi diya.

Thalorien tumhare paas ghutnon ke bal baitha, lekin usne tumhein chhoone se pehle apni staff ke crystal ko tumhare kareeb rakha.

Uski aankhon mein ab woh pehle wali academic curiosity nahi thi.

Woh sach mein chintit tha.

Usne tumhare aas-paas ki magical energy ko mehsoos karne ki koshish ki.

Aur usi pal, uski aankhon mein ek aisa bhaav ubhra jo court mein kisi aur ne nahi dekha.

Pehchaan.

Lekin kis cheez ki pehchaan?

Woh abhi kuch nahi bola.

Usne bas apni staff ko aur mazbooti se pakda aur royal healer ke aane ka intezaar karne laga.
