# Does the Dragon Princess Really Hate Me?
## Chapter 2 — Act 5: The Maid with the Kitchen Set

Location: Silverpine Wood ka kinaara, Blackvein Wilds ki boundary, gehri raat

Clearing me dhool abhi tak poori tarah baithi nahi thi.

Kyle behosh pada tha. Rivona, Seraphine, Vaelora, Elowen aur Nymera uski body ko gherkar khadi thin. Unki saansen irregular thi. Unki talwaren chipped, cracked aur dented thin. Do ghante ki ladai ne unke haathon ka kaanpna badha diya tha. Unke boots geeli mitti me dhans rahe the.

Aur unke saamne woh monster ab pehle se bada khada tha.

Usne apna sir peeche kiya. Uske seene ke andar laal roshni tez hui. Gardan ke neeche neeli roshni pagalpan me dhadakne lagi. Usne apne dono agle panje hawa me uthaye. Kaanton ke beech se neeli sparks nikalne lagi.

Vaelora: "Woh kuch bada karne wala hai! Formation tight karo!"

Monster ne apna Super Attack chhoda.

Ek bhayanak shockwave ke saath uske panjon se kaanton aur energy ki aandhi nikli. Setup saaf tha — dono panje upar, seena phoolta hua. Execution ek pal me hua — hawa phati, pedon ki chhaal udi. Impact ne zameen par daraaren daal din. Consequence ke taur par poora clearing dhool aur jalti mitti ki boo me doob gaya.

Rivona: "Roko ise! Ek saath!"

Is baar dar ne unhe rokne nahi diya. Seraphine, Vaelora, Elowen aur Nymera — chaaron ne apni bachi hui magical energy ko apne weapons me channel kiya. Rivona ne bhi apni talwar me apni dragon magic daali. Paanch weapons ek saath aage badhe.

Weapons monster ke devastating attack se takraaye.

Takraav pichhli baar se bhi bada tha. Ek aur flash of light aayi. Massive shockwave chaaron taraf door tak gayi. Ped jhuk gaye. Dhool aur smoke ka cloud poore clearing par chha gaya. Kaanon me ghanti bajne lagi.

Jab roshni aur shockwave guzre, unke saare weapons completely shattered ho chuke the.

Rivona ki talwar ka aaga hissa tootkar mitti me gira. Seraphine ki talwar do tukdon me bati thi. Vaelora ka dagger handle se toot gaya tha. Elowen ki short-blade mudkar bekaar ho gayi thi. Nymera ki bhaari talwar beech se chatak gayi thi.

Aur Rivona ke chaaron friends zameen par gir gaye — exhaustion se. Woh behosh nahi the. Bas itne exhausted the ki khade nahi reh sakte the. Nymera bhi side me gir gayi, uski tooti talwar uske haath se chhoot gayi.

Rivona akeli khadi reh gayi. Usne monster ko dekha. Time uske liye thoda slow ho gaya.

Monster growl karke uski taraf badhne laga. Uske har kadam par zameen kaanpti.

Rivona mann me sochne lagi:

"Kya yahi mera end hai? Kya main yahin marne wali hoon? Kya aise meri maut hogi? Achanak yeh life mujhse chheeni ja rahi hai. Meri friend khatre me hai, Kyle behosh pada hai, aur main marne wali hoon. Yeh mere saath kya ho raha hai? Mere saath aisa kyun ho raha hai? Pehle meri life barbaad ho gayi, woh zabardasti ka bond, Ancient Marriage Registry [woh pracheen relic jo shaadi ke bandhan ko pehchanta hai] ne mujhe ek anjaan human se baandh diya, maine apni poori life kingdom ke liye taiyaar ki thi, aur ab maut."

Phir usne socha:

"Koi parwah nahi. Meri life pehle hi barbaad ho chuki hai. Agar main mar gayi toh Love free ho jaayega. Wo bechara bhi meri kismat ke kaaran phas gaya, wo to bas ek aam kisan hai jo kheti se mohabbat karta hai, sahi hai agar wo free ho gaya to hum me se kisi ko to achchi zindagi milegi. Shayad yahi theek hai. Main apne friends se help nahi maangungi. Main khud kuch karungi. Ek magic hai, ek highly destructive suicide self-destructive magic, agar maine use perform kiya to main is monster ko maar sakti hoon. Mere friends bach jaayenge. Haan, yahi karungi."

Usne apne mann me spell recite karna shuru kiya.

Usne Dragon God ko pukara.

"Dragon God... Destruction aapka daas hai... main us daas ko pukarti hoon... mere saamne jo khada hai use mita do..."

Paas hi Vaelora ka dhyaan Rivona ki taraf gaya. Rivona ke chehre ka rang, uski kaanpti ungliyan aur uske honthon ki halchal dekhkar Vaelora ko bhaan ho gaya ki Rivona kuch khatarnak, suicidal type magic karne wali hai.

Vaelora: "Nahi! Tum kya kar rahi ho? Ruko!"

Uski awaaz sunkar baaki dost bhi Rivona ki taraf dekhne lage. Seraphine, Elowen aur Nymera ko bhi samajh aa gaya ki Rivona kya kar rahi hai. Sab ek saath chillaaye:

Sab: "Tum ye kya kar rahi ho?!"

Woh spell complete karne wali thi ki monster ne apna claw seedha uske saamne rakh diya. Woh use maarne ke liye taiyaar tha. Panja uske chehre se bas kuch haath door tha. Hawa me uski garmi mehsoos ho rahi thi.

Aur tabhi bijli se bhi tez kuch Rivona ki taraf udta hua aaya. Woh seedha uske saamne land hua.

Ek lightning-like beam of light uske guzarne ke saath monster ke haath ko cheer gayi.

Ek huge roar aur impact hua. Pedon se pattiyan jhad gayin. Dhool udi.

Monster ka haath katkar uske shareer se alag ho gaya.

Sab shocked the. Kyle ne zameen par lete hue thodi consciousness regain ki. Usne dekha jo hua tha.

Sabke saamne ek woman khadi thi. Woh half-dwarf aur half-human thi, aur maid uniform pehni hui thi. Uski back sabki taraf thi. Usne apna face thoda side me ghumaaya aur kaha:

Maera: "Dragon race ke log bhi hain, alag-alag race ke bhi hain. Dragon race hone ke baad bhi tumse is monster se nahi lada gaya. Pathetic."

Sabne dekha uske haathon me kya tha — Rivona aur uske doston ne saaf dekha. Uske paas kitchen set tha aur uske haathon me ek haath me butter knife aur doosre haath me fork tha aur wo apne haathon se X formation banayi hui thi.

Usne peeche se side-eye diya aur kaha:

Maera: "Koi baat nahi, ab main aa gayi hoon, sab theek ho jayega."

Woh monster ko dekhne lagi aur analyze karne lagi.

Maera: "Ye kya cheez hai? Lagta hai ise kisi ne banaya hai. Koi baat nahi, chhote. Dikhne me thoda ajeeb lagta hai. Pata nahi kaise describe karun ise. Hedgehog ki tarah, ya bhediye ki tarah, ya ek chuhe ki tarah? Samajh nahi aa raha hai. Ajeeb sa monster hai."

Maera: "Koi baat nahi, zyada der tak nahi rahega."

Uska casual andaaz us danger se bilkul contrast tha jisne Rivona aur uske friends ko do ghante tak toda tha.

Monster loudly growl kiya. Sabne uski awaaz suni.

Vaelora aur baakiyon ne upar dekha. Unhone ek royal skyship dekhi.

Love, Ilyra aur Garrick royal skyship se neeche dekh rahe the. Yeh dekhkar Rivona ko hope ki ek nayi feeling aayi.

Rivona ne bas ek ladki ko maid uniform me dekha tha. Usne mann me socha: ye kaun hai? Ek maid jungle ke beechon-beech kya kar rahi hai?

Tab maid ne monster ki taraf dekha aur kaha:

Maera: "Chalo dekhein is monster mein kitna dum hai."

Usne kitchen set ka box khola. Samay uske liye achanak se kaafi ho gaya kyunki wo bahut tez thi. Hawa me hi kitchen set se nikle alag-alag tarah ke chammach, utensils, fork, knives, usne apni ungliyon se tak-tak-tak-tak karti hui itni tezi se maara ki goliyon ki raftaar se bhi tez, sound ki raftaar se bhi tez. Socho kitni tez raftaar se, bahut hi tez raftaar se, itna ki sunik-boom jaisi awaazen aayin aur knives aage badhin.

Har utensil apne alag target par gaya. Pehla knife seedha haath me laga aur bada boom blast hua, maans aur kaante hawa me bikhar gaye. Doosra sar se takraaya aur phir boom, monster ka sir peeche jhatka kha gaya. Teesra pair me ghusa aur blast ne neeche ki mitti uda di. Uske baad shareer par ek ke baad ek — boom, boom, boom, boom — jaise dum-dum-dum-dum ki dhamak poore clearing me goonj gayi. Poora attack itni tezi se hua ki sabki aankhen uska peecha nahi kar paayin, samajhne me struggle hui ki woh kya dekh rahe the.

Agar background music hota toh maza aa jaata — usne toh dekhte hi dekhte uske body ke tukde-tukde karke poora scenery hi phaad diya.

Rivona, uske saare friends aur Kyle ne jo dekha usse shocked the.

Rivona ne mann me socha:

"Ye kaun hai jo is ladai ko ek khel ki tarah khel rahi hai, jaise ye koi sport ho. Masti me, koi tension nahi, koi dar nahi, kuch bhi nahi."

Is beech, Nymera side me leti hui thi aur usne joke kiya:

Nymera: "Kya ye kitchen set se lad rahi hai?"

Tabhi sabko asli baat samajh aayi — uske haathon me jo knives, forks aur utensils dikhe the, woh sajawat nahi the, woh unhin se lad rahi thi. Sab Nymera ki taraf mude.

Unchi, disbelieving voice me unhone respond kiya:

Sab ek saath: "Hain?"

Phir unhone wapas us maid ko dekha.

Sab ek saath: "Hain?"

Woh believe nahi kar pa rahe the jo dekh rahe the.

Maid ne apne attack ka result dekha aur kaha:

Maera: "Oh, ye to shuru hone se pehle hi khatam ho gayi."

Usne phenki hui knives abhi hawa me thin. Woh zameen par girne wali thin, kyunki unka kaam ho chuka tha. Lekin woh itni tez move hui ki knives ke hilne se pehle, zameen par girne se pehle, usne unhe hawa me hi pakad liya. Usne unhe saaf kiya, ponchha, aur kitchen set ke box me wapas usi tarah arrange karke rakh diya jaise pehle thi.

Yeh dekhkar Rivona, uske friends aur Kyle ne socha, yeh kya ho raha hai, yeh kaise ho sakta hai. Sabke chehre par wahi hairaani thi, aur isme ek ajeeb comedy thi — itni badi ladai ke beech koi apne bartan saaf karke box me rakh raha tha.

Maera: "Kuch zyada hi jaldi nahi ho gaya ye?"

Haath saaf karte hue usne add kiya:

Maera: "Kitchen set ganda ho gaya."

Uska casual attitude us terrifying battle se bilkul contrast tha jo Rivona aur uske friends ne abhi jheli thi.

Lekin situation settle hone se pehle monster ke pieces phir se uthne lage.

Rivona ne dekha ki tukde hawa me kaanp rahe hain aur ek-doosre ki taraf khinch rahe hain. Uska chehra safed pad gaya. Woh dar ke maare chillayi:

Rivona: "Monster ke tukdon ko ek saath mat judne dena! Roko unhe!"

Woh ek-doosre ki taraf move karne lage aur judne lage. Pieces reconnect hokar ek naya, larger monster banane lage. Monster ka kata hua haath bhi wapas jud gaya.

Maid ne yeh hote hue dekha aur kaha:

Maera: "Achha, toh ye baat hai. Lagta hai ye ladai lambi chalne wali hai."
