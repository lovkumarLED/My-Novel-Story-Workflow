# Does the Dragon Princess Really Hate Me?
## Chapter 1 — Act 6: The Man Who Never Arrived

Aurelthys Imperial Palace — Royal Reception Hall, late morning.

Baarish abhi bhi palace ki unchi khidkiyon par dastak de rahi thi. Bahar, Aurelthys ki sadkein geeli pattharon se chamak rahi thi, aur aasman mein garajti bijli kabhi-kabhi royal hall ke sunahre pillars ko roshan kar deti thi.

Hall ke andar sab kuch shahi tha—crystal chandeliers, dragon emperors ke purane portraits, aur deewaron par latke hue battle standards. Lekin aaj us royal grandeur ke beech ek ajeeb sa tension tha.

Ek taraf Dragon Empire ki Crown Princess Rivona Vaeltharis thi.

Doosri taraf ek aisa insaan tha jise kuch ghante pehle tak kisi ne apni zindagi ka hissa banane ke baare mein socha bhi nahi tha.

Aur un dono ke beech khada tha Ancient Marriage Registry ka woh faisla, jisne poore royal court ko hila diya tha.

Tumhare saamne rakhe khane aur paani ko dekhkar tumne King Aldric ka shukriya ada kiya. Tum khana khane lage, aur phir apni kahani sunate hue unse kaha ki tumne kuch galat nahi kiya.

King ne tumhari baat ko beech mein nahi kaata.

Unka chehra ab bhi serious tha, lekin woh tumhe us tarah nahi dekh rahe the jaise koi apradhi ko dekhta hai. Woh tumhe samajhne ki koshish kar rahe the.

Jab tumne apni maa ki sunayi hui purani kahaniyon ka zikr kiya—Legendary Hero, Dragon Wars aur unki sena ke baare mein—toh King Aldric ki nazar tum par tik gayi.

King Aldric: "Tumhari maa ne tumhe hamare purane itihaas ke baare mein bataya tha?"

Unhone khidki ke paar dekha. Kuch der tak woh khamosh rahe, jaise tumhari baat ne unke andar koi purani yaad jaga di ho.

King Aldric: "Log aksar Legendary Hero ko yaad rakhte hain. Uski taakat, uski jeet, uske kaarnamon ko. Lekin bahut kam log un logon ko yaad rakhte hain jo uske saath khade hokar lade the."

Woh tumhari taraf mude.

King Aldric: "Mere pita unhi logon mein se ek the. Woh Legendary Hero ke commanders mein se ek the. Unhone Dragon Wars ke dauran uske saath milkar ladai ki thi."

Unki awaaz mein apne pita ke liye gehra samman tha.

King Aldric: "Aur main? Main us waqt ek junior military rank par tha. Main us sena ka ek chhota sa officer tha. Mujhe apni jagah banane ke liye bahut kuch seekhna pada tha."

Unke hothon par halki si muskaan aayi.

King Aldric: "Mere pita mujhe aksar kehte the ki battlefield par tumhara surname tumhe pehchaan de sakta hai, lekin tumhari asli keemat tumhare faisle tay karte hain."

Phir unki muskaan dheere se mit gayi.

King Aldric: "Woh yuddh bahut purana hai. Lekin kuch yaadein waqt ke saath dhundhli nahi hoti."

Ek royal guard ne apni nazar neeche kar li. Shayad usne pehli baar Emperor ko apne parivaar ke ateet ke baare mein itni khulkar baat karte suna tha.

King Aldric ne tumhare saamne rakhe paani ke jug ki taraf dekha.

King Aldric: "Tumne kaha ki tum ek kisaan ho. Tumhari fasal baarish mein barbaad ho gayi, tumhare paas khane ke paise nahi bache, aur tum pandrah din safar karke yahan pahunche."

Woh ek pal ruke.

King Aldric: "Main abhi tumhari poori kahani ki sachchai nahi jaan sakta. Lekin main tumhari gareebi ko tumhare khilaaf saboot nahi banaunga."

Phir unka expression dobara gambhir ho gaya.

King Aldric: "Lekin Registry ka maamla alag hai. Usne meri beti ko tumhare saath ek marriage bond mein baandh diya hai. Iska asar sirf tum dono par nahi, balki poore Empire aur hamare doosre kingdoms ke saath rishton par bhi padega."

Unhone apne haath peeche baandh liye.

King Aldric: "Isliye main tumhari baat bhi sununga aur sach ki jaanch bhi karwaunga. Dono cheezein saath-saath hongi."

Unki awaaz mein ek Emperor ka faisla tha—na andha bharosa, na bina saboot saza.

Ek naam, jo Rivona ke zehan se nikla nahi tha
Royal hall se kuch door, Princess Rivona apne private chambers ki taraf ja rahi thi. Uske saath uski chaar dost—Seraphine, Vaelora, Elowen aur Nymera—maujood thi.

Seraphine: "Rivona, humein is marriage bond ko jald se jald challenge karna hoga. Main kisi aam insaan ko tumhari zindagi aur Dragon Empire ke succession ke beech aane nahi doongi."

Vaelora: "Pehle Registry ke record ki jaanch hogi. Agar humne galat kadam uthaya, toh High Court mein hamari position kamzor ho sakti hai."

Nymera: "Aur agar woh sach mein kuch nahi jaanta? Uski haalat dekhkar toh woh kisi royal conspiracy ka hissa nahi lag raha tha."

Seraphine: "Tum har kisi ko benefit of the doubt kyun deti ho?"

Nymera: "Kyunki main faisla karne se pehle sach jaan lena pasand karti hoon."

Rivona ne unki baaton par koi jawab nahi diya.

Uska gussa Love par tha, lekin uski pareshani ka ek aur hissa tha jiske baare mein woh filhaal kisi se baat nahi karna chahti thi.

Ek naam baar-baar uske zehan mein aa raha tha.

Kyle Dravenor.

Kyle ek purane aur prabhavshaali dragon noble family se tha. Rivona aur Kyle ne saath mein Imperial Academy se graduation kiya tha. Woh ek exceptional warrior tha—disciplined, confident, aur battlefield par apne opponents ke liye khatarnak.

Lekin Rivona ke liye woh sirf ek talented warrior nahi tha.

Woh un chand logon mein se ek tha jinke saath Rivona apni royal position ko kuch der ke liye bhool sakti thi. Academy ke dino mein dono ne training ki thi, arguments kiye the, aur kai baar ek-doosre ki madad bhi ki thi.

Rivona uske saath comfortable thi.

Shayad zaroorat se thodi zyada.

Yeh love nahi tha—kam se kam Rivona ne kabhi ise us naam se nahi dekha tha. Lekin uske dil mein Kyle ke liye ek khaas jagah zaroor thi.

Aur aaj, jab uski zindagi ka sabse bada crisis shuru hua tha, Kyle wahan nahi tha.

Usne socha tha ki Kyle aaj ke royal engagement ceremony mein zaroor aayega.

Woh usse milna chahti thi. Shayad usse kuch kehna chahti thi. Shayad bas uski maujoodgi chahti thi, kyunki Kyle ke saamne use har baat explain karne ki zaroorat nahi padti thi.

Lekin woh aaya hi nahi.

Aur ab ek anjaan insaan ko uska pati kaha ja raha tha.

Elowen: "Rivona? Tum khamosh kyun ho?"

Rivona ne palak jhapki, jaise apne khayalon se wapas aayi ho.

Rivona: "Main soch rahi hoon ki Kyle kahan hai."

Chaaron dost ek pal ke liye khamosh ho gayin.

Nymera ne apni bhaunhein uthayin.

Nymera: "Kyle? Tumhe lagta hai use aaj ki ceremony mein hona chahiye tha?"

Rivona: "Woh jaanta tha ki aaj ek important day hai."

Uski awaaz mein gussa tha, lekin woh gussa Love ke liye wale gusse se alag tha. Ismein disappointment ka ek halka sa rang bhi tha.

Vaelora: "Ho sakta hai use kisi military assignment par bheja gaya ho. Kyle apni duties ko seriously leta hai."

Rivona: "Mujhe pata hai woh apni duties ko seriously leta hai."

Usne turant jawab diya, phir ek pal ke liye ruk gayi.

Rivona: "Lekin woh mujhe ek message toh bhej sakta tha."

Seraphine ne apne baazu baandh liye.

Seraphine: "Abhi Kyle ke baare mein sochne ka waqt nahi hai. Humein us human ke baare mein faisla karna hai."

Rivona ka chehra phir se sakht ho gaya.

Rivona: "Main jaanti hoon."

Usne apne chambers ke darwaze ki taraf dekha.

Rivona: "Main us aadmi par bharosa nahi karti. Main nahi jaanti ki Registry ne use kyun chuna. Aur jab tak mujhe jawaab nahi milta, woh meri nazar ke saamne rahega."

Woh ek pal ruki, phir dheere se boli—

Rivona: "Lekin Kyle ko dhoondho. Main jaanna chahti hoon ki woh aaj kahan tha."

Nymera ne sir hila diya.

Nymera: "Main pata karwa deti hoon."

Jab Rivona apne chambers mein gayi, uske chehre par wahi royal composure tha jo usne saalon ki training se seekha tha.

Lekin uske zehan mein ab do alag sawaal the.

Ek: Ancient Marriage Registry ne Love ko kyun chuna?

Doosra: Kyle aaj aaya kyun nahi?

Aur abhi use inmein se kisi ka bhi jawaab nahi pata tha.
