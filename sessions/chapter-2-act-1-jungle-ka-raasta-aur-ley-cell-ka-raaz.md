# Does the Dragon Princess Really Hate Me?
## Chapter 2 — Act 1: Jungle Ka Raasta Aur Ley-Cell Ka Raaz

Location: Blackvein Wilds; Drakon Court se Silverpine Wood ki raah

Shaam ka aakhri ujala Blackvein Wilds ke upar phaili ghani shaakhon mein atak kar dheere-dheere bujh raha tha. Royal skyship ko jungle ke andar seedha le jaana mumkin nahi tha; pilot ne ek chhoti si khuli jagah par utara, jahan se aage ka safar paidal tha. Neeche utarte hi Garrick ne apne aas-paas nazar daudayi. Har taraf itne bade ped the ki unke tanon ke beech poori patrol chhup sakti thi.

Usne route-chart dobara dekha, phir jungle ko. Uske chehre par woh expression tha jo tab aata hai jab map sach bol raha ho aur aadmi chahta ho ki woh jhooth bol raha hota.

Garrick: "Yeh Blackvein Wilds hain? Empire ka sabse khatarnak jungle?"

Ilyra ne apni satchel kandhe par theek ki aur neeche ghani jhaadiyon ko dekhne lagi. Kisi taraf se patton ki sar-sarahat aayi. Woh turant seedhi ho gayi.

Ilyra: "Yahan toh royal patrol bhi bina poori team aur healer ke aane se mana karti hai. Love yahan rehta hai? Sach mein?"

Garrick ne door ke andhere mein dekha, jaise koi monster bas unki baat khatam hone ka intezaar kar raha ho.

Garrick: "Son, tumne pehle kaha tha ghar jungle mein hai. Tumne yeh nahi kaha tha ki woh *is* jungle mein hai. Yahan toh raaste ka signboard bhi apni jaan bachane ke liye bhaag jaaye."

Ilyra ne aas-paas ki ghaas mein panje ke bade nishaan dekhe. Usne ek pal ke liye unhe medical case samajhne ki koshish ki, phir apni satchel aur mazbooti se pakad li.

Ilyra: "Aur tum keh rahe the ki tum yahan akela rehte aaye ho? Tumhe kabhi darr nahi laga? Khaana, paani, dawa—sab kaise sambhalte ho?"

Love: "Yeh jagah itni khatarnak nahi hai. Yahan achchhe jaanwar rehte hain. Kabhi-kabhi chhote-mote monsters aa jaate hain, toh hum dekh lete hain. Bas."

Kuch pal tak jungle mein sirf door ke kisi pakshi ki awaaz goonjti rahi.

Garrick ne dheere se Ilyra ki taraf dekha.

Garrick: "Chhote-mote monsters. Is jungle mein."

Ilyra: "Main bhi wahi soch rahi hoon. Shayad hum dono ko apni medical aur military definitions dobara likhni chahiye."

Garrick ne apni talwar ke qabze par haath rakha. Uske chehre ki fikr mein ab uljhan bhi shamil thi.

Garrick: "Agar yeh chhote hain, toh main bade dekhna nahi chahta."

Jhaadiyon mein phir khar-khar hui. Garrick ne talwar aadhi kheench li. Ilyra ne apni satchel ko aise seene se lagaya jaise uske andar dawa ke saath ek chhoti deewar bhi ho. Ek gol-matol, singh-wala jaanwar patton ke beech se nikla, unhe dekha, aur bina kisi dilchaspi ke ghaas chabane laga.

Ilyra ne saans chhodi.

Ilyra: "Achchha. Yeh un achchhe jaanwaron mein se hai?"

Garrick: "Agar yeh humein khaane ka iraada nahi rakhta, toh aaj ke liye main ise achchha hi maanunga."

Jaanwar ne ghaas chabana jaari rakha, jaise usne dono ki rai ko bekaar paaya ho. Garrick ne talwar wapas rakh di—bahut dheere, aur bina jaanwar ki taraf peeth kiye.

Raasta aur gehra hota gaya, bade ped ek-doosre ke upar jhuk kar aasman ko chhupa rahe the. Kuch door chalne ke baad Ilyra rukti, zameen, jhaadiyon aur aas-paas ki har cheez ko dhyaan se dekhti; phir aage badh jaati. Garrick bhi har aahat par chaukas ho raha tha. Lekin uske andar ek aur bechaini dheere-dheere badh rahi thi—use baar-baar lagta jaise koi unhe dekh raha ho.

Woh apni trained senses se kuch pakadne ki koshish karta: koi movement, saans, panjon ki aahat, ya hawa mein kisi creature ki boo. Kuch nahi. Phir bhi usse yakeen sa hota ja raha tha ki jungle mein unke aas-paas aise monsters maujood the jinhe usne zindagi mein kabhi dekha tak nahi tha—aur unki maujoodgi mehsoos karne ke bawajood woh unka pata nahi laga pa raha tha.

Unmein se kisi ne hamla nahi kiya. Jhaadiyon ke paar kabhi koi bhaari saaya hila, phir gayab ho gaya; patton ki halki si khar-kharahat unke saath-saath chalti rahi, magar koi saamne nahi aaya. Garrick ko laga jaise is jungle ke creatures ne is hisse ko kisi bahut shaktishaali hasti ki territory maan liya ho. Shayad woh us hasti ko naraaz nahi karna chahte the—chahe woh koi jaanwar ho ya insaan. Yeh sirf uska andaaza tha, lekin har kadam par uska ehsaas mazboot hota gaya.

Phir pedon ke beech ek ghar nazar aaya—kaale, daraawne jungle ke beech ek sundar sa farmhouse. Ghar ke aas-paas jalti lights ne andhere ko peeche dhakel diya tha; unki roshni mein jagah utni khaufnaak nahi lag rahi thi. Paas hi do theek-thaak khet the, bahut bade nahi, aur unki fasal ki haalat kharaab thi. Baarish ne crop barbaad kar di thi—Love ne safar mein pehle hi unhe yeh bataya tha.

Garrick ne ghar ko dekha, phir uske peeche uthti kaali shaakhon ko.

Garrick: "Ghar toh sach mein bahut khoobsurat hai, son. Bas location dekh kar lagta hai padosiyon se milne se pehle unka threat assessment karna padega."

Ilyra ne roshni mein farmhouse aur dono kheton ko dekha. Uske chehre par ab bhi jungle ko lekar chinta thi, lekin ghar ke aas-paas ki garmahat dekh kar uski aankhon ki sakhti kuch kam hui.

Ilyra: "Kam se kam yahan lights hain. Aur khet bhi. Main abhi tak yeh tay nahi kar paayi hoon ki yeh tasalli ki baat hai ya is baat ka saboot ki tum is jagah ke saath kitne comfortable ho, Love."

Tabhi saamne ka gate khulne ki awaaz aayi. Bahar ek khubsurat, mature-looking aurat nikli—aadhi dwarf aur aadhi insaan. Uski nazar Love par padi, aur uske chehre par intezaar ki saari thakan ek pal mein khushi ban gayi.

Garrick aur Ilyra ek-doosre ko dekhne hi wale the ki aurat achanak unki taraf daud padi. Uski raftaar itni tez thi ki Garrick ko pehle sirf ek dhundhla saaya nazar aaya. Yeh kisi aam insaan ki daud nahi thi; uski nazar usse pakadne se pehle hi woh unke paas aa pahunchi.

Garrick ka haath reflex se talwar ki taraf gaya, phir woh wahi ruk gaya. Aurat seedhi Love tak pahunchi aur usse mazbooti se gale laga liya.

Maera: "Love, tum aa gaye? Itni der kahan reh gaye the? Tumhe pata hai, maine kitna intezaar kiya? Tumne mujhe kitna dara diya tha! Agar tum nahi aate, toh main aaj khud tumhe dhoondhne nikalne wali thi. Uff, agli baar se aisa mat karna—pata nahi kya ho jaata! Maine pehle hi kaha tha ki main tumhare saath hoon, lekin nahi, tumhe toh wahin bhaag kar jaana tha. Achha hua tum waqt par aa gaye. Zara aur der karte, toh main khud tumhe dhoondhne nikal padti."

Garrick aur Ilyra dono sakte mein usse dekhte rahe. Love ne unhe kabhi nahi bataya tha ki jis shakhs ko woh ghar se saath le jaane aaya hai, woh ek aurat hai—woh bhi aisi jo palak jhapakte hi gate se un tak pahunch sakti thi.

Garrick ne farmhouse ki taraf dekha, phir Maera ko, phir apne haath ko jo abhi tak talwar ke paas tha.

Garrick: "Ghar khoobsurat hai. Maalik bhi... kaafi tezi se mehmaan-nawaazi karti hain."

Ilyra ne apni satchel theek ki, abhi bhi Maera ki raftaar ko samajhne ki koshish mein.

Ilyra: "Love, tumne bas kaha tha ki kisi ko saath lana hai. Tum yeh batana bhool gaye the ki woh hamare paas daud kar aayengi—ya shayad humein pata chalne se pehle hi aa jaayengi."

Maera ne Love ko gale lagaye hue hi dono saathiyon ki taraf dekha. Uski muskurahat garamjoshi bhari thi, jaise unka hairaan reh jaana bilkul samajh mein aata ho.

Maera: "Aap dono bhi andar aaiye. Jungle mein bahar khade rehkar baat karna achchha idea nahi hai."

Garrick ne ek baar phir andhere pedon ki taraf dekha, phir jalti lights aur khule gate ki taraf. Ghar ke andar se aati roshni ne jungle ko dehleez ke paar rok rakha tha—kam se kam filhaal.

Usi shaam, Drakon Court ke doosre hisse mein, Rivona apne safar ke liye taiyaar thi, lekin abhi rawana nahi hui thi. Uski chaar saheliyan—Seraphine, Vaelora, Elowen aur Nymera—uske saath thin. Thalorien ne unhe Silverpine Wood tak pahunchne ke liye ek pehla route-map diya tha; final report abhi aani baaki thi. Silverpine Blackvein Wilds se milta tha, aur Kyle usi kam-khatarnak jungle ke kinaare par lad raha tha. Abhi kisi ko nahi pata tha ki woh kis haalat mein tha.

Rivona ne apni cloak ki clasp band ki aur saamne khade pilot se route ki aakhri tasdeeq li.

Rivona: "Hum seedhe Silverpine ke outer trail par jaayenge. Kyle ke paas pahunchne se pehle koi akeli aage nahi badhegi."

Seraphine ne apne gloves kas kar pehne.

Seraphine: "Samajh gayi. Hum saath chalengi, aur bina jaanch ke kisi ley-cell [ek chhota magical cell jo leyline ki urja jama karke devices ko chalata hai] ke paas nahi jaayengi."

Silverpine ki report mein us damaged ley-cell ka zikr tha. Aise cells aam taur par skyship aur magitech ko power dete the; is baar kisi ne us urja ke saath chhedchhad ki thi, aur aas-paas ke jeevon ka rawayya badal gaya tha.

Vaelora ne map par ungli rakh kar boundary dikhayi.

Vaelora: "Silverpine khud Blackvein jitna khatarnak nahi. Lekin dono junglon ke milne wali jagah par hum ise aam patrol nahi samajh sakte."

Elowen ne apne satchel mein rakhe notes ko jaancha.

Elowen: "Pehle Kyle ko dhoondhenge. Phir us cell aur badle hue jeevon ke nishaan dekhenge. Humein pata hona chahiye ki wahan kya hua—magar usse pehle use surakshit karna hai."

Nymera ne apni mazboot ungliyan belt par kasin aur seedhi khadi ho gayi.

Nymera: "Toh phir taiyaar rehte hain. Kyle ko wapas laayenge, aur jo bhi saamne aaya, hum sab milkar sambhalengi."

Rivona ne apne doston ki taraf dekha. Uski bechaini ab bhi thi, lekin ab woh akeli nahi thi. King ne uski rawangi ko manzoori di thi, magar scout-party ki poori jaanch ke baad. Isliye chaaron saheliyan taiyaar rahin, route-map aur skyship unke paas the, aur unhe bas scouts ke lautne ya unka sandesh aane ka intezaar karna tha. Silverpine ke kinaare par Kyle aur ek anjaana khatra unka intezaar kar rahe the.

## Haalat ka chhota sa recap

Love, Garrick aur Ilyra Blackvein Wilds ke andar Love ke farmhouse tak pahunch gaye. Garrick ko laga ki jungle ke kuch anjaan aur bahut shaktishaali monsters unhe dekh rahe hain, lekin woh is ilaake ko kisi aur ki territory samajh kar saamne nahi aaye. Love ne apne saathiyon ki ghabrahat ka jawab apne andaaz mein diya—yahan achchhe jaanwar rehte hain aur bas kabhi-kabhi chhote-mote monsters aa jaate hain. Ghar aur baarish se kharaab hue dono kheton ke paas Maera gate se nikal kar Love ko gale lagane daudi; uski raftaar dekh kar Garrick aur Ilyra hairaan reh gaye. Isi shaam Rivona aur uski chaar saheliyan Silverpine ke liye taiyaar rahin, jabki Thalorien ki scout-party unse pehle nikal chuki thi. Kyle Blackvein ki boundary ke paas tha; unhe abhi uski haalat ya jungle ke khatre ka poora pata nahi tha.
