# Does the Dragon Princess Really Hate Me?
## Chapter 1 — Act 1: Woh Shaadi Jo Kabhi Nahi Honi Chahiye Thi

Legendary human hero ko Dragon Wars khatam kiye hue 723 saal beet chuke hain.
Duniya ab badal chuki hai.
Humans, elves ke saath trade karte hain. Demons international conferences mein participate karte hain. Dragon knights human merchants ko mountains ke dangerous raaston se escort karte hain. Magic-powered carriages magnificent cities ki white marble streets par chalti hain, aur enormous castles aasman ko chhoote hue nazar aate hain.
Hero ke banaye hue Three Laws har kingdom ki capital mein monuments par likhe hue hain.
Sadiyon se peace bani hui hai.
Lekin peace ka matlab yeh nahi ki har koi ek doosre ko sach mein equal maanta hai.
Draconic Empire of Vyrathis mein aaj bhi dragon blood kisi ki social position decide karta hai. Ancient noble families military, treasury aur imperial court ko control karti hain. Ek dragon ka hukm armies ko move karwa sakta hai, lekin ek commoner ki awaaz palace ke gate tak pahunchne ke liye bhi struggle kar sakti hai.
Aur imperial capital, Aurelthys, mein ek aisi royal engagement ceremony ki preparations chal rahi hain jo poori generation mein sabse important maani ja rahi hai.
Princess Rivona Vaeltharis, dragon empire ki crown princess, aaj apni shaadi ke future ko lekar announcement karne wali hai.
Teen powerful men is din ka intezaar kar rahe hain.
Prince Vekar Draven, dragon noble.
Sir John Ashford, human diplomat.
Aur Crown Prince Charles VII, sabse powerful human warrior aur duniya ke sabse bade human kingdom ka heir.
Yeh marriage dragons aur humans ke beech political balance maintain karne ke liye plan ki gayi hai.
Kisi ko nahi pata ki ek poor human is poori situation mein enter karne wala hai.
Kisi ko nahi pata ki ancient laws khud royal family ke khilaaf khadi ho jayengi.
Aur kisi ko nahi pata ki woh human asal mein kya chhupa raha hai.

Chapter 1 — Imperial Gates Par Ek Gareeb Insaan

Aurelthys, Imperial Capital. Subah 9:17 AM.
Baarish capital ki white marble streets par gir rahi hai.
Noble families ke crests wali carriages palace gates se guzar rahi hain. Black-and-gold armor pehne dragon knights apne enormous wings fold karke khade hain. Palace ke towers ke upar kai dragons badalon ke beech chakkar laga rahe hain.
Eastern entrance ke paas ek young human khada hai.
Love.
Uske kapde purane hain. Shoes par mitti lagi hui hai. Uske paas na koi noble crest hai, na expensive weapon, aur na hi wealth ka koi visible sign.
Guards ko woh ek ordinary poor traveler lagta hai jo galti se city ke sabse restricted area mein aa gaya ho.
Ek guard uske paas aata hai.
Imperial Guard: "Oye, tum! Yeh Imperial Palace ka entrance hai. Batao, yahan kya kaam hai tumhara?"
Love jawab de, usse pehle hi main courtyard se achanak shor sunai deta hai.
Ek magnificent royal carriage aa chuki hai.
Uska darwaza khulta hai.
Princess Rivona bahar nikalti hai.

Uske silver-white baal black-and-gold royal gown ke upar gir rahe hain. Baalon ke beech se do elegant golden horns nikle hue hain, aur uske temples par halki golden scales chamak rahi hain.
Uski amber eyes mein ek ruler ka confidence hai, jo paida hi rule karne ke liye hui ho.
Uske peeche uski chaar closest friends chal rahi hain.
Seraphine crimson silk mein bilkul perfect lag rahi hai. Vaelora ke chehre par hamesha ki tarah cold expression hai. Elowen apne haath mein leather-bound documents lekar chal rahi hai, jabki Nymera casually apna ek haath sword ke hilt par rakhe hue hai.
Rivona pehle se hi irritated hai.
Seraphine: "Your Highness, human delegation aa chuki hai. Prince Charles Hall of Crowns mein aapka wait kar rahe hain."
Rivona: "Mujhe pata hai, Seraphine."
Vaelora: "Toh phir humein chalna chahiye. Court ek ghante se wait kar rahi hai."
Nymera: "Aur court paanch minute aur wait kar legi."
Elowen apni smile chhupane ki koshish karti hai.
Rivona Nymera ko warning bhari nazar se dekhti hai.
Phir uski nazar eastern gate ke paas ho rahi disturbance par padti hai.
Ek poor human.
Aisi jagah khada hai jahan sirf invited guests ko aane ki permission hai.
Rivona: "Woh human imperial entrance ke andar kya kar raha hai?"
Guard turant bow karta hai.
Imperial Guard: "Maafi chahta hoon, Your Highness. Main ise yahan se hatane hi wala tha."
Rivona gate ki taraf chalti hai.
Uske aas-paas khade log chup ho jaate hain.
Uski presence hi guards ko seedha khada hone par majboor kar deti hai.
Woh Love se kuch kadam door rukti hai aur use head-to-toe dekhti hai.
Rivona: "Tumhe dekhkar lagta hai tumne Imperial Palace ko charity house samajh liya hai."
Seraphine dheere se hans deti hai.
Seraphine: "Shayad yeh leftovers maangne aaya hai."
Elowen apni friend ko displeased expression ke saath dekhti hai.
Elowen: "Seraphine. Woh bhi ek insaan hai, koi joke nahi."
Seraphine: "Maine toh sirf ek observation di hai."
Rivona unki baaton ko ignore karti hai. Uski nazar Love par hi tiki rehti hai.
Rivona: "Yeh imperial residence hai. Yahan entry restricted hai. Batao tum kaun ho, yahan kyun aaye ho, aur tumhe kya chahiye?"
Love jawab dene hi wala hota hai ki palace ki seedhiyon se ek deep voice sunai deti hai.
King Aldric: "Rivona. Pehle is insaan ko bolne do."
Dragon King marble staircase se neeche utarta hai.

King Aldric Vaeltharis Love ko aisi nazar se dekhta hai jise samajhna mushkil hai.
Ek pal ke liye uski nazar Love ke chehre par ruk jaati hai.
Kuch badalta hai.
Bahut subtle. Almost invisible.
Uski ungliyan golden cane par thodi tight ho jaati hain.
Phir woh expression gayab ho jaata hai.
King Aldric: "Young man, tum ek important din par yahan aaye ho. Aaj poora empire ek royal ceremony ke liye jama hua hai. Agar tumhare paas mere gates par khade hone ki koi legitimate wajah hai, toh bina dare batao."
Rivona apne father ki taraf mudti hai.
Rivona: "Father, aap sach mein ek unidentified commoner ko palace mein aane dene ke baare mein soch rahe hain?"

Rivona: "Woh human hai. Aur ek poor human. Na uske paas noble title hai, na family name, aur na hi court se koi apparent connection."

Rivona ka jaw tight ho jaata hai.
Rivona: "Mujhe laws pata hain."

Nymera ab Love ko curiosity ke saath dekh rahi hai. Elowen relieved lagti hai ki king ne interfere kiya. Seraphine openly annoyed hai.
