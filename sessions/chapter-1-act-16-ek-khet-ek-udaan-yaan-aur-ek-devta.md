# Does the Dragon Princess Really Hate Me?
## Chapter 1 — Act 16: Ek Khet, Ek Udaan-Yaan, Aur Ek Devta

Location: Royal Healer Wing, Drakon Court

Love ne ek lambi saans li aur use dheere se chhod diya. Thakaan ab bhi uske chehre par thi, lekin uski awaaz pehle se zyada sambhali hui thi.

Love: "Theek hai. Main in sab cheezon mein aapki madad karunga. Lekin usse pehle mujhe kuch chahiye. Agar mujhe yahan lambe waqt tak rehna hai, toh zyada nahi—bas kuch zaroori cheezen."

King Aldric ne apni nazar us par tikaye rakhi. Na haan kaha, na inkaar. Darbar mein koi bhi shart sunne se pehle uska poora hona zaroori hota tha.

Love: "Pehli baat, mujhe apne ghar jaana hai—jitni jaldi ho sake. Wahan koi hai, jise mujhe yahan lana hai. Aur mujhe apne maa-baap ki tasveer aur meri kuch cheezen bhi wapas chahiye."

Garrick ke sakht chehre par pehchaani hui narmi ubhri.

Garrick: "Main tumhare saath chalunga, son. Tumhe apne ghar tak akela nahi jaana padega."

Love ne gardan seedhi ki, jaise abhi apni sabse zaroori darkhwast yaad aayi ho.

Love: "Aur mujhe rajmahal mein ek khet bhi chahiye. Main kisaan hoon. Thodi-bahut farming skill toh aani chahiye na?"

Kamre mein ek chhoti si khamoshi chha gayi.

Rivona ki bhaunh halki si uthi.

Rivona: "Tumne apni sharton mein ek khet bhi jod diya hai."

Love: "Agar mujhe mahal mein rehna hai, toh main bas khaali baitha nahi reh sakta. Aur agar main kisaan hoon, toh mujhe kuch ugaane toh aana chahiye."

Thalorien ne apni ungli se hawa mein ek chhota sa ghera banaya, jaise mahal ke nakshon par koi jagah naap raha ho.

Thalorien: "Outer kitchen courtyard ke paas ek zameen ka tukda hai. Abhi us par sirf pathar aur ek bahut udaas sa guldasta ugta hai."

Rivona: "Woh guldasta udaas nahi hai. Woh bas tumhari tarah kisi se baat nahi karta."

Garrick ne apni moochh ke neeche muskurahat chhupayi. Aldric ne use dekha, aur woh turant phir se seedha ho gaya.

Aldric: "Us zameen ka istemaal kiya ja sakta hai. Chhota khet hoga—pehle. Agar tum mitti ko mahal ke protocol se zyada samajh paaye, toh us par baad mein baat karenge."

Ilyra ne apne haath baandh liye.

Ilyra: "Aur ghar jaane ki baat tab hogi jab main tumhe safar ke liye theek samjhoongi. Tum abhi-abhi behoshi se uthe ho."

Love ki maang ka teesra hissa hawa mein latak gaya—aur iss baar Thalorien ne turant jawaab diya.

Thalorien: "Leyline Docks par ek royal skyship taiyaar ki ja sakti hai. Magitech se chalegi, udne wale jaanwar ki zaroorat nahi. Route seedha rakhenge, aur pilot ko ley-cells par hadd se zyada zor nahi dena hoga."

Love: "Koi mere saath jaayega? Mujhe sach mein wahan jaana hi padega. Wapas aakar aap jo kahenge, main woh karunga."

Garrick: "Maine kaha na, main chalunga. Tum ghar ka raasta bata dena; main baaki dekh loonga."

Rivona ne Love ko dekha. Uski awaaz ab bhi thandi thi, magar usmein pehle jaisa seedha tiraskaar nahi tha.

Rivona: "Apne maa-baap ki tasveer aur apni cheezen laane ke liye tumhe ijazat maangne ki zaroorat nahi honi chahiye thi. Woh tumhari hain."

Aldric ne dheere se sir hilaaya.

Aldric: "Garrick tumhare saath jaayega. Thalorien royal skyship aur route ka intazaam karega. Ilyra jab takraar band karke tumhe safar ke laayak samjhegi, tab hum rawangi ka waqt tay karenge."

Ilyra ne uski taraf dekha.

Ilyra: "Maine abhi tak safar ki ijazat nahi di."

Aldric: "Maine kaha—jab tum ijazat dogi."

Ilyra: "Achha. Toh pehli baar aaj kisi ne meri baat suni. Isse bhi record kar lena, Scholar."

Thalorien ne bahut sanjeedgi se sir jhuka diya, jaise waqai us baat ko royal archive mein darj karne wala ho.

Usi pal kamre ke healing crystals bujh gaye.

Ek saans jitne andhere ke baad, unmein se ek crystal ke andar sunehri roshni jal uthi. Roshni hawa mein phaili, mudti gayi, aur ek vishaal celestial dragon ki aakriti ban gayi. Uske pankhon ke kinaaron par aag ki patli lakeerein chamak rahi thin.

King Aldric turant khade ho gaye. Rivona ki ungliyan uski baahon se khul gayin. Thalorien ne apna sir jhuka diya. Garrick ne apna haath talwar ke paas rakha, lekin use bahar nahi nikala.

Vyrathion: "Kyle wapas aa gaya hai."

Us awaaz ne kamre ki deewaron ko nahi, seedha har dil ko chhoo liya.

Vyrathion ki drishti ek pal ke liye crystal se nikal kar un sab par ruki. Uske baad roshni ke andar ek aur drishya ubhar aaya—ghane jungle ke beech ek tooti hui ley-cell, jiske chaaron taraf mitti ka rang kaala pad chuka tha.

Uske paas ek ajeeb jeev khada tha. Uski peeth par lakdi jaise kaante the, magar uske pairon ke neeche ki zameen par dhatu ke tukde aur pighli hui neeli roshni chamak rahi thi. Woh kisi pehchaani hui mythical beast jaisa nahi tha. Uski saans ke saath uske badan ke andar laal roshni phadphadaati thi.

Drishya ke door waale hisse mein ek akela yoddha pedon ke beech se guzra—ek pal ke liye uska chehra dikhai diya.

Kyle.

Rivona ka chehra ekdam safed pad gaya.

Vyrathion: "Iron Covenant ne ley-cells ke saath chhedchhad karke jungle ke jeevon ko badalna shuru kar diya hai. Kyle usi jungle ke kinaare par hai. Woh zinda hai. Lekin uske aas-paas ka khatra bhi zinda hai."

Roshni mein kaale nishaan ek pal ko aur tez chamke—phir drishya bujh gaya.

Healing crystals dobara neeli roshni se jal uthe. Kamre mein kuch pal tak koi nahi bola.

Aldric ne pehle Rivona ki taraf dekha, phir Thalorien ki taraf.

Aldric: "Kyle ki khabar ki tasdeeq hogi. Iron Covenant ke baare mein bhi. Garrick, ghar ke safar ka intazaam jaari rakho. Love ne apni zaroorat saaf bata di hai."

Rivona ne apni nazar us bujhte hue crystal par jama rakhi.

Rivona: "Agar Kyle waqai wapas aa gaya hai, toh jungle jaana padega. Lekin hum bina taiyaari ke kisi ko wahan nahi bhejenge."

Thalorien: "Main us ley-cell ke nishaan ko pehchaanne ki koshish karunga. Aur main yeh bhi pata lagaunga ki woh jeev prakritik roop se badla, ya kisi ne use jaan-boojhkar banaya."

Garrick ne Love ki taraf dekha.

Garrick: "Pehle tum ghar jaoge, son. Tasveer aur jo kuch tumhe chahiye, woh le aayenge. Aur jis shakhs ko tum yahan lana chahte ho, use bhi. Jungle ka faisla tumhare lautne ke baad hoga."

Ilyra ne paani ka glass phir se Love ke kareeb kar diya.

Ilyra: "Aur usse pehle tum aaram karoge. Khet tumhara intezaar kar lega."

Rivona: "Agar woh udaas guldasta tab tak bach gaya toh."
