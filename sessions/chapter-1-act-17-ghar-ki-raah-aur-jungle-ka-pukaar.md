# Does the Dragon Princess Really Hate Me?
## Chapter 1 — Act 17: Ghar Ki Raah Aur Jungle Ka Pukaar

Location: Royal Healer Wing and Leyline Docks, Drakon Court

Vyrathion ke sandesh ke baad healer wing mein khamoshi kuch der tak jami rahi. Rivona ki aankhen ab bhi us roshni ke khaali ho chuke crystal par tiki thin. Uske chehre ka rang ud gaya tha; ungliyan muthi mein band hoti gayin, jaise bechaini ko bas isi tarah qaboo kiya ja sakta ho.

Phir woh apne pita ki taraf mudi.

Rivona: "Mujhe Kyle chahiye. Abhi. Humein kisi ko turant uske paas bhejna hoga—use support chahiye, aur agar woh jungle ke kinaare akela hai toh har pal qeemti hai."

Aldric ka chehra sakht raha, lekin uski nazar mein chhupi chinta ko chhupana aasaan nahi tha. Vyrathion ka sandesh hukm nahi tha; phir bhi usse nazarandaaz karna samajhdari nahi hoti.

Aldric: "Hum ek trained party bhejenge. Lekin tum bina jaanch aur taiyaari ke seedha us jungle mein nahi jaogi."

Rivona ka jabda tan gaya. Uski awaaz mein rajkumari ka hukm tha, magar uske neeche ek dost ka darr saaf sunayi deta tha.

Rivona: "Main use wahan akela nahi chhodungi. Main khud jaungi. Main warrior hoon, Father. Aur mere dost mere saath honge."

Seraphine ne bina der kiye apna haath seene par rakha.

Seraphine: "Main tumhare saath chalungi. Kyle ko dhoondhna hai, toh hum use dhoondh kar rahengi."

Vaelora ne apne gloves theek kiye, uski nazar ab pehle se zyada gambhir thi.

Vaelora: "Main bhi. Hum use wapas laayenge—lekin is baar bina taiyaari ke kisi ley-cell ke paas nahi jayenge."

Elowen ne Rivona ke kandhe ke paas aa kar dheere se sir hilaaya.

Elowen: "Main saath chalungi. Pehle Kyle ko surakshit karenge, phir us jeev aur ley-cell ke nishaan ko samajhne ki koshish karenge. Humein dono baaton ka jawaab chahiye."

Nymera ne apni mazboot ungliyan belt par kasin aur seedhi khadi ho gayi.

Nymera: "Main bhi. Jungle mein jo bhi saamne aaya, hum shoulder to shoulder rahengi."

Thalorien ne jaldi se ek parchment khola. Uski ungli Vyrathion ke dikhaye hue jungle ke hisse par ruki.

Thalorien: "Main route aur ley-cell ke nishaan ki jaanch karunga. Ek scout party pehle ja sakti hai, lekin Princess ki team ko bhi rawangi se pehle poori jaankari milni chahiye."

Aldric ne gehri saans li. Beti ko khatre ki taraf jaane dena use manzoor nahi tha; use rokna bhi mumkin nahi lag raha tha. Kuch pal ke baad usne apni mohar wali anguthi mez par rakh di.

Aldric: "Tumhari rawangi ko meri manzoori hai—tumhare chaaron saathiyon ke saath. Thalorien pehle raasta aur scout report taiyaar karega. Tumhari party ek saath rawana hogi, aur bina zaroorat koi alag nahi hoga. Main tumhe khatre mein bhejne se khush nahi hoon, Rivona. Magar tumhari baat sahi hai: Kyle ko madad chahiye ho sakti hai."

Rivona ne ek chhota, bechain sa sir hilaaya. Use tasalli nahi mili, par ab use intezaar karne ke bajay kuch karna tha. Chaaron dost uske paas the; unke chehron par alag-alag qisam ki chinta thi, magar kisi ne peeche hatne ki baat nahi ki.

Shaam tak healer wing ka mahaul badal gaya. Roshni khidkiyon se dheemi pad chuki thi, aur Ilyra ne Love ki tabiyat dobara jaanch kar apne instruments sametne shuru kiye. Safar ke laayak hone ke bawajood uski aankhon mein ab bhi ehtiyaat thi.

Ilyra: "Tumhari haalat pehle se behtar hai, lekin iska matlab yeh nahi ki main tumhe bina dekh-rekh ke door bhej doon. Abhi tumhe aaram chahiye. Ghar ka safar kal tak ruk sakta hai."

Garrick darwaze ke paas apna travel-cloak sambhale khada tha. Uske chehre par wohi purani, shaant fikr thi.

Garrick: "Skyship taiyaar hai, Ilyra. Main Love ko akela nahi chhodunga. Safar seedha rakhenge aur zaroorat padi toh beech mein ruk jayenge."

Ilyra ne uski taraf dekha, phir Love ki taraf. Uski ungliyan medical satchel ke strap par kas gayin. Love ne usse apne saath chalne ko kaha—saaf tha ki woh us shakhs ki fikr mein der nahi karna chahta jo ghar par uska intezaar kar raha tha. Usne apni baat par zidd bhi ki.

Ilyra: "Tumhe lagta hai mujhe saath le jaane se main safar ko manzoori de dungi?"

Love ne uski taraf dekha. Uski awaaz mein kamzori ab bhi thi, lekin uski baat mein ek bechain, sachchi umeed thi.

Love: "Mujhe jaana hai. Mere liye wahan jaana bahut zaroori hai. Please, mujhe wahan jaane se mat rokiye. Agar aapko meri itni fikr hai, toh aap bhi toh hamare saath chaliye na. Maza aayega. Aap dono mera ghar dekhenge."

Usne yeh baat bahut umeed se kahi.

Kuch lamhon baad Ilyra ne thaki hui saans chhodi.

Ilyra: "Theek hai. Main chalungi. Lekin tum meri baat maanoge—paani, dawa, aur agar tabiyat bigdi toh hum rukenge. Main tumhe raaste mein phir se behosh nahi hone dungi."

Garrick ne halka sa sar jhukaya, jaise usne pehle hi maan liya ho ki Ilyra aakhirkaar yahi faisla karegi.

Garrick: "Toh phir hum teeno chalenge. Tumhari dekh-rekh Ilyra karegi, aur raasta main sambhalunga."

Teeno healer wing se nikle. Mahal ke lambe corridor mein shaam ke lamps jal chuke the. Door Leyline Docks par royal skyship ke neeche neeli roshni daud rahi thi; uske magitech engines ka halka hum pathar ke platform mein mehsoos hota tha. Upar pahadon ke beech hawa thandi thi, aur aasman par suraj ki aakhri laali dheere-dheere bujh rahi thi.

Garrick pehle ramp par chadha. Ilyra ne apni satchel sambhali aur ek baar phir Love ki haalat dekh kar uske saath chal di. Unka manzil tha Love ka ghar—aur woh shakhs jo wahan uska intezaar kar raha tha.

Udhar mahal ke andar, Rivona aur uske chaar dost scout report aur jungle ke route ke liye taiyaar ho rahe the. Dono safar alag the, magar dono ek hi pareshan kar dene wale sandesh se shuru hue the: jungle mein Kyle akela ho sakta tha, aur Love ke ghar par koi uska intezaar kar raha tha.

Shaam tak Love safar ke laayak hua; Ilyra pehle use rokna chahti thi, lekin uski zidd par saath chalne ko maan gayi. Love, Garrick aur Ilyra royal skyship se uske ghar ki taraf rawana hue. Ghar par intezaar karne wale shakhs ka raaz abhi saamne nahi aaya.
