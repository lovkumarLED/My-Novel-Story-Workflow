# Does the Dragon Princess Really Hate Me?
## Chapter 2 — Act 6: Skyship se Chhalang

Location: Love ki royal skyship, Silverpine-Blackvein battlefield ke upar, gehri raat

Royal skyship ladai ke ilaake ke paas aa rahi thi.

Neeche jungle ka ek bada hissa tabah pada tha. Ped tootkar aade-tedhe gire the. Zameen par gehre craters the. Hawa me smoke, dust aur magical energy phaili hui thi. Kabhi neeli-laal roshni ka flash poore clearing ko ujla kar deta, kabhi sab kuch phir se andhere me doob jaata. Upar se saaf dikh raha tha — paanch figures ek behosh body ke aas-paas ghera banaye khadi thin, aur unke saamne ghar jitna bada monster tha.

Tabhi neeche clearing se ek roshni uthi — tez flash — aur uske peeche bahut bhayaanak shockwave nikli. Shockwave upar aayi aur royal skyship ko zor se hila gayi. Ship ek taraf jhuk gayi. Cargo hold me boriyaan ek taraf sarak gayin. Garrick rail se takraate-takraate bacha. Ilyra ne satchel seene se laga li. Love farsh par pheesal kar khidki se takraaya.

Pilot ne controls ko dono haathon se jakad liya. Usne ship ko seedha kiya, engine ki neeli roshni tez ki, aur skyship ko wapas santulan me laaya. Phir usne maathe ka paseena ponchha aur bola:

Pilot: "Marte-marte bache!"

Garrick ne rail kas kar pakdi aur bola:

Garrick: "Bhai, sab bach gaye, bas! Pilot, tumne toh aaj kamaal kar diya!"

Pilot ne halki hansi me kaha:

Pilot: "Aaj toh meri girlfriend ko do baar kahunga ki main usse bahut pyaar karta hoon — ek baar woh wala jo girte waqt kehna tha, ek baar woh wala jo bachne ke baad kehna hai!"

Phir skyship wahan pahunchi aur sabne neeche dekha.

Garrick ne khidki se neeche dekha. Uska dimaag kharaab ho gaya. Andar se woh bahut bhayaanak tareeke se chaunk gaya. Usne rail pakad li.

Garrick: "Ye kya? Rajkumari aur uske dost pehle yahan kyun aa gaye? Aisa nahi hona chahiye tha! Scout report ka intezaar karna tha! Aur ye itna bhayaanak monster, ye kya cheez hai?"

Ilyra uske peeche khidki par aayi. Usne neeche ki roshni dekhi — monster ki gardan ke neeche dhadakti neeli roshni. Uska chehra sakht ho gaya.

Ilyra: "Ye koi aam monster nahi hai. Ye poori tarah ek kharaab ley-cell se banaya gaya hai. Us ley-cell ke saath bahut zyada chhedkhani ki gayi hai. Bada ajeeb monster hai ye, ek khaas tarah ka mutated monster."

Maera monster ko dekhkar boli:

Maera: "Kitna ajeeb sa monster hai. Kya woh is monster se lad nahi paa rahe hain?"

Garrick ne Maera ki taraf dekha. Phir usne neeche ki taraf ishaara kiya.

Garrick: "Unki haalat dekho. Rivona akeli khadi hai, uski talwar tooti hui hai, kapde fate hain, chehre par dhool aur paseena hai. Seraphine ki talwar do tukdon me bati hai, Vaelora ka dagger handle se toota hai, Elowen ki blade mudi hui hai, Nymera ki bhaari talwar beech se chatki hui hai. Chaaron friends exhaustion se zameen par gire hain — khade hone ki taakat nahi bachi. Kyle behosh pada hai, uska armor toota hai, khoon beh chuka hai. Tum ye haalat dekh rahi ho aur soch rahi ho ki woh isse lad paa rahe hain ya nahi paa rahe hain?"

Maera ne kuch nahi kaha. Woh bas neeche dekhti rahi.

Garrick ne apni talwar ka qabza kas liya.

Garrick: "Humein jaldi kuch karna chahiye. Humein rajkumari ko bachana hi padega. Unhe kuch nahi ho sakta."

Ilyra ne turant uska haath pakad liya.

Ilyra: "Nahi Garrick, tum aisa nahi kar sakte. Mujhe nahi lagta tum akele lad paaoge. Mujhe nahi lagta hum me se koi akele usse lad paayega."

Garrick ne neeche monster ko dekha. Uska size, uske kaante, uski raftaar. Usne dheere se sir hilaaya.

Garrick: "Sahi kaha. Is tarah ke monster ka threat level to shayad S-rank [Adventurers Guild ka sabse uncha normal rank] ka hoga, usse bhi zyada. Aise monster ke liye adventurers ki ek achchi-khaasi badi team chahiye, ya phir poori ek trained soldiers ki army chahiye. Akele jaana seedha maut hai."

Tabhi peeche se Love ki awaaz aayi.

Love: "Maera, ye dekhkar mujhe pasand nahi aa raha. Humein jaldi pahunchna hai. Kuch karo na."

Maera ne Love ki taraf dekha. Usne ek aankh maari.

Maera: "Fikr mat karo, main hoon na."

Aur woh kood gayi. Bas kood gayi skyship se — neeche, seedha battlefield ki taraf.

Garrick aur Ilyra use dekhkar chillaaye, funny hairani me:

Garrick aur Ilyra: "Ye kood gayi! Kaise kood gayi?"

Ilyra: "Kaise kood gayi? Hain?"

Hawa me Maera ka maid uniform ud raha tha. Woh seedha neeche ja rahi thi.

Pilot ne controls sambhalte hue chillaya:

Pilot: "Woh kood gayi? Itni unchai se?"

Koi kuch samajhta, usse pehle Maera hawa cheerkar neeche ja chuki thi. Garrick, Ilyra aur Pilot khidki par jhuke saans roke dekh rahe the. Unke peeche Love kohni tikaye aaram se khada tha, jaise roz ka kaam ho. Usne haath hilaate hue kaha:

Love: "Maera! Zyada time mat lagana! Jaldi khatam karke aana!"

Garrick, Ilyra aur Pilot ne ek saath apni gardan tedhi ki aur Love ko dekha. Teenon ke chehre par wahi ajeeb, confused, insane-funny expression tha.

Garrick, Ilyra aur Pilot: **"Hain?"**

Neeche clearing me Rivona akeli khadi thi. Usne apne mann me Dragon God ko pukaarne wala adhura spell shuru kiya tha. Monster ka claw uske chehre ke saamne tha. Paas lete Vaelora ne chillakar use roka tha, baaki dost bhi chillaaye the — lekin spell poora hone se pehle bijli se tez kuch Rivona ke saamne land hua.

Ek lightning-like beam monster ke haath ko cheer gayi. Huge roar aur impact hua. Monster ka haath katkar alag ho gaya.

Skyship se Love, Garrick aur Ilyra ne dekha — woh Maera thi. Maid uniform me, back sabki taraf, ek haath me butter knife aur doosre haath me fork, haathon se X formation banaye.

Uske baad jo hua, woh hum pehle hi dekh chuke hain — X formation wala save, kitchen set ka blast attack, sabka Hain-Hain, knives ka pakadkar box me rakhna, aur tukdon ka judkar bada monster banna. In sab ke baare me hum jaante hi hain. Ab current scenario par aate hain.

---

Ab Maera ke saamne ek naya monster tha, jo dobara khud ko jod chuka tha.

Woh dekhne me thoda badal gaya tha. Pehle samajh nahi aa raha tha woh kaisa hai, hazy sa lagta tha — lekin ab woh ek drill-snake ki tarah tha. Snake jaisa form, lekin uska chehra aur sir drill ki tarah muda hua tha, jaise deewar chhedne wala drill. Uski aankhen laal chamak rahi thin, munh me tez daant the, upar kaanton ki kataaren thin, poore badan par alag-alag spikes nikle the. Ek snake, lekin drill ki tarah.

Woh zameen ke andar ghus gaya. Mitti upar uthi. Phir woh bahar nikla — seedha Maera ki taraf jhapatta.

Maera ne zameen ka kaanpna mehsoos kiya aur palak jhapakte side me koodi. Agle hi pal drill-face usi jagah se bahar phoota jahan woh khadi thi — mitti ka phuwara utha, paas se guzarta hua woh peeche pade toote tane ke malbe se takraaya. Lakdi ke tukde aur mitti hawa me bikhre. Maera ne dekhte hi samajh liya — ye form zameen ko apna raasta banata hai, agla hamla neeche se aayega.

Usne intezaar nahi kiya. Monster ke mudne se pehle woh uske shareer par chadh gayi. Spikes ke beech pair rakhte hue, fisalte-bachte woh uske reengte badan par daudi — ek spike uske kandhe ko chhoota, doosre par usne pair tikaya aur uchhal gayi. Hawa me hi usne box se utensils nikaale — tak-tak-tak-tak. Pehla chammach gardan par laga, dhamaka hua, maans phata. Doosra knife shareer me ghusa, phir dhamaka. Teesra poonchh ki taraf. Body jagah-jagah se phat gayi, tukde hawa me ude aur zameen par bikhre.

Lekin zameen par girte hi tukde kaanpne lage. Neeli roshni ek tukde se doosre tak daudi — aur is baar judne ki raftaar pehle se dugni thi. Maera ke pair zameen par tikte, usse pehle aadhe tukde wapas jud chuke the.

Rivona ne dekha. Woh chillayi:

Rivona: "Kuch karo! Use dobara mat judne do! Mujhe nahi pata tum kaun ho, lekin suno — ise judne mat do! Aisa kuch karo ki uska shareer jud na paaye!"

Maera ne suna. Uske mann me ek hi baat aayi — ise jala deti hoon.

Woh hawa se neeche aayi, ghutne modkar zameen par utri. Bina spell padhe usne seedha haath aage kiya. Hatheli se aag phooti — bhayaanak aag, jaise jet engine ka muh khul gaya ho, jaise bade rocket ke engine se full speed me aag nikal rahi ho. Aag ki dhaar seedha monster ke shareer par lagi. Dhakka itna zor ka tha ki monster ka reengta shareer mitti me ghisat gaya. Aaspaas ka area pehle hi jala-kaala tha — lekin is aag ne peeche ki mitti hi pighla di, woh lava jaise behne lagi. Hawa kaanpne lagi, garmi se Maera ke chehre par paseena aa gaya.

Upar skyship me Garrick ki aankhen phat gayin:

Garrick: "Ye... ye kya tha? Bina spell padhe... itni aag? Ye kaise? Main... main samajh hi nahi paa raha Maera ne abhi kya kiya!"

Ilyra ne sir pakad liya:

Ilyra: "Bina spell ke? Bina ek shabd bole? Aisi aag nikaalna insane hai! Ye... ye kaise possible hai?"

Neeche Rivona ke doston ke beech dusri behes chhid gayi. Seraphine ne Vaelora se kaha:

Seraphine: "Ye... ye mage hai? Koi top-class mage?"

Vaelora ne turant sir hilaaya:

Vaelora: "Nahi! Top-class mage bhi bina spell ke aisa nahi kar sakta! Aisa karne ke liye use khud me really, really insane amount of mana chahiye hoga!"

Nymera ne munh khola, phir band kar liya:

Nymera: "Toh... toh ye hai kya?"

Elowen bas Maera ko dekhti rahi:

Elowen: "Main... ginna chhod diya maine..."

Phir aag hati. Monster khada tha. Uska shareer kaala pada tha, ek spike pighal kar jhuk gaya tha — bas. Itne bade hamle par itni chhoti haani. Maera ko pata chala — iska resistance bahut zyada hai. Itna damage khaane ke baad woh aur evolve hota gaya, isliye iska resistance ab bahut zyada hai.

Zameen par leti Vaelora ne ye dekha. Usne dheemi awaaz me kaha:

Vaelora: "Itni aag... aur uska shareer sirf kaala pada... ye cheez kya hai?"

Elowen ne haanfkar jawab diya, nazar monster par:

Elowen: "Resistance... har baar badh raha hai..."

Maera sochne lagi — ise maare kaise? Monster ko maare kaise?

Tabhi Maera ki nazar padi — monster ka ek bahut hi chhota tukda, ungli ke size se bhi chhota, uske side se rengta guzar raha tha. Maera ne use dekha. Maera ne us par ungli se chhoti si aag chhodi. Tukda jal gaya — poora, ash. Khatam ho gaya.

Maera ki aankhen chamkin. Use todna hai — itne tukde karo ki judne ka waqt hi na mile.

Maera: "Achha... chhote, hai na."

Woh daudi. Monster abhi burn se sambhal raha tha — woh uske lehraate shareer ke neeche se nikli, ek ubhare hue spike ko pakadkar upar chadhi, reengte badan par daudti hui poonchh se gardan tak gayi. Phir uchhli — hawa me palti khaakar ulta, pair upar, sir neeche — aur dono haathon se utensils chhod diye — tak-tak-tak-tak. Pehla knife sir ke paas gardan me ghusa, dhamaka, maans uchhla. Doosra shareer par, phir dhamaka. Teesra poonchh ki taraf, chautha gardan ke neeche. Har hit par blast, har blast par tukde.

Aur woh zameen par aayi hi nahi. Hawa me udte hue ek tukde par usne pair rakha, us par se koodkar doosre tukde par — udte tukdon par balance banate hue, chammach phenkti, maans phaadti — phaadti gayi, phaadti gayi. Woh kabhi ruki nahi.

Uske utensils hawa me hi rehte. Woh full speed me jaati, phenke hue chammach ko pakadti, ghoomkar ungli se tak karke phir phenk deti — tak-tak-tak-tak. Kabhi koi chammach neeche girta, toh use laat maarkar upar uchhaal deti. Uski speed itni thi ki woh har pal analyze kar rahi thi — kahan koodna hai, kahan nahi. Ek tukde se doosre tukde par, ek blast se doosra blast — aisa woh baar-baar karti rahi, tab tak jab tak monster ke bahut chhote-chhote tukde nahi ho gaye.

Lekin use aur chhote tukde chahiye the. Toh woh zameen par aayi. Ab woh zameen se chammach upar ki taraf phenkne lagi — tak-tak-tak-tak-tak — un chhote tukdon ki taraf, taaki unhe aur chhota kar paaye. Chammach neeche aate toh turant phenk deti — kabhi ungli se maar kar, kabhi neeche gire chammach ko laat maar kar upar. Tak-tak-tak-tak-tak.

Ye sab dekhkar neeche Rivona aur uske dost hairaan the. Upar skyship me Love khidki se chipka tha.

Aur Garrick ko ab ehsaas ho raha tha ki Maera kya cheez hai. Farmhouse me jab Maera ne itne confidence se kaha tha "main sambhaal loongi, ismein darr kya hai" — use laga tha sab mazaak hai. Usne dheemi awaaz me Ilyra se kaha:

Garrick: "Ab samajh aa raha hai... is shaam farmhouse me usne itne confidence se kaha tha 'main sambhaal loongi' — mujhe laga mazaak hai. Maine aaj tak aisa kuch nahi dekha. Dwarf dekhe hain, taakatwar hote hain — lekin ek half-dwarf me itni taakat? Kaise? Main ye process hi nahi kar paa raha hoon. Lekin ab itna samajh aa gaya — uska confidence aisa hi nahi tha."

Ilyra ne bas sir hilaaya, nazar Maera par:

Ilyra: "Ye speed... ye taakat... ye kya hai..."

Monster ko judne ka mauka hi nahi mila.

Neeche Nymera ne ye dekhkar hairani me kaha:

Nymera: "Woh ruk hi nahi rahi... woh akele poori army jitna lad rahi hai..."

Upar skyship me Garrick khidki se chipak gaya:

Garrick: "Dekho use! Woh giregi nahi? Are... woh phir kood gayi!"

Ilyra ne saans roke kaha:

Ilyra: "Woh ruk hi nahi rahi..."

Poore environment me ab sirf tukde hi bikhre hue the — bahut hi chhote-chhote tukde, insaan ki chhoti ungli se bhi chhote size ke tukde, har taraf. Zameen par, hawa me, craters me — har jagah. Har taraf khoon phaila hua tha aur usme se steam uth rahi thi. Hawa me jali hui mitti, pighle pathar aur monster ke jale maans ki ajeeb gandh bhari thi. Peeche ki mitti lava jaise jam gayi thi. Do ghante ki ladai, blast attacks aur jet-jaisi aag ke baad poora clearing kaala pad chuka tha — toote tane, gehre craters, bikhre weapons, behosh pade sainik — jaise poora battlefield saans rokkar agle pal ka intezaar kar raha ho. Aur monster ka crystal [us damaged ley-cell ka bikhra hua core] bhi tukdon me bikhra pada tha, neeli roshni me timtimata hua.

Maera neeche aayi. Usne sabse bade crystal-tukde par ek chammach de maara. Chammach lagte hi usne dusra haath upar uthaya — ek bada spell. Spell se pehle woh Dwarf God [dwarves ke god Durzak Stoneheart] ko yaad karti hai. Uski awaaz poore clearing me goonji:

Maera: "Dwarf God, main aapki beti hoon, mujhe aapse kuch nahi chahiye. Main chahti hoon ki jis tarah tum duniya ko protect karte ho, sabko protect karo, mere in sabhi logon ko protect karo."

Jaise hi usne kaha, zameen se sunehri roshni uthi aur sabke aaspaas shields ban gayin. Upar skyship ke paas shield aayi — Garrick shield dekhkar chaunka, Ilyra aur bhi chaunki, dono ne shield ko haath lagakar dekha. Neeche Rivona aur uske doston ko shields ne gher liya — woh bhi chaunk gaye ki ye ho kya raha hai. Aur jitne ghayal sainik zameen par pade the aur sainikon ki laashen wahan thin, unhe bhi shields ne dheere se gher liya.

Phir woh spell karti hai. Itna bhayaanak spell. Woh dono haath aasmaan ki taraf karke pukarti hai:

Maera: "Destruction, main tumhein pukaar rahi hoon, mere paas aao. Apni aag, aag ki shakti mujhe do, itni aag paida karo jitna sooraj chamakta hai, mujhe tumhari shakti chahiye, Destructive beam of fire!"

Ek bada spell hua. Maera ke haathon se aag aur poori roshni nikli — laser ki tarah upar ki taraf, seedha, bahut upar, baadalon ko cheerkar paar karti hui. Roshni itni tez thi ki sabko aankhen band karni padin. Uske baad ilaaka, jis jagah spell hua, bhatti jaise garam ho gaya — hawa jalne lagi, zameen laal padne lagi. Aur monster ki har cheez — tukde, kaante, crystal — ash me badal gayi. Hawa me sirf ash udi.

Raat itni ujli ho gayi thi ki poora surrounding ilaaka din jaise jagmaga utha. Roshni itni door gayi ki door-daraaz gaon tak pahunch gayi — woh gaon jo paas nahi, bahut door tha. Wahan do gaonwale chaunkkar uth baithe.

Jorren: "Are! Subah ho gayi kya? Main toh abhi soya tha!"

Pell: "Subah? Aadhi raat me murga bhi nahi bola! Ye kaisi subah hai jo aayi aur chali gayi?"

Jorren: "Toh ye roshni kya thi? Meri aankhen kharaab ho gayin kya?"

Pell: "Teri aankhen toh pehle se hi kharaab hain. Chal so ja, sapna tha!"
