# Does the Dragon Princess Really Hate Me?
## Chapter 2 — Act 3: Jungle Ke Upar, Subah Se Pehle

Location: Love ka farmhouse aur Blackvein Wilds ke upar, raat

Farmhouse ke andar packing ab ek chhoti si afra-tafri ban chuki thi.

Darwaze ke paas boriyaan, tokriyaan aur auzaaron ke bundle jama ho gaye the. Garrick ek-ek cheez ko dekhkar tay kar raha tha ki kya skyship ke cargo hold mein surakshit rakha ja sakta hai. Ilyra ne dawaaiyon aur medical samaan ko alag, aasaan pahunch wali jagah par rakhwaya. Maera ne kapdon aur ghar ki zaroori cheezon ko itni tezi se baandha jaise use subah tak mahal pahunchna nahi, balki usse pehle poore ghar ko ek hi bori mein sama dena ho.

Love bhi unke saath samaan jama kar raha tha. Chulhe ki aakhri aanch bujha di gayi thi; lanterns ki roshni mein ghar ab bhi garam aur basa hua lag raha tha, magar mez aur shelves dheere-dheere khaali ho rahe the. Maera ne Love ke maa-baap ki tasveer ko ek saaf, mulayam kapde mein lapetkar uske samaan ke upar sambhaal kar rakh diya, taaki safar mein tasveer ko koi nuksaan na pahunche.

Phir Love ne uski taraf dekha, aur safar ki thakaan ke beech uski awaaz mein ek nayi chamak aa gayi.

Love: "Sweetheart, tumhe pata hai? Mahal mein mujhe ek khet bhi mila hai—achchha-khaasa bada. Yahan ke khet se bhi zyada. Hum dono wahan milkar kheti kar sakte hain."

Maera ne haath mein pakda kapda modte hue uski taraf dekha.

Maera: "Haan, darling? Tumne kaha khet mila hai?"

Love: "Haan, sweetheart! Socho, mahal mein hamara apna khet hoga. Hum tarah-tarah ki faslein ugaayenge—anaaj, sabziyaan, jo bhi mitti aur mausam ko pasand aaye. Bahut maza aayega."

Maera ne bori zameen par rakh di. Uski muskurahat khil uthi.

Maera: "Kyun nahi, darling? Hum aisi fasal ugaayenge ki mahal ke rasoiye roz hamare darwaaze par poochhne aayenge—‘Aaj kya kaat sakte hain?’"

Love: "Aur main kahunga, ‘Pehle batao, beej kisne boya tha?’"

Maera: "Phir woh kahenge, ‘Aap dono ne.’ Aur tum kahoge—"

Love: "‘Toh tokri ka aadha hissa hamara!’"

Maera hansi, aur Love bhi uske saath hans pada. Agle kuch lamhon tak dono beej, faslon aur mahal ke khet ki planning mein itne kho gaye ki aas-paas bikhra samaan aur raat ki jaldi jaise kuch der ko bhool gaye. Maera ne Love ke kandhe se dhool ka resha hata diya; Love ne kheti ke auzaaron ki tokri uske paas rakh di.

Garrick ke haath mein bandhne ki rassi thi. Woh un dono ko dekhte-dekhte bori ki gaanth zaroorat se zyada kas gaya.

Ilyra ne dheere se kaha:

Ilyra: "Garrick, rassi ko aur kasoge toh bori ke saath auzaar bhi do tukdon mein pahunch jaayenge."

Garrick ne rassi chhod di aur Love-Maera ki taraf dekha.

Garrick: "Main bas yeh tay kar raha hoon ki in dono ko khet ki baat karte dekhkar mujhe khushi honi chahiye ya inke liye alag se ek farmhouse dhoondhna chahiye."

Ilyra ne bhi un dono ko dekha. Uske chehre par jhijhak thi, par muskurahat bhi.

Ilyra: "Ajeeb toh lag raha hai… lekin inhe itna khush dekhkar achchha bhi lag raha hai."

Garrick: "Mujhe bhi. Bas mahal ka khet inke romance aur sabziyon ka bojh sambhaal le."

Ilyra: "Khet sambhaal lega. Mahal ke rasoiye shayad nahi."

Maera ne unki dabi-dabi baat sun li aur muskurate hue palti.

Maera: "Aap dono chinta mat kijiye. Aapke liye bhi kuch uga denge."

Garrick: "Mujhe bas itna chahiye ki safar mein mujhse kheti ki salah na maangi jaaye."

Love: "Lekin Garrick, aapko toh khet pasand hain na?"

Garrick ne agla bundle uthaya.

Garrick: "Pasand hain, son. Bas unke baare mein raat bhar ki baithak nahi."

Love aur Maera phir muskura diye. Unki halki nok-jhonk abhi chal hi rahi thi ki Garrick ne bori zameen par rakhi aur khaali hote kamre par nazar daali.

Garrick: "Achchha, khet ki planning baad mein bhi ho jaayegi. Agar humein subah hone se pehle mahal pahunchna hai, toh ek mushkil hai."

Love: "Kaisi mushkil?"

Garrick: "Seedha aur surakshit raasta liya toh subah tak Drakon Court pahunchna mushkil hoga. Jaldi pahunchne ke liye skyship ko ek naya route lena padega."

Ilyra ne apni satchel ka strap thaama.

Ilyra: "Naya route matlab?"

Garrick ne khidki ke paar ghane, andhere pedon ki taraf dekha.

Garrick: "Blackvein Wilds ke upar se. Seedha raasta hai, lekin yeh empire ka sabse khatarnak jungle hai."

Maera ne bina ek pal gavaaye apne paas ki bori utha li.

Maera: "Toh phir jungle ke upar se hi chalte hain. Skyship hai—raasta paar kar legi."

Garrick aur Ilyra ek saath uski taraf mude.

Dono ne ek saath hairani se zor se kaha:

Garrick aur Ilyra: "Kya!"

Ilyra: "Aap keh rahi hain hum Blackvein ke upar se ud jaayen?"

Garrick: "Maera, wahan se toh trained soldiers bhi guzarna pasand nahi karte. Yeh koi chhota-sa jangal nahi hai!"

Maera ne un dono ki ghabrahat dekhi, phir kandhe uchka diye.

Maera: "Itna kyun dar rahe hain? Jungle ke upar se jaana toh aam baat hai."

Garrick hairani se zor se bola:

Garrick: "Kiske liye aam baat hai!"

Ilyra: "Neeche ke ped itne unche hain ki unki shaakhein ship tak pahunch sakti hain. Aur agar koi flying monster saamne aa gaya toh? Hawa ka rukh badal gaya toh? Ya engine ne—"

Maera ne haath utha kar uske sawaalon ki lambi list rok di.

Maera: "Aap dono har khatre ko ek saath ginne lagte hain. Main sambhaal loongi. Aur agar main bhi nahi sambhaal paayi, toh Love hai na—yeh sambhaal lega."

Kuch pal ke liye kamre mein bilkul khamoshi chha gayi.

Garrick aur Ilyra ne Maera ki baat ko hairani me dohraaya, jaise unhe apne kaanon par yakeen na ho raha ho:

Garrick aur Ilyra: "Yeh sambhaal lega..."

Dono ki nazar Love par tik gayi—usi aam kisaan par, jisne khud unhe bataya tha ki woh bas khana maangne mahal gaya tha. Ab woh ek kheti ka auzaar haath mein liye khada tha, jaise kisi bhi pal usse jungle ke monsters ke baare mein nahi, fasal ke beej ke baare mein poochha jaane wala ho.

Ilyra: "Maera, aap kehna kya chaah rahi hain?"

Garrick: "Son, tumne toh khud kaha tha ki tum ek aam kisaan ho."

Love ne kandhe uchkaaye aur agla bundle baandhne laga.

Love: "Haan, main ek aam kisaan hoon, maine jhooth thodi na kaha. Aur yeh toh chhoti-moti baat hai. Main sambhaal loonga. Hamesha se karta aaya hoon. Humein jaldi pahunchna hai na? Warna sab log gussa ho jaayenge. Aur phir court ka kaam bhi toh hai—jaanch, poochhtaach. Scholar Thalorien ko bhi mujhse bahut kuch poochhna hai."

Garrick uska chehra dekhta raha, jaise jawaab mein chhupa koi raaz pakadne ki koshish kar raha ho. Ilyra ne bhi Love aur Maera ke beech nazar ghumaayi. Maera ne bas bori ki gaanth aur kas di.

Garrick aur Ilyra dono chaunk gaye. Unhe samajh me nahi aa raha tha Love ne abhi kya kaha tha. Dono bahut aashcharyachakit the — Garrick ki aankhen phail gayin, Ilyra ki ungliyan satchel par kas gayin.

Garrick: "Yeh kya keh raha hai? Roz ki baat hai? Roz monster kaata hai ya fasal? Mujhe toh samajh me nahi aa raha hai. Mujhe lagta hai mujhe baatein sunai de rahi hain, main paagal ho raha hoon. Maine aaj bahut kuch dekh liya. Lady Ilyra, aapke paas koi dawaai hai?"

Ilyra: "Uski zaroorat toh mujhe bhi pad rahi hai. Garrick, yeh sab kya ho raha hai?"

Garrick: "Mujhe nahi pata tum log kya jaante ho, tum log kya karoge mujhe. Aur tum log yahan monster kaatte ho ya fasal, lekin mujhe filhaal kuch nahi pata. Shayad yeh sab mazaak hai. Filhaal toh humein mahal jaldi pahunchna hai kyunki rajkumari kisi bhi waqt Kyle ki khoj me nikal sakti hai aur mujhe unke saath jaana padega safety ensure karne ke liye. Toh main woh raasta lene ke liye taiyaar hoon, humein thoda jokhim toh uthana hi padega."

Garrick: "Theek hai. Magar agar aaj raat hum mar gaye, toh afterlife mein bhi tum dono ka peecha nahi chhodunga. Itna kosunga ki wahan ke log mujhe chup karane aa jaayenge."

Maera ne ek aankh maari.

Maera: "Uski naubat hi nahi aayegi."

Ilyra ne apni satchel uthaayi.

Ilyra: "Main sirf isliye maan rahi hoon kyunki kisi ko mahal pahunchkar in dono ko khet ke alawa doosre kaam bhi yaad dilane honge."

Love: "Mujhe kaam yaad rahega. Bas pehle khet dekh lenge."

Garrick: "Yahi toh mujhe dara raha hai, son."

### Blackvein ke kinaare ki landing clearing

Kuch hi der baad sab samaan clearing tak laaya gaya, jahan royal skyship unke intezaar mein thi. Raat ki nami uske dhaatu aur lakdi ke badan par moti boondon ki tarah chamak rahi thi. Magitech engines ke andar neeli roshni dheere-dheere daudti thi; pilot ne unhe door se dekhkar cargo ramp neeche kar diya.

Garrick aur Love ne crates aur auzaar hold mein rakhne mein madad ki. Maera ne sabse bhaari bori phir se uthayi aur bina kisi ki madad liye ramp par chadha di. Ilyra ne uske peeche jaate hue bori ko dekha, phir apne haath mein pakdi chhoti medical satchel ko.

Ilyra: "Main apni satchel ko ab halka mehsoos kar rahi hoon. Ya phir apne aap ko kamzor."

Maera: "Satchel mein dawaaiyaan hain. Use bhaari karne ki zaroorat nahi."

Garrick: "Aur bori mein kya hai? Pathar?"

Maera: "Kheti ka samaan."

Garrick: "Yeh jawab mujhe aur dara raha hai."

Pilot ne cargo band karte hue Garrick ko route-chart dikhaya.

Pilot: "Aapke chune hue seedhe route mein humein Blackvein ka canopy paar karna hoga. Hawa abhi shaant hai, lekin canopy ke upar uska rukh badal sakta hai. Main ship ko jitna ho sake uncha rakhunga."

Garrick ne chart dekha, phir uske paar andhere jungle ko.

Garrick: "Aapne kaha tha hawa abhi shaant hai. 'Abhi' ka matlab kitni der?"

Pilot: "Jitni der tak woh apna iraada na badle."

Ilyra: "Yeh bilkul bharosemand jawaab nahi tha."

Pilot: "Yeh aasman ka bharosemand jawaab tha, Lady Ilyra."

Maera ne ramp ke paas khade hokar jungle ke andhere canopy ko dekha. Uske chehre par wohi shaant yaqeen tha jo farmhouse mein tha.

Maera: "Chaliye. Subah tak mahal pahunchna hai."

Garrick ne ek gehri saans li aur apni talwar ka strap kas liya.

Garrick: "Mujhe ab bhi samajh nahi aa raha ki Maera ka bharosa mujhe tasalli de raha hai ya meri mushkil badha raha hai."

Ilyra: "Udaan shuru hone ke baad pata chal jaayega."

Cargo hold mein aakhri bundle bhi rakh diya gaya. Garrick ne crates ki kundi jaanchi, Ilyra ne dawaaiyon ki satchel apne paas baandhi, aur Maera ne farmhouse se laaye chhote samaan par ek nazar daali. Sab kuch pack ho chuka tha. Skyship tayyaar thi; bas unhe usmein savaar hona baaki tha.

Love ne khule ramp aur andhere kheton ke beech nazar daudayi. Phir uski aankhen kheton ke saamne bane do saada patthar ke nishanon par tik gayin—uske maa-baap ki kabrein. Woh kuch pal chup raha, jaise apne aap ko samjha raha ho ki jaane se pehle unse milne ka yeh aakhri mauka hai.

Love: "Main bas ek baar unke paas jaakar aata hoon."

Maera ne bina kuch poochhe uske saath kadam badha diye. Garrick aur Ilyra ne unhe jaane diya; is baar kisi ne jaldi karne ko nahi kaha.

Kheton ke saamne ki mitti raat ki nami se thandi thi. Love kabron ke paas rukkar neeche jhuk gaya. Usne dono patthar saaf kiye, phir haath kuch pal un par tikaye rakha. Uske peeche Maera khamoshi se khadi rahi.

Love: "Maa, Papa… aap dono ne hamesha mera khayal rakha. Jab hamare paas zyada kuch nahi tha, tab bhi aapne mujhe aur Maera ko apna pyaar diya. Mujhe bahut dukh ho raha hai ki main yeh jagah chhodkar ja raha hoon. Lekin main wapas aaunga. Yeh jagah aap dono ke liye bahut maayne rakhti thi—mere liye bhi. Main zaroor lautunga."

Uski awaaz aakhri jumle par halki si bharra gayi. Maera paas aayi aur uske kandhe par haath rakh diya.

Maera: "Koi baat nahi, Love. Hum sab milkar yeh waqt jhel lenge. Tum akele nahi ho."

Love ne uske haath par apna haath rakha aur ek chhoti si saans chhodi. Phir dono ne kabron ko aakhri baar dekha aur saath-saath skyship ki roshni ki taraf laut pade.

Ramp ke paas Garrick unka intezaar kar raha tha. Usne kuch nahi poochha; bas ek taraf hatkar unhe pehle chadhne diya. Love aur Maera saath mein ramp par chadhe, phir Ilyra aur Garrick unke peeche andar aa gaye.

Darwaaza band hua, restraints kas diye gaye, aur cargo hold ki boriyaan apni jagah par jam gayin.

Magitech engines ki gunj gehri hui. Skyship ke neeche neeli roshni phaili, aur uska badan zameen se aahista utha. Clearing ki ghaas peeche jhuk gayi; farmhouse ki lanterns pedon ke beech chhoti hoti chali gayin.

Pilot ne saamne ke andhere canopy ki taraf rukh kiya.

Blackvein Wilds unke neeche ek anant kaali lehar ki tarah phaila tha. Unchi shaakhein hawa mein kaante jaise ubhri hui thin. Door kahin jungle ke andar ek awaaz goonji—itni gehri ki ship ke farsh tak halki si thartharahat pahunchi.

Garrick ne seat ka kinara pakad liya.

Garrick: "Bas ek baat bata dijiye—yeh awaaz zameen se aayi thi ya aasman se?"

Maera ne khidki ke baahar dekha, phir uski taraf muskurayi.

Maera: "Aapko sach mein abhi jawaab chahiye?"

Ilyra ne apni satchel ko dono haathon se pakad liya. Pilot ne engine ko aur zor diya, aur royal skyship jungle ke canopy ke upar seedhi chadhne lagi.

Subah hone se pehle unhe Drakon Court pahunchna tha.

Lekin Blackvein ke upar ki raat abhi lambi thi.

## Haalat ka chhota sa recap

Love, Maera, Garrick aur Ilyra farmhouse ka samaan jaldi-jaldi pack karte hain. Maera Love ke maa-baap ki tasveer ko uske samaan ke saath sambhaal kar rakhti hai. Packing ke beech Love Maera ko batata hai ki mahal mein unhe ek achchha-khaasa khet mila hai. Dono sweetheart aur darling ke pyaar-bhare andaaz mein apni faslon ki planning karne lagte hain; Garrick aur Ilyra unka romance dekhkar jhijhakte bhi hain aur unse mazaak bhi karte hain. Phir Garrick kehta hai ki subah tak Drakon Court pahunchne ke liye naya route lena hoga: Blackvein Wilds ke upar se. Maera ise aam baat samajhti hai, jis par Garrick aur Ilyra ghabra jaate hain. Woh unche pedon, flying monsters aur hawa ke khatre ginwaate hain. Maera kehti hai woh sambhaal legi, aur zaroorat padi toh Love bhi; dono us aam kisaan ko dekhkar hairaan hote hain, lekin Love ko jaldi pahunchkar court aur Scholar Thalorien ke sawaalon ka saamna karna hai. Samaan skyship mein chadhaane ke baad Love kheton ke saamne apne maa-baap ki kabron par aakhri baar vida leta hai. Maera uske saath rehti hai, kandhe par haath rakhkar kehti hai ki dono is mushkil waqt ko saath jhel lenge. Phir sab skyship par savaar hote hain aur raat ke Blackvein canopy ki taraf udaan bharte hain. Doosri taraf Rivona aur uski saheliyan abhi bhi zakhmi Kyle ke saamne vishaal monster ka morcha sambhaal rahi hain.

## Tumhari baari, Love

Royal skyship Blackvein Wilds ke canopy ke upar chadh rahi hai. Garrick aur Ilyra abhi bhi is baat ko samajhne ki koshish kar rahe hain ki Maera ko kyun yaqeen hai ki zaroorat padi toh Love sab sambhaal lega. Jungle se aayi awaaz ka srot dikhayi nahi diya.

Love ab kya kehta hai ya karta hai?
