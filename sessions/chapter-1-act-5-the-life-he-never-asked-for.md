# Does the Dragon Princess Really Hate Me?
## Chapter 1 — Act 5: The Life He Never Asked For

Imperial Palace of Aurelthys — Royal Reception Hall

Tumne aakhirkaar apna khana khatam kar liya.

Table par rakhi soup ki katori khaali thi, bread ka aakhri tukda bhi khatam ho chuka tha, aur paani ka jug ab pehle se kaafi halka tha. Bahar baarish ki awaaz dheemi padne lagi thi, lekin palace ke andar ab bhi wahi bhaari khamoshi thi.

King Aldric tumhari baat sunne ke liye wahin ruke rahe.

Jab tumne bataya ki tum anath the, aur ek chhote kisaan parivaar ne tumhe adopt karke apna bachcha banaya tha, toh unke chehre ka expression badal gaya.

Woh tumhari taraf kuch der tak dekhte rahe.

King Aldric: "Toh tumhare maa-baap ne tumhe janam nahi diya tha... lekin unhone tumhe apna bachcha banaya, tumhe paala, aur tumhe jeena sikhaya."

Unhone dheere se sir hilaya.

King Aldric: "Mere liye, Love, maa-baap hone ka matlab sirf kisi ko janam dena nahi hai. Kisi ko apna kehna, uski zimmedari lena aur use bada karna bhi utna hi maayne rakhta hai."

Unki awaaz mein ek aisa samman tha jo kisi royal formality se nahi aaya tha.

King Aldric: "Tum kehte ho ki tumhe sirf kheti karni aati hai. Lekin tumne apne maa-baap ke baare mein jis tarah baat ki, usse mujhe lagta hai ki unhone tumhe sirf kheti karna nahi sikhaya. Unhone tumhe apne rishte ki keemat samajhna bhi sikhaya."

Phir tumne unse woh sawaal poochha jo tumhare liye sabse zyada zaroori tha.

Agar Registry ka faisla sach nikla, toh kya tumhari zindagi badal jayegi?

Tumne saaf kaha ki tum aisa nahi chahte. Tum apne ghar lautna chahte ho, apne maa-baap ke khet mein kaam karna chahte ho, aur bas ek aam zindagi jeena chahte ho.

King Aldric ka chehra dheere-dheere gambhir ho gaya.

Unhone turant tumhe koi jhootha vaada nahi diya.

King Aldric: "Love, main tumse jhooth nahi bolunga. Agar Registry ka marriage bond valid nikla, toh tumhari zindagi badalne ki sambhavna bahut zyada hai."

Woh kuch kadam chale aur ek bade stained-glass window ke saamne ruk gaye. Us par Legendary Hero ko alag-alag races ke warriors ke saath khada dikhaya gaya tha.

King Aldric: "Tumhe samajhna hoga ki meri beti sirf ek dragon princess nahi hai. Woh Dragon Empire ki Crown Princess hai. Uski shaadi ka asar royal succession, political alliances aur doosre kingdoms ke saath hamare rishton par pad sakta hai."

Unhone apna sir tumhari taraf ghumaya.

King Aldric: "Agar tumhara marriage bond valid hai, toh court ko yeh tay karna hoga ki uska legal matlab kya hai, tum dono ke kya rights hain, aur kya ise challenge kiya ja sakta hai. Main tumhe yeh nahi keh sakta ki tum kal hi apne gaon laut jaoge."

Ek pal ke liye woh khamosh rahe.

King Aldric: "Lekin iska matlab yeh bhi nahi hai ki tumhe apni poori zindagi yahin bitaani padegi."

Unhone apni awaaz mein pehle se zyada narmi laate hue kaha—

King Aldric: "Main tumhe ek baat saaf kar deta hoon. Tumhari marzi bhi maayne rakhti hai. Agar tumne kisi ko dhokha nahi diya, kisi par zabardasti nahi ki aur tum khud is rishte ko nahi chahte, toh main tumhari baat High Court ke saamne rakhunga."

Unki aankhen tum par tik gayin.

King Aldric: "Main yeh vaada nahi kar sakta ki kanoon ka faisla tumhari pasand ke mutabik hoga. Lekin main tumhe apni zindagi ke baare mein bolne ka mauka zaroor dunga."

Phir unhone dheere se joda—

King Aldric: "Aur jab tak yeh sab chal raha hai, tumhe apne maa-baap ki yaad ya apne khet se rishta todne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Tumhara ghar tumhara ghar hi rahega."

### Ek baap ki nazar se

King Aldric kuch pal tak chup rahe.

Phir unhone ek aisa sawaal poochha jiski tumne shayad umeed nahi ki thi.

King Aldric: "Tumne kaha ki tumhare maa-baap ne tumhe ek behtar insaan banaya. Kya unhone kabhi tumse kaha tha ki tumhe apni zindagi mein kya banna chahiye?"

Unhone turant hi apne sawaal ko thoda badla.

King Aldric: "Mera matlab hai, kya unhone tumse kabhi kaha tha ki tumhe kisi bade shehar jaana chahiye, kisi aur tarah ka kaam karna chahiye, ya apni zindagi ko kheti se aage badhana chahiye? Ya unki bas itni khwahish thi ki tum apne ghar mein khush raho?"

Unke lehje mein ab koi royal interrogation nahi tha. Woh tumhari zindagi ko samajhne ki koshish kar rahe the.

Lekin isi waqt hall ke bade darwaze par ek royal attendant dikhai di.

Woh Princess Rivona ki personal attendant, Liora Fernbrook, thi. Usne King ke saamne jhukkar kaha—

Liora: "Your Majesty, Princess Rivona aapse milna chahti hain. Unhone kaha hai ki Ancient Marriage Registry ke record ke baare mein unhe abhi baat karni hai."

King Aldric ki aankhen ek pal ke liye band hui, jaise unhe pehle se pata tha ki yeh baat hone wali hai.

King Aldric: "Use andar bhej do."

Liora ne sir jhukaya aur darwaze ki taraf laut gayi.

Kuch hi pal baad, Princess Rivona hall mein aayi.

Uska chehra shaant tha, lekin uski amber aankhon mein gussa ab bhi saaf dikh raha tha. Uski nazar pehle King par gayi, phir tum par.

Rivona: "Father, mujhe Registry ka original record dekhna hai. Aur main chahti hoon ki is aadmi ko bhi High Court ki investigation mein formally suna jaaye."

King ne uski taraf dekha.

King Aldric: "Yeh toh hona hi hai."

Rivona ne apne baazu baandh liye.

Rivona: "Aur jab tak investigation poori nahi hoti, main chahti hoon ki yeh palace mein rahe. Agar Registry ka bond sach hai, toh mujhe yeh samajhna hoga ki yeh kaise hua."

Usne tumhari taraf seedha dekha.

Rivona: "Iska matlab yeh mat samajhna ki main tum par bharosa karti hoon. Main bas sach jaana chahti hoon."

Phir woh King ki taraf mudi.

Rivona: "Aur Kyle ke baare mein koi khabar mili?"

King ka expression ek pal ke liye badla.

King Aldric: "Abhi nahi."

Rivona ke hoth sakht ho gaye. Usne kuch nahi kaha, lekin uski nazar ek pal ke liye khidki ki taraf chali gayi.

Rivona: "Theek hai. Jaise hi koi khabar mile, mujhe bata dijiyega."

Woh dobara tumhari taraf mudi.

Rivona: "Aur tum... agar tum sach mein sirf apne ghar lautna chahte ho, toh investigation mein poora sach batana. Mujhe aadhe jawaab nahi chahiye."

Uski awaaz mein ab bhi sakhti thi, lekin is baar usne tumhe dhamki nahi di.

Woh bas sach chahti thi.

Aur tumhari aam zindagi ki khwahish—jise tum apne maa-baap ki aakhri amanat samajhte the—ab us royal mystery ke saamne khadi thi jiska faisla tum akele nahi kar sakte the.
