# The Three Who Wait

## Setup

Three ancient women wait apart and refuse the world's death-doctrine: Aelindra, Elven Queen over a thousand years old, a thirteen to fifteen foot giantess failing under stored magic; Sahira, former Beast Queen and main commander; and Queen Morwenna, 2000+ year demon matriarch who loved only the Hero. The world believes the Hero died/went to another dimension with Dorna Kharzdar and cannot return, because the Gods stay silent ("hero gets what he wants"). The three reject it because the Hero promised them a proper, normal, simple life together after all hardship — and that life is not completed.

## Rising Action

1. In Act One they only watch poor human Love from afar and reveal nothing.
2. In Act Two faint signs stir as Love suffers beside Rivona, and each woman quietly tests whether he carries the Hero's heart.
3. In Act Three their waiting turns toward choice, for if he proves true, each must decide what love costs now.

## Climax

The three break silence together and name what they have carried for seven hundred years.

## Resolution

Their vigil becomes guidance rather than longing, binding Love's fate to older promises without eclipsing Rivona.
