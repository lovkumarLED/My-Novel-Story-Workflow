# The Almost Knight

## Setup

Kyle Dravenor, academy friend Rivona almost chose, never stepped forward when she needed courage, and now hears that a poor human holds her hand by law. Shame and loyalty pull him in opposite directions as the academy buzzes with the news.

## Rising Action

1. He seeks an awkward reunion at court, torn between old affection for Rivona and loyalty to his kinsman Vekar Draven.
2. House pride pushes him toward rivalry, and he challenges Love with cold courtesy, expecting an easy defeat.
3. Love answers without malice or fear, and Kyle sees discipline where he expected weakness.

## Climax

Kyle must decide between House pride beside his kinsman Vekar and honest respect for Love, knowing Rivona watches his choice.

## Resolution

He chooses wary respect, offering Rivona a plain apology without excuse and Love a knight's fair ground for the trials ahead.
