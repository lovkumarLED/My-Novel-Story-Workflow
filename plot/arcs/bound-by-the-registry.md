# Bound by the Registry

## Setup

Poor human Love comes to the palace gates on the morning of dragon crown princess Rivona Vaeltharis's engagement ceremony and faces her cold contempt before the watching court.

## Rising Action

1. The Ancient Marriage Registry awakens and binds Love to Rivona, stunning the court and displacing dragon prince Vekar Draven, diplomat John Ashford, and Charles VII, strongest of humans.
2. King Aldric upholds the law and orders a court investigation that finds no trickery.
3. Forced cohabitation begins under custom and guard, while Seraphine and Vaelora turn hostile and Elowen and Nymera stay fair.

## Climax

Rivona tests Love before the court, expecting cowardice, but his steady honesty under insult unsettles her scorn.

## Resolution

She grants a first unwilling respect, leaving their law-bound union tense, tender, and open to slow change.
